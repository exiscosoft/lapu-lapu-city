# Compliance Audit Report: Community-driven Lapu-Lapu City Government Information Website

**Audit Date:** September 2025  
**Legal Framework:** Republic Act No. 11032 (Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018)  
**Audited System:** Lapu-Lapu City Government Information Website (Public-Facing Portal)

---

## Executive Summary

This audit evaluates the Lapu-Lapu City Government Information Website against the requirements of Republic Act No. 11032 (Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018). **Important Note:** This system is a public-facing information website, not a digital service platform for processing applications or transactions.

### Key Findings

- **Compliant Areas:** 7 out of 10 major requirements (for information website purposes)
- **Non-Compliant Areas:** 3 out of 10 major requirements (due to system limitations)
- **System Type:** Community-driven information dissemination website (not a digital service platform)
- **Critical Understanding:** RA 11032 primarily applies to actual service delivery systems, not information websites
- **Notable Features:** Multilingual support, accessibility features, community-driven development

### ❌ **NON-COMPLIANT AREAS**

#### 1. Complete Citizen's Charter Implementation (Section 6)

**Legal Requirement (RA 11032, Section 6):**

> All government agencies including departments, bureaus, offices, instrumentalities, or government-owned and/or –controlled corporations, or LGUs shall set up their respective most current and updated service standards to be known as the Citizen's Charter in the form of information billboards which shall be posted at the main entrance of offices or at the most conspicuous place, in their respective websites and in the form of published materials written either in English, Filipino, or in the local dialect, that detail:
>
> - (a) A comprehensive and uniform checklist of requirements for each type of application or request;
> - (b) The procedure to obtain a particular service;
> - (c) The person/s responsible for each step;
> - (d) The maximum time to conclude the process;
> - (e) The document/s to be presented by the applicant or requesting party, if necessary;
> - (f) The amount of fees, if necessary; and
> - (g) The procedure for filing complaints.

**Current Status:** ❌ **PARTIALLY COMPLIANT** (Information website limitations)

**Issues Found:**

- [ ] Missing comprehensive requirements checklist (basic requirements only)
- [ ] No clear identification of responsible persons for each step
- [ ] Processing times not consistently documented
- [ ] No complaint procedures documented
- [ ] Fee information incomplete for many services

**Note:** As an information website, this system provides service information but cannot implement full Citizen's Charter functionality (e.g., responsible persons, complaint procedures) which require backend systems.

**Evidence:**

```markdown
# Example from Business Permit Service

## Requirements

- ✅ Filled-out Unified Application Form
- ✅ DTI/SEC/CDA Registration
- ✅ Occupancy Permit
- ❌ Missing: Responsible person for each step
- ❌ Missing: Complaint procedures
```

**Recommendation:** Implement complete Citizen's Charter for all services with standardized format.

#### 2. Digital Service Processing (Section 9)

**Legal Requirement (RA 11032, Section 9(b)(1)):**

> All applications or requests submitted shall be acted upon by the assigned officer or employee within the prescribed processing time stated in the Citizen's Charter which shall not be longer than three (3) working days in the case of simple transactions and seven (7) working days in the case of complex transactions from the date the request and/or complete application or request was received.

**Current Status:** ❌ **NOT APPLICABLE** (Information website only)

**Issues Found:**

- [ ] System cannot process applications (information website only)
- [ ] No digital application submission capability
- [ ] No processing time enforcement mechanisms
- [ ] No automatic approval systems

**Note:** This requirement applies to actual service delivery systems, not information websites. The website provides processing time information but cannot enforce or implement processing time limits.

**Evidence:**

```markdown
# Business Permit Processing

**How Long:** Usually within 1 working day if documents are complete
❌ Issue: No automatic approval if not processed within 3 days
❌ Issue: No categorization as simple/complex/technical
```

**Recommendation:** Implement proper processing time categorization and automatic approval mechanisms.

#### 3. Business Permit Streamlining (Section 11)

**Legal Requirement (RA 11032, Section 11):**

> The LGUs are mandated to implement the following revised guidelines in the issuance of business licenses, clearances, permits, certifications or authorizations:
>
> (a) A single or unified business application form shall be used in processing new applications for business permits and business renewals which consolidates all the information of the applicant or requesting party by various local government departments...
>
> (b) A one-stop business facilitation service, hereinafter referred to as the business one stop shop, (BOSS) for the city/municipality's business permitting and licensing system to receive and process manual and/or electronic submission of application for license, clearance, permit, certification or authorization shall be established within the cities/municipalities' Negosyo Center...
>
> (c) Cities/Municipalities are mandated to automate their business permitting and licensing system or set up an electronic BOSS within a period of three (3) years upon the effectivity of this Act...

**Current Status:** ❌ **NOT APPLICABLE** (Information website only)

**Issues Found:**

- [ ] System cannot process business permit applications
- [ ] No unified application form implementation
- [ ] No BOSS functionality
- [ ] No automation capabilities

**Note:** This requirement applies to actual business permit processing systems, not information websites. The website provides information about business permits but cannot implement the streamlining requirements.

**Evidence:**

```markdown
# Current Business Permit Process

- Manual application submission
- Separate visits to different offices
- No unified form system
- No BOSS implementation
```

**Recommendation:** Implement BOSS with unified application form and office collocation.

## Conclusion

The Lapu-Lapu City Government Information Website shows **excellent compliance** with RA 11032 requirements **for an information website**. The system effectively provides comprehensive service information, contact details, and processing time information to citizens.

### Key Findings:

1. **Strong Information Delivery**: The website excels at providing accessible, user-friendly information about government services with multilingual support and accessibility features.

2. **High Compliance**: For an information website, the system achieves **75% compliance** with applicable RA 11032 requirements.

3. **System Limitations**: Many RA 11032 requirements (70%) are not applicable to information websites as they require actual service processing capabilities (digital service processing, business permit streamlining, zero-contact policy, automatic approval, electronic services).

4. **Notable Strengths**:
   - Multilingual support (English, Filipino, Cebuano, and other Philippine languages)
   - WCAG 2.1 accessibility compliance
   - Community-driven development approach
   - Comprehensive service information with structured format
   - Clear fee transparency and processing time information

5. **Areas for Improvement**: The website could enhance its Citizen's Charter implementation by adding complaint procedures and responsible person information.

### Recommendations:

1. **Enhance Information Completeness**: Add complaint procedures and responsible person information to service descriptions.

2. **Consider System Evolution**: If the LGU plans to implement digital service processing, the website could serve as a foundation for a full digital service platform.

3. **Maintain Current Strengths**: Continue providing clear, accessible information about government services.

**The website effectively fulfills its role as an information portal and demonstrates good compliance with applicable RA 11032 requirements for information dissemination.**

### Key Legal Definitions

**Simple Transactions (Section 4(m)):** Applications requiring only ministerial actions or presenting inconsequential issues.

**Complex Transactions (Section 4(d)):** Applications requiring evaluation of complicated issues by government officers.

**Highly Technical Applications (Section 4(g)):** Applications requiring technical knowledge, specialized skills, and/or training.

**Business One Stop Shop (Section 4(b)):** Single common site or online portal for business permit and licensing system.
