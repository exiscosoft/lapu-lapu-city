# City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) issues fishing, hauling, research and tree-cutting permits, runs garbage collection, and acts on complaints about environmental problems in Lapu-Lapu City. It also takes in rescued wildlife, gives out seeds, and holds coastal cleanups and environmental seminars.

## Mandate and Functions

The office lists these frontline services in the Citizen's Charter.

**Securing permits**

- Fishing permits
- Boat registration
- Permits to operate
- Hauling permits for common waste rocks and soil
- Permits to conduct research
- Auxiliary invoices

**Document issuance**

- Endorsement letters for tree cutting and trimming
- Certifications for refund of the cash bond on tarpaulins
- Mangrove planting permits and certifications

**Garbage collection**

- City garbage collection
- Private hauler garbage collection (residual waste hauled to the sanitary landfill for the city government)

**Other services**

- Complaints on environmental problems
- Wildlife turn-over
- Backyard Gardening / Urban Agriculture Program (seed distribution)
- Coastal cleanup activities
- Seminars and information, education and communication (IEC) activities, also called Dalaw Turo

## Services for the Public

- [Get a Fishing Permit and Register Your Boat](/services/agriculture-fisheries/get-a-fishing-permit-and-register-your-boat): fishing permits, boat registration and permits to operate.
- [Get a Hauling Permit for Soil and Rocks](/services/environment/get-a-hauling-permit-for-soil-and-rocks): permits to haul common waste rocks and soil.
- [Get a Permit to Conduct Environmental Research](/services/environment/get-a-permit-to-conduct-environmental-research): for students, professors, researchers, and public or private groups doing research.
- [Get an Auxiliary Invoice to Ship Fish Products](/services/agriculture-fisheries/get-an-auxiliary-invoice-to-ship-fish-products): for anyone who ships fish and fishery products; also available on weekends.
- [Get a Tree Cutting or Trimming Endorsement](/services/environment/get-a-tree-cutting-or-trimming-endorsement): an endorsement letter before you cut or trim trees.
- [Get a Permit for Banners, Streamers or Signage](/services/business/get-a-permit-for-banners-streamers-or-signage): CENRO issues the certification for refund of your tarpaulin cash bond.
- [Organize a Coastal Cleanup or Mangrove Planting Activity](/services/environment/join-clean-up-drives-and-tree-planting-activities): mangrove planting permits and certifications, and coastal cleanups.
- [Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup](/services/garbage-waste-disposal/check-garbage-collection-schedules-and-request-pickup): free collection for households along national roads and city streets; segregate your garbage first.
- [Report Environmental Problems](/services/environment/report-illegal-logging-quarrying-or-fishing): send a complaint about an environmental problem.
- [Turn Over Rescued Wildlife](/services/environment/turn-over-rescued-wildlife): hand over wild animals you have rescued.
- [Ask for Free Seeds for Backyard Gardening](/services/agriculture-fisheries/ask-for-free-seeds-seedlings-fertilizers-or-fingerlings): seed distribution under the Backyard Gardening / Urban Agriculture Program.
- [Request an Environmental Seminar for Your Community](/services/environment/request-an-environmental-seminar-for-your-community): ask CENRO to hold a seminar or Dalaw Turo for your group.

## Office-only Services

This service is for the city government, not the public. The city pays private haulers to take residual waste from the Material Recovery Facility (MRF) to the sanitary landfill.
