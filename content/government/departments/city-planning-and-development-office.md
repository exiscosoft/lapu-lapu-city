# City Planning and Development Office (CPDO) — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Planning and Development Office (CPDO) checks that buildings and subdivisions in Lapu-Lapu City follow the city's zoning and land use rules. Lot owners, lessees, contractors, corporations and land developers come here for locational clearances, zoning certifications and subdivision permits.

## Mandate and Functions

The office lists these frontline services in the Citizen's Charter:

- Issuing locational clearances needed for a building permit
- Issuing zoning and land use certifications
- Issuing locational clearances and development permits for complex subdivision applications
- Processing subdivision approval applications

Fees follow the Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board (HLURB) Revised Schedule of Fees (Resolution No. 912, Series of 2013).

---

## Services for the Public

- [Request Zoning, Land Use, or Building Permits](/services/housing-land-use/request-zoning-land-use-or-building-permits): get a locational clearance for your building permit, or a zoning or land use certification.
- [Apply for a Subdivision Development Permit](/services/housing-land-use/apply-for-a-subdivision-development-permit): locational clearance and development permit for complex subdivisions, subdivision approval, and the HLURB 2013 schedule of fees.
