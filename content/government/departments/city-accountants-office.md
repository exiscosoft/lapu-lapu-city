# City Accountant's Office — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Accountant's Office checks and processes the city government's payrolls and payments to suppliers, contractors and other creditors. It also issues certifications such as net pay, premium and loan payment certifications, city clearances and Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) tax credit forms.

## Mandate and Functions

The office lists two frontline services in the Citizen's Charter.

**Processing of:**

- Payrolls
- Disbursement vouchers for payment to creditors, suppliers and utilities
- Salaries and wages, honoraria and allowances of city employees and officials
- Cash advances, reimbursements and liquidations for travel, petty cash and trust liabilities

**Issuance of certifications:**

- Net pay
- Government Service Insurance System (GSIS), Pag-IBIG and PhilHealth premium payments
- Bank loan repayments
- Availability of funds
- City clearance
- BIR tax credit forms (Forms 2306 and 2307) and business tax

These services are for city officials and employees, national agencies, creditors, suppliers and contractors, government financial institutions and government-owned or -controlled corporations, private financial institutions, and anyone with business dealings with the city government.
