# Lapu-Lapu City Library — Lapu-Lapu City

The Lapu-Lapu City Library gives all residents free access to information, education, culture and leisure, to promote literacy and lifelong learning. Residents can register as members and borrow books and magazines.

## Mandate and Functions

The library provides free access to information, education, culture and leisure to all community members, promoting literacy and lifelong learning.

Its frontline services are:

- **Member registration**: free for all residents of Lapu-Lapu City. Bring a government-issued ID that shows your name and address. Takes about 10 minutes.
- **Borrower's card**: free for registered members. Books or magazines returned late cost ₱5.00 per day overdue. Takes about 11 minutes.
