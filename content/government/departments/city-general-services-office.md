# City General Services Office (CGSO) — Lapu-Lapu City

The City General Services Office (CGSO) handles the city government's supplies, property, records archive, utilities and building maintenance. For the public, it issues bidding documents and releases purchase orders to suppliers, and releases archived records on request.

## Mandate and Functions

The office lists these frontline services in the Citizen's Charter:

- Issuing gas slips to authorized government motor vehicles
- Processing payment of utility bills for city offices
- Issuing bidding documents to interested bidders and suppliers
- Releasing purchase orders to winning bidders and suppliers
- Releasing records and documents from the city archives
- Issuing Property Acknowledgement Reports (PAR) and Property Return Slips (PRS) to city employees
- Issuing CGSO clearances to city employees
- Releasing on-stock supplies to city offices
- Minor carpentry repair and repainting for city offices
- Providing audio systems for city offices
