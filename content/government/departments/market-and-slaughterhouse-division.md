# Market and Slaughterhouse Division — Lapu-Lapu City

The Market and Slaughterhouse Division runs Lapu-Lapu City's public market and slaughterhouse. It collects stall rent and other market fees, issues market clearances to stall lessees, and handles the slaughter of animals brought in by meat dealers.

## Mandate and Functions

### Mandate

To manage, regulate and oversee the operation of the Public Market and Slaughterhouse facilities. It is responsible for upholding safe, orderly and clean operations while enforcing existing laws, and making sure that revenues are properly collected.

### Vision

Hygienic, secure and well-managed market and slaughterhouse facilities that ensure efficient service delivery, food safety and sustainable economic growth while offering dependable and easily accessible facilities for vendors, farmers, traders and customers.

### Mission

To effectively oversee and regulate market and slaughterhouse operations by guaranteeing hygienic, safe and orderly facilities, strict sanitation and meat inspection regulations, appropriate revenue collection and high-quality public service, all the while promoting local economic development, food safety, and the well-being of vendors, farmers, traders and consumers.

### Frontline services

- Issuing official receipts for stall rent and electricity payments
- Issuing market clearances to stall lessees (₱150.00)
- Issuing cash tickets to vendors who use extra space or bring goods into the market
- Slaughtering animals and collecting slaughterhouse fees

---

## Services for the Public

- [Rent Stalls in Public Markets](/services/business/rent-stalls-in-public-markets): Pay stall rent and electricity, get a market clearance (₱150.00), and pay for cash tickets as a vendor.
- [Have Livestock Slaughtered and Meat Inspected](/services/agriculture-fisheries/have-livestock-slaughtered-and-meat-inspected): Slaughter of cattle, hogs, goats and sheep at the city slaughterhouse, with per-head fees.
