# Public Employment Service Office — Lapu-Lapu City

The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) helps job seekers find local and overseas work, and helps employers find workers. It also runs the city's summer jobs for youth, the Government Internship Program and career guidance for students.

## Mandate and Functions

PESO's services under the Citizen's Charter are free. The office:

- Screens job seekers by skills and ability and refers them to suitable jobs with private employers, here and abroad
- Accredits companies, checks that they operate legitimately, and posts their job vacancies
- Organizes job fairs and signs up employers who want to take part
- Runs the Summer Job for the Youth Program, which gives poor but deserving students, out-of-school youth and children of displaced workers short-term jobs to help them stay in school
- Runs the Government Internship Program (GIP) for young people aged 18 to 30 who want to work on government projects and programs
- Gives career guidance through the Career Development and Support Program (CDSP) for Grade 10, Grade 12 and graduating college students, on request of their school

## Services for the Public

- [Find a Job Through PESO](/services/business/find-a-job-through-peso): Free job referral and placement for job seekers, graduates, displaced workers and returning overseas Filipino workers (OFWs), plus career guidance for students.
- [Hire Workers or Join a Job Fair as an Employer](/services/business/hire-workers-or-join-a-job-fair-as-an-employer): Free PESO accreditation for companies, job vacancy posting and job fair participation.
- [Apply for the Summer Job Program](/services/business/apply-for-the-summer-job-program): Short-term jobs for students and out-of-school youth aged 15 to 25 from low-income families.
- [Apply for the Government Internship Program](/services/business/apply-for-the-government-internship-program): Internships in government offices for people aged 18 to 30 who have finished at least high school or a technical-vocational course.

### Feedback and Complaints

To give feedback, fill out a feedback survey form and drop it in the PESO drop box. To complain, write a letter to the PESO Manager or ask for a dialogue with the office.

| Feedback and Complaint Mechanism |                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| How to send feedback             | All clients are encouraged to accomplish feedback survey and drop the forms to the PESO drop box.                  |
| How feedback is processed        | All feedback forms are compiled and discussed among the PESO personnel for assessment and appropriate action plan. |
| How to file a complaint          | Write a complaint letter addressed to the PESO Manager and/or request for a schedule of dialogue with forms.       |
| How complaints are processed     | All verbal or written complaints received, will be responded accordingly by PESO Manager.                          |
