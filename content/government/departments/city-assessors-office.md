# Office of the City Assessor — Lapu-Lapu City

The Office of the City Assessor records and values land, buildings, machinery and other real property in Lapu-Lapu City for tax purposes. Property owners and their authorized representatives come here for tax declarations, property certifications and maps.

## Mandate and Functions

The office works through its Real Property Appraisal and Assessment Division, Tax Mapping Division, Assessment Records Management Division and Administrative Division. Its services cover:

- Issuing tax declarations for property transfers, new buildings and other improvements, machinery and equipment, land declared for the first time, and lot partitions or subdivisions
- Re-assessing property classification or taxability, and cancelling tax declarations for property that was destroyed or demolished
- Annotating sales, donations and transfers of unsegregated portions, and encumbrances such as adverse claims, mortgages, tax liens and court orders
- Issuing certified copies of tax declarations, certificates of land holdings, certificates of no improvement, property verification or trace-back histories, and vicinity or lot location maps

### How the office processes requests

- Requests are accepted over the counter only. There is no online submission yet.
- Processing starts only when your documents are complete. Incomplete documents sent by courier or post are not processed, and are discarded 15 working days after you are notified if you do not complete them.
- Bring valid proof of identity. If you act for the owner, bring the owner's written authorization. This follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012.
- Follow up on a pending request only through the Releasing Section of the Records Division. Do not contact the staff processing your request.
- Processing times are for a single transaction. They can be longer when the queue is long, when a discrepancy is found, or when an undeclared building or improvement is found on the property. Undeclared improvements must then be declared for taxation under Sections 202 to 205 of the Local Government Code (Republic Act No. 7160).

## Services for the Public

- [Get a Tax Declaration for Your Property](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-tax-declaration-for-your-property): new tax declarations for transfers, new buildings and machinery, land declared for the first time, and lot partitions or subdivisions.
- [Update or Cancel a Tax Declaration](/services/housing-land-use/update-or-cancel-a-tax-declaration): re-assessment, cancellation after damage or demolition, and annotations of sales, mortgages and other encumbrances.
- [Request Property Records from the Assessor](/services/housing-land-use/request-property-records-from-the-assessor): certified copies of tax declarations, certificates of land holdings or no improvement, trace-back histories, and vicinity or lot maps.
