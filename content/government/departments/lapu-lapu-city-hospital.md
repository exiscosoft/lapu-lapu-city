# Lapu-Lapu City Hospital — Lapu-Lapu City

Lapu-Lapu City Hospital is the city government's hospital in Gun-ob. It gives emergency care, outpatient consultations, laboratory tests and imaging to the public, and helps patients who cannot afford their bills through its Malasakit Center.

## Mandate and Functions

The hospital's Citizen's Charter covers seven departments:

| Department                                               | What it does for you                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Hours                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Emergency Department**                                 | Treats patients who need emergency care. Open to the general public.                                                                                                                                                             | 24 hours                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Outpatient Department**                                | Sees Lapu-Lapu City residents and other patients with non-emergency cases. Bring a referral from your health center, or your index card if you are a returning patient.                                                          | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM. On weekends and holidays, go to the Emergency Department.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Laboratory Department**                                | Runs blood, urine, stool and other tests for admitted and non-admitted patients with a doctor's request.                                                                                                                         | 24 hours                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Radiology Department**                                 | X-ray, general and OB-Gyne ultrasound, two-dimensional echocardiography (2D echo) and electrocardiogram (ECG), with a doctor's request.                                                                                          | X-ray: 24 hours. General ultrasound: Monday to Friday, 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM, and Wednesday, 4:30 PM to 6:30 PM. OB-Gyne ultrasound: Tuesday, 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM. ECG: Monday to Thursday, 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, and Friday, 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. |
| **Malasakit Center / Medical Social Service Department** | Checks PhilHealth eligibility, enrolls eligible patients for free PhilHealth benefits, and helps indigent and financially incapacitated patients pay for tests, medicines and hospital bills or refers them to partner agencies. | Monday to Sunday, 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Billing Section**                                      | Prepares the statement of account for admitted patients before discharge.                                                                                                                                                        | Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM; Sunday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Medical Records**                                      | Issues medical and medico-legal certificates, discharge summaries, and birth and death certificates for patients of the hospital.                                                                                                | Not stated in the charter; ask the hospital                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

## Services for the Public

- [Pay Your Hospital Bill Before Discharge](/services/health-services/pay-your-hospital-bill-before-discharge): How admitted patients get their statement of account and settle the bill before going home.
- [Get Emergency Care at a City Hospital](/services/health-services/get-emergency-care-at-a-city-hospital): Emergency treatment, open 24 hours, for anyone who needs urgent medical care.
- [Get Laboratory Tests at Lapu-Lapu City Hospital](/services/health-services/get-laboratory-tests-at-lapu-lapu-city-hospital): Lab tests with a doctor's request, open 24 hours, with turnaround times by test type.
- [Get Help Paying Hospital Bills at the Malasakit Center](/services/health-services/get-help-paying-hospital-bills-at-the-malasakit-center): Financial help with tests, medicines and bills for indigent and financially incapacitated patients.
- [Request Hospital Medical Records and Certificates](/services/health-services/request-hospital-medical-records-and-certificates): Medical certificates, discharge summaries, and birth and death certificates for patients of the hospital.
- [Consult a Doctor at a Hospital Outpatient Department](/services/health-services/consult-a-doctor-at-a-hospital-outpatient-department): Non-emergency consultations, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
- [Get an X-ray, Ultrasound, 2D Echo or ECG](/services/health-services/get-an-x-ray-ultrasound-2d-echo-or-ecg): Imaging and heart tests with a doctor's request; 2D echo costs ₱2,000.00 and ECG costs ₱100.00.
