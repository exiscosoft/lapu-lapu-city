# Manpower and Human Development Training Center — Lapu-Lapu City

The Manpower and Human Development Training Center (MHDTC) gives free sewing and dressmaking training to Lapu-Lapu City residents, to prepare them for jobs in the garment industry or for their own small business.

## Mandate and Functions

The center runs two free training programs:

- **Industrial High-Speed Sewing Machine Operations**: training for employment in the garment and manufacturing industry. Trainees learn machine parts and maintenance, threading, stitching methods, quality control and workplace safety, with a focus on speed and accuracy. The training runs 14 to 20 days, and graduates are endorsed to companies in the Mactan Economic Zone (MEZ) for final assessment. Open to registered voters and residents of Lapu-Lapu City aged 18 to 40.
- **Community-Based Training in Dressmaking Leading to Dressmaking NC II**: livelihood training in high-speed sewing, garment production and pattern drafting, for small business or employment. The training runs 22 days. Open to registered voters of Lapu-Lapu City of legal age.

After you submit your documents, the center contacts you by text or call with your training schedule.
