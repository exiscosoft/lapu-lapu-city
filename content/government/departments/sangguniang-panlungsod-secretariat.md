# Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod — Lapu-Lapu City

The Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod is the secretariat of the City Council. It issues certified true copies of approved ordinances and resolutions, answers letters of inquiry, and certifies notices posted on the council's bulletin board.

## Mandate and Functions

The office supports the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Council) by keeping and releasing its records and handling requests from the public. To learn about the councilors and the Vice Mayor who presides over the council, see the [Legislative Department](/government/departments/legislative) page.

The office handles four services:

| Service                                                               | Fee                                                        | Processing time                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Certified true copies of approved resolutions and ordinances          | ₱20.00 per page plus documentary stamp tax (if applicable) | About 1 hour and 35 minutes; up to 2 days and 35 minutes if the record must be pulled from paper files |
| Reply to a simple letter of inquiry                                   | Free                                                       | Within 3 working days and about 31 minutes                                                             |
| Reply to a complex letter of inquiry                                  | Free                                                       | Up to 7 working days and about 31 minutes                                                              |
| Certification of posting on the Sangguniang Panlungsod bulletin board | ₱20.00 per page plus documentary stamp tax (if applicable) | 2 hours and 10 minutes, not counting the required posting period                                       |

You can send a letter of inquiry by walk-in, email or written letter. These services cover non-legislative requests only: questions about the status of documents, verification of records and general information. Requests that need action by the council, such as getting an item on the session agenda, a committee hearing or a new ordinance or resolution, are not covered.

The certification of posting covers only notices posted on the Sangguniang Panlungsod bulletin board, not other bulletin boards in City Hall. Utility companies, government agencies and other groups that must prove a notice was posted use this service.

### C. Issuance of Certification of Posting at the Sangguniang Panlungsod Bulletin Board

**Description of the service**

– This service covers the posting of notices at the designated bulletin board of the Sangguniang Panlungsod and the issuance of a certification attesting that the notice was posted for the required period.

|                         |                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Office of Division**  | Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod                                                                                        |
| **Classification**      | Simple Transactions                                                                                                                          |
| **Type of Transaction** | Transactions involving utility service providers, private entities, and government agencies.                                                 |
| **Who may avail**       | Utility service providers ( e.g., water and electric companies), government agencies, and other entities requiring certification of posting. |

#### Checklist Requirements

| Checklist Requirements     | Where to Secure |
| -------------------------- | --------------- |
| Letter request for posting | Client          |
| Notice to be Posted        | Client          |

#### Steps

| Client Steps                 | Agency Action                                            | Fess to be Paid                                                                                     | Processing Time                        | Person Responsible     |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 1.Submit request with Notice | Receive and verify completeness                          | None                                                                                                | 15 minutes                             | Receiving Clerk        |
| 2.                           | Record request and schedule of posting                   | None                                                                                                | 15 minutes                             | Records / Admin. Staff |
| 3.                           | Post Notice on the Sangguniang Panlungsod bulletin board | None                                                                                                | 20 minutes                             | Assigned Personnel     |
| 4. Wait for posting period   | Monitor posting within the designated bulletin board     | None                                                                                                | Depends on the required posting period | Assigned Personnel     |
| 5. Request Certification     | Prepare Certification for Posting                        | P 20.00 per page + Documentary Stamp Tax ( as applicable) to be paid at the City Treasurer's Office | 1 hour                                 | Records Personnel      |
| 6. Claim Certification       | Release Certification of Posting                         | None                                                                                                | 20 minutes                             | Records Personnel      |
|                              | **TOTAL**                                                | P 20.00 per page + Documentary Stamp Tax ( as applicable) to be paid at the City Treasurer's Office | 2 hours and 10 minutes                 |                        |

**Important Notes:**

- Processing time excludes the required posting period.
- Payment shall be made at the City Treasurer's Office.
- Only complete and legible notices shall be accepted.
- The certification issued by the Office **<u>only</u>** covers posting made at the Sangguniang Panlungsod bulletin board.
- The certification does not include postings in other bulletin boards or areas within City Hall.
- The office does not validate or endorse the contents of the notices or the application of the requesting party.
