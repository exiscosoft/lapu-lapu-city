# City Traffic Management System (CTMS) — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Traffic Management System (CTMS) manages traffic and enforces traffic ordinances, laws and rules on all streets and roads in Lapu-Lapu City. You deal with this office to pay traffic violation fines, settle traffic disputes, and claim towed or impounded vehicles.

## Mandate and Functions

### Legal Mandate

City Ordinance No. 659 created the Lapu-Lapu City Traffic Commission (LACITRACOM). City Ordinance No. 243-2002 later repealed LACITRACOM and created a special body under the City Mayor's Office called the City Traffic Management System (CTMS), to manage traffic operations on all streets and roads in Lapu-Lapu City effectively and efficiently.

### Vision

A safe and traffic-free Lapu-Lapu City

### Mission

To provide prompt, efficient and quality services in traffic management and enforcement

### Functions and Services

**Main:**

1. Traffic management
2. Enforcement of city traffic ordinances, traffic laws, rules and regulations

**Other services:**

1. Traffic patrols
2. Road clearing operations involving motor vehicles (clamping and towing of illegally parked vehicles)
3. Storage and impounding of vehicles, and their release
4. Traffic dispute resolution
5. Installation, repair and replacement of traffic signs
6. Installation, marking, maintenance and painting of traffic road markings
7. Collection of payments for traffic violations
8. Filing of complaints against traffic violators who fail or refuse to pay within the prescribed period
9. Incident reports
10. Inspection of tricycles for franchise renewal, and issuance of the related clearance
11. Issuance of clearances required for business renewal of applicable businesses
12. Issuance of recommendations for businesses applying for truck-ban exemption
13. Assistance to the City Clearing Office/Team in clearing operations
14. Assistance to the Lapu-Lapu City Police Office (LLCPO) Traffic Enforcement Unit (TEU) during motor vehicle accidents

### Service Units

**1. Traffic Enforcers**

- Main unit for enforcing traffic ordinances, laws and rules in the field
- First responders during motor vehicle accidents
- Main unit for traffic management

**2. Engineering Unit**

- Installation, repair and replacement of traffic signs
- Installation, marking, maintenance and painting of traffic road markings

**3. Towing/Clamping Unit**

- Tows and clamps illegally parked vehicles
- Assists the City Clearing Office during clearing operations
- Keeps records of towed and clamped vehicles

**4. Impounding Unit**

- Stores towed and impounded vehicles in the impounding area
- Handles the storage and release of towed or impounded vehicles
- Keeps records of stored and released vehicles
- Guards the impounding area

**5. IT and Records Unit**

- Manages the traffic program/software and records
- Encodes issued traffic citation tickets
- Updates the traffic program/software when citation tickets are paid
- Endorses unsettled citation tickets to the Legal Unit for filing of complaints
- Issues clearances of no pending or unsettled violations for business permit renewal, delivery permits, tricycle franchise renewal and other legitimate purposes
- Issues certifications for requests about office records

**6. Legal Unit**

- Gives legal support to the office
- Informs the office of updates and changes in traffic enforcement laws, rules and regulations
- Receives unsettled tickets from the IT and Records Unit for filing of complaints
- Interviews traffic enforcers about their complaints and attests to them
- Gives legal opinions
- Answers complaints
- Notarizes affidavits, undertakings and incident reports related to the office's functions and services, for traffic enforcers and walk-in clients
- Traffic dispute resolution

**7. Desk Officers**

- Traffic dispute resolution
- Keeps traffic blotter records
- Inspects tricycle units for tricycle franchise renewal
- Receives communications, notices and complaints
- Helps check requirements for the release of towed, clamped and impounded vehicles
- Monitors city traffic through radio communication
- Relays traffic updates by radio to traffic enforcers in the field

**8. Cashier**

- Receives payments for traffic violations, towing and clamping fees, storage fees and clearances, and issues official receipts
- Collects the green ticket (duplicate of the traffic citation ticket) when you pay, and endorses it with the official receipt number to the IT and Records Unit so your payment is recorded
