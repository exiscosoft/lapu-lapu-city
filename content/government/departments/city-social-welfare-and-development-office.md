# City Social Welfare and Development Office — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) protects the social welfare rights of Lapu-Lapu City residents. It helps families in crisis, young children, women and children who suffer abuse, senior citizens, persons with disabilities (PWDs), solo parents and disaster victims.

## Mandate and Functions

### Mandate

An office responsible for the protection of social welfare rights and the promotion of social development.

### Vision

An office which provides immediate assistance to all Oponganons with love, care and compassion.

### Mission

- To efficiently deliver timely and inclusive basic services that are responsive to the needs of the Oponganons and empower the marginalized and disadvantaged sector through sustainable programs.
- To implement programs that will capacitate, develop and protect the marginalized and disadvantaged sector as well as maximize their potentials.
- To provide basic services to the indigents, victims of calamities and other disadvantaged sectors of the city.

### Goals

- To provide timely and inclusive services to all sectors with respect, fairness, confidentiality and gender responsiveness.
- To protect children, women, PWDs and the elderly against all forms of abuse, violence and discrimination.
- To develop our youth through practical skills development, values formation and the Alternative Learning System.
- To promote social family responsibilities.
- To harness volunteers for community involvement and development.

### Service Pledge

The CSWDO staff pledge to give quality, prompt and courteous service from Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, without a noon break. They commit to know and follow service standards in their duties and to keep improving how they serve, in the best interest of every Oponganon client.

All CSWDO services listed in the charter are free.

## Services for the Public

- [Get financial assistance in a crisis (AICS)](/services/social-welfare/get-financial-assistance-in-a-crisis-aics): Financial assistance in crisis situations (AICS) for medical bills, burial and other emergencies, for residents found eligible by a social worker.
- [Enroll Your Child in City Daycare (Early Childhood Care and Development)](/services/education/enroll-children-in-lgu-daycare-or-preschool-programs): Free Early Childhood Care and Development (ECCD) classes for children aged 3 to 4 years and 11 months.
- [Receive relief goods or cash aid during disasters](/services/social-welfare/receive-relief-goods-or-cash-aid-during-disasters): Emergency relief for individuals and families hit by disasters, available 24 hours a day.
- [Get therapy and special education for children with disabilities](/services/social-welfare/get-therapy-and-special-education-for-children-with-disabilities): Free physical therapy, occupational therapy, special education and social rehabilitation for children with disabilities aged 0 to 14.
- [Get Married by the City Mayor or at the Kasalan ng Bayan](/services/social-welfare/get-married-at-city-hall-or-kasalan-ng-bayan): Free yearly mass wedding for indigent resident couples who have lived together for at least five years.
- [Apply for a Marriage License](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-a-marriage-license): Includes the pre-marriage orientation and counselling that couples attend before getting a license, held every Wednesday and Friday morning.
- [Visit the Free Monthly Mental Health Clinic](/services/health-services/visit-the-free-monthly-mental-health-clinic): Free monthly clinic for people with mental health concerns, including walk-ins and those referred by health workers.
- [Get livelihood capital assistance](/services/social-welfare/get-livelihood-capital-assistance): Added capital for indigent individuals or groups whose existing livelihood has limited capital.
- [Join livelihood or skills training programs](/services/social-welfare/join-livelihood-or-skills-training-programs): Free practical skills training with TESDA and other partners for high school graduates aged 18 to 60.
- [Get family and community welfare support](/services/social-welfare/get-family-and-community-welfare-support): Help for distressed or abused returning overseas Filipino workers (OFWs), women and children who are victims of violence, and persons deprived of liberty (PDL) on probation.
- [Claim senior citizen cash gifts and incentives](/services/social-welfare/claim-senior-citizen-cash-gifts-and-incentives): Midyear and year-end cash aid for registered senior citizens and PWDs, birthday cash gifts and the centenarian cash award.
- [Apply for a senior citizen, solo parent, or PWD ID](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-senior-citizen-solo-parent-or-pwd-assistance): Free solo parent, PWD and senior citizen IDs and purchase booklets, released in 1 to 2 weeks.
- [Request a social case study or welfare certificate](/services/social-welfare/request-a-social-case-study-or-welfare-certificate): Free social case study reports, certificates of indigency, referral letters and similar documents, usually ready in 2 to 6 days.
