# City Veterinary Office — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Veterinary Office (CVO) registers and vaccinates dogs and cats, takes in and rehomes unwanted pets, and impounds stray animals. Its Public Health Regulatory Division also inspects animals and meat at the city slaughterhouse.

## Mandate and Functions

The office has two groups of frontline services.

**Pet and animal control services**, for dog and cat owners in the city:

- Register dogs and cats (free, one-time registration)
- Give anti-rabies shots to dogs and cats, at the office or through home visits
- Accept pets that owners voluntarily surrender
- Put impounded pets up for adoption
- Impound unwanted or stray dogs and cats on request

**Slaughterhouse regulation**, for meat suppliers and dealers:

- Inspect animals before and after slaughter (ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection) so that only healthy animals reach the market
- Inspect meat from slaughterhouses other than the city's that is sold in the city's public markets and supermarkets (post-abattoir inspection)

## Services for the Public

- [Register Your Dog or Cat](/services/agriculture-fisheries/register-your-dog-or-cat): Free one-time registration for dogs and cats; replacing a lost green card costs ₱100.00.
- [Get Your Dog or Cat Vaccinated Against Rabies](/services/agriculture-fisheries/get-your-dog-or-cat-vaccinated-against-rabies): Free anti-rabies shots for registered dogs and cats aged 3 months and older, at the office or by home service on call.
- [Surrender or Adopt a Pet](/services/agriculture-fisheries/surrender-or-adopt-a-pet): Surrender a pet you can no longer keep (₱1,000.00), or adopt an unclaimed impounded pet (₱250.00 per pet).
- [Report Stray Dogs and Cats for Impounding](/services/agriculture-fisheries/report-stray-dogs-and-cats-for-impounding): Request an impounding operation for stray or unwanted dogs and cats in your area.
- [Have Livestock Slaughtered and Meat Inspected](/services/agriculture-fisheries/have-livestock-slaughtered-and-meat-inspected): Required inspection of animals for slaughter and of meat from other slaughterhouses, with per-head fees.
