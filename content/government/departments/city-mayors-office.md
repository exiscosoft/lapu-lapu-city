# Office of the City Mayor — Lapu-Lapu City

The Office of the City Mayor issues special permits for events, venue bookings, banners and deliveries, and certifications that residents need for work, travel and marriage. It also handles applications for the board and bar passer incentive and for senior citizen birthday cash gifts that were not claimed on time.

## Mandate and Functions

The Office of the City Mayor handles these frontline services for the public:

- Special permits for dance benefits, processions, motorcades, fluvial parades, fun runs, marathons and similar activities
- Bookings of city venues: the Hoops Dome, Court 1, and the Olango Hoops Dome, Gymnasium and Mini-City Hall
- Special permits for temporary banners, streamers and promotional materials, and for permanent directional signs
- Delivery permits for businesses with trucking or delivery services
- Certificates of unemployment, certifications for the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA) apostille, and certifications of good moral character
- Certifications for religious leaders applying for a Certificate of Registration of Authority to Solemnize Marriage (CRASM), and schedules for marriage solemnization by the City Mayor
- Applications for the board and bar passer incentive
- Re-payroll of senior citizen birthday cash gifts that were not claimed during the scheduled distribution

## Services for the Public

- [Get a Special Permit for a Procession, Fun Run or Dance Benefit](/services/infrastructure-public-works/get-a-special-permit-for-a-procession-fun-run-or-dance-benefit): permits for dance benefits, processions, motorcades, fluvial parades, fun runs and marathons.
- [Rent a City Venue or Gym](/services/infrastructure-public-works/use-public-facilities-like-gyms-barangay-halls-or-multipurpose-centers): book the Hoops Dome, Court 1, or the Olango Hoops Dome, Gymnasium and Mini-City Hall.
- [Get a Permit for Banners, Streamers or Signage](/services/business/get-a-permit-for-banners-streamers-or-signage): temporary banners and promotional materials, and permanent directional signs.
- [Get a Delivery Permit](/services/business/get-a-delivery-permit): for business establishments with trucking or delivery services.
- [Get Certifications for Employment or Work Abroad](/services/business/get-certifications-for-employment-or-work-abroad): certificate of unemployment, certification for DFA apostille, and certification of good moral character.
- [Get Married by the City Mayor or at the Kasalan ng Bayan](/services/social-welfare/get-married-at-city-hall-or-kasalan-ng-bayan): book a marriage solemnization schedule; religious leaders can also get the CRASM certification here.
- [Claim the Board or Bar Exam Passer Incentive](/services/education/claim-the-board-or-bar-exam-passer-incentive): for Lapu-Lapu City residents who passed a Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) board exam or the bar exam.
- [Claim senior citizen cash gifts and incentives](/services/social-welfare/claim-senior-citizen-cash-gifts-and-incentives): ask for re-payroll of a birthday cash gift you could not claim during the scheduled distribution.
