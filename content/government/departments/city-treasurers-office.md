# Office of the City Treasurer — Lapu-Lapu City

The Office of the City Treasurer collects real property tax, business tax and other local taxes, fees and charges. It also issues real property tax clearances and certifications, and processes the closure of registered businesses.

---

## About the Office

| Information | Details                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hours**   | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, except holidays. If a quarterly real property tax due date falls on a weekend or holiday, the office opens that day to accept payments only. |

---

## Mandate and Functions

The office lists these frontline services in the Citizen's Charter:

- Collecting payments for real property tax
- Collecting payments for business tax, other local taxes, fees and charges
- Issuing real property tax clearances
- Issuing certifications and acting on other requests
- Processing the closure or retirement of a registered business
- Processing business permit applications through the Business One-Stop-Shop (BOSS), over the counter or online

Real property tax is due quarterly: March 31, June 30, September 30 and December 31.

- [Get a Real Property Tax Clearance or Certification](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance-or-certification): tax clearances, certifications and other requests.
