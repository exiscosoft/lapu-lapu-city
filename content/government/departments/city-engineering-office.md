# City Engineering Office and Office of the Building Official — Lapu-Lapu City

The Office of the Building Official (OBO) at the City Engineering Office processes building permits and certificates of occupancy in Lapu-Lapu City. It serves developers, contractors, individual project owners and government agencies.

---

## Mandate and Functions

The office enforces the National Building Code of the Philippines (Presidential Decree 1096). Under Section 301 of the Code, no person, firm, corporation or government agency may build, alter, repair, move, convert or demolish a building or structure without first getting a building permit from the Building Official. A building may not be used or occupied, or have its use changed, until the Building Official issues a Certificate of Occupancy.

The office lists two frontline services in the Citizen's Charter:

- Processing and approval of applications for construction, renovation, repair or demolition of structures (building permit)
- Issuing certificates of occupancy for residential and commercial buildings (occupancy permit), after a site inspection
