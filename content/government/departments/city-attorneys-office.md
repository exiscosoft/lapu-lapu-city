# Office of the City Attorney — Lapu-Lapu City

The Office of the City Attorney is the city government's legal office. For the public, it issues, renews and amends the Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP) that tricycle operators need.

## Mandate and Functions

The office lists these frontline services in the Citizen's Charter:

- Issuance of the Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP) for Olango Island
- Renewal of the MTOP
- Amendment of the MTOP for a change of ownership
- Amendment of the MTOP for a change of motorcycle unit

A new MTOP (Olango Island) can be issued to Filipino citizens, and to partnerships or corporations with at least 60% Filipino ownership. Renewals and amendments are for current MTOP holders.
