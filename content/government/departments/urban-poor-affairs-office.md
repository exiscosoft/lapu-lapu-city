# Urban Poor Affairs Office — Lapu-Lapu City

The Urban Poor Affairs Office (UPAO) works with urban poor associations in Lapu-Lapu City. It accredits these associations and issues certificates that their members need to get an electrical permit.

## Mandate and Functions

UPAO has two frontline services, both free of charge:

- **Accreditation of urban poor associations**: the office checks an association's officers, members, meeting records and history, and the office head signs its accreditation.
- **Certificate for electrical permit**: for urban poor residents who are members of an accredited association.

Area coordinators handle requests for each group of barangays, including a coordinator for the Olango area.

---

## Services for the Public

- [Accredit an Urban Poor Association](/services/housing-land-use/accredit-an-urban-poor-association): Free accreditation for urban poor associations; submit your list of officers and members, meeting minutes and history to your area coordinator.
- [Get an Electrical Permit Certificate for Urban Poor Families](/services/housing-land-use/get-an-electrical-permit-certificate-for-urban-poor-families): Free certificate for members of an accredited urban poor association who are applying for an electrical permit.
