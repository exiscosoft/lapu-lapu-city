# Lapu-Lapu City College — Lapu-Lapu City

This page covers the Office of the Registrar of Lapu-Lapu City College (LLCC). The office enrolls students and issues school records such as transcripts, certifications and replacement diplomas.

---

## About the Office

| Information        | Details                                                    |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Office**         | Office of the Registrar                                    |
| **Student portal** | portal.llcc.edu.ph (online enrollment and record requests) |
| **Payments**       | Cashier's Office in City Hall                              |

---

## Mandate and Functions

The Office of the Registrar's frontline services, as listed in the Citizen's Charter:

| Service                                                                                                                            | Fee                                                                                                                          | Processing time     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Official Transcript of Records (TOR)                                                                                               | ₱350.00                                                                                                                      | 5 to 7 working days |
| Certification (enrollment, general weighted average, good moral character, units earned, evaluation of subjects taken, and others) | ₱50.00                                                                                                                       | 1 working day       |
| Endorsement to the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) for Certification, Authentication and Verification (CAV)                  | ₱50.00 for the endorsement letter, ₱25.00 per page of attachments, ₱50.00 for a certification of units (undergraduates only) | 30 minutes          |
| Reissued study load or grade slip                                                                                                  | ₱20.00 per copy                                                                                                              | 10 minutes          |
| Authentication or certified true copy of documents                                                                                 | ₱25.00 per page                                                                                                              | 10 minutes          |
| Reissued diploma                                                                                                                   | ₱150.00                                                                                                                      | 2 to 3 weeks        |

The office also runs on-site enrollment for new, transferee and returnee students after pre-enrollment. You can also request a transcript or certification online through the student portal.

## Services for the Public

- [Request School Records from Lapu-Lapu City College](/services/education/request-school-records-from-lapu-lapu-city-college): Transcripts, certifications, authenticated copies, CAV endorsements and replacement diplomas, in person or online.
- [Enroll at Lapu-Lapu City College](/services/education/enroll-at-lapu-lapu-city-college): On-site enrollment after pre-enrollment for new, transferee and returnee students.
