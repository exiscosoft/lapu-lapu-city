# Local Civil Registrar — Lapu-Lapu City

The Local Civil Registrar's Office (LCRO) records births, marriages and deaths in Lapu-Lapu City. It also issues copies of civil registry documents, processes corrections and court orders, and issues marriage licenses.

## Mandate and Functions

The office works to strengthen public awareness and help people register vital events and civil documents on time. It is building an IT-driven records system for storing and finding civil registry records, so that the city keeps a complete, accurate and up-to-date civil registry and gives people efficient, accessible service.

Its frontline services are:

- **Civil registration**: registering births, marriages and deaths, on time or late
- **Legal and court order registration**: legitimation, use of the father's surname (Republic Act (RA) 9255), supplemental reports, corrections of clerical errors and changes of first name (RA 9048), corrections of sex or birth day and month (RA 10172), court decrees, foundlings and prenuptial agreements
- **Information and records management**: certified true copies of birth, marriage and death certificates
- **Marriage license issuance**
- **BREQ services**: Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) copies of birth, marriage and death certificates on security paper, and the Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) or Certificate of Marriage (CEMAR)
- **Electronic endorsement**: sending newly registered records, or records missing from the PSA database, to the PSA

## Services for the Public

- [Register a Birth, Death, or Marriage](/services/social-welfare/register-a-birth-death-or-marriage): Timely registration is free; late registration costs ₱100.00.
- [Correct or Update a Civil Registry Record](/services/social-welfare/correct-or-update-a-civil-registry-record): Fix clerical errors, change a first name, record legitimation or use of the father's surname, and register court decrees.
- [Get a Copy of a Birth, Marriage, or Death Certificate](/services/social-welfare/get-a-copy-of-a-birth-marriage-or-death-certificate): Certified true copies from the city's records, PSA copies and CENOMAR, and endorsement of records to the PSA.
- [Apply for a Marriage License](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-a-marriage-license): For couples aged 18 and above where at least one partner lives in Lapu-Lapu City; fees total ₱152.00.
