# City Budget Office — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Budget Office prepares the city government's budget, releases allotments to city offices, and checks that funds are available before the city spends them. It also reviews barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) budgets, and provides copies of budget documents on request.

## Mandate and Functions

The office lists these frontline services in the Citizen's Charter:

- **Budget preparation and allotment release**: reviews and consolidates budget proposals from department heads for the Local Finance Committee, and processes allotment releases and augmentations approved by the City Mayor.
- **Financial processing**: classifies and certifies available appropriations on obligation requests for salaries, honoraria, contract services, bonuses, cash assistance, allowances and grants; for projects, supplies and services; and for purchase requests.
- **Barangay budget review and advisory services**: reviews barangay annual and supplemental budgets and Sangguniang Kabataan annual and supplemental budgets, and endorses them to the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Council).
- **Administrative services**: gives copies of documents such as obligation requests, budgets and the Statement of Appropriations, Allotments, Obligations and Balances (SAAOB).

---

## Services for the Public

Most of this office's services are for city departments, suppliers and contractors, and barangay and SK officials. Anyone, from government or the private sector, can ask for copies of budget documents. See "Request for Copies of Documents" below.
