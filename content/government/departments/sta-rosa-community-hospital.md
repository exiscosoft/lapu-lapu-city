# Sta. Rosa Community Hospital — Lapu-Lapu City

Sta. Rosa Community Hospital (SRCH) is a PhilHealth-accredited Level 1 hospital in Olango. It gives emergency care, ward admission, outpatient consultations and medical records services to the people of Lapu-Lapu City.

## Mandate and Functions

**Core values:** Love, Service and Compassion.

**Vision:** Center of Quality and Excellent Services in the entire City of Lapu-Lapu.

**Mission:** Provider of Equitable, Accessible, Effective, Efficient, Safe and Quality Holistic Care for all.

**Goals and objectives:** Better health outcomes, a responsive health care system and financial risk protection for the less fortunate.

**Facility category:** Level 1 — Lapu-Lapu City Government, Department of Health (DOH) and PhilHealth Insurance Corporation.

The hospital's services are:

| Service                                       | Who it is for                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Hours                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Emergency Department (medical cases)**      | Patients who need immediate medical attention                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | 24/7                                                                |
| **Emergency Department (medico-legal cases)** | Medico-legal cases within 24 hours. Discounts apply for persons with disabilities (PWDs), senior citizens, indigents (with a certificate of indigency), sponsored members of the Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) and kasambahay (household workers); free for persons deprived of liberty (PDL) | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, except holidays. 24/7 for PDL |
| **Ward services**                             | Admitted patients                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | 24/7                                                                |
| **Outpatient Department**                     | Patients who need a medical or dental consultation, prenatal check-up, eye check-up or other services                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, except weekends and holidays  |
| **Records Section**                           | Patients and walk-in clients who need documents such as birth certificates, medical certificates and PhilHealth claims                                                                                                                                                                                    | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM                                |

Fees are posted at the hospital's billing section, Outpatient Department and Records Section.

## Services for the Public

- [Get Emergency Care at a City Hospital](/services/health-services/get-emergency-care-at-a-city-hospital): Emergency treatment 24/7 for medical cases, and handling of medico-legal cases.
- [Go to the Local Hospital for Treatment or Confinement](/services/health-services/go-to-the-local-hospital-for-treatment-or-confinement): Ward admission and care for patients who need to stay in the hospital, 24/7.
- [Consult a Doctor at a Hospital Outpatient Department](/services/health-services/consult-a-doctor-at-a-hospital-outpatient-department): Medical, dental, prenatal and eye consultations, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- [Request Hospital Medical Records and Certificates](/services/health-services/request-hospital-medical-records-and-certificates): Birth certificates, medical certificates and PhilHealth claim documents from the Records Section, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
