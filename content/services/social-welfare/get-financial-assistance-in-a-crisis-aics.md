# Get financial assistance in a crisis (AICS) — Lapu-Lapu City

If you or your family face a crisis, such as hospital bills, a death in the family, or abuse, you can ask the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) for help through its Financial Assistance in Crisis Situations (AICS) program. It is free and open to residents of Lapu-Lapu City.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO); you claim the cash at the City Treasurer's Office (CTO) |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (except holidays)                                                       |
| **Requirements**    | Barangay certificate of residency and indigency, plus documents for your type of need                       |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                                                        |
| **Processing Time** | 1 month and 30 minutes                                                                                      |

### Types of help

The charter lists requirements for these kinds of assistance:

- Medical assistance (hospital bills, medicines, diagnostic procedures, implants, chemotherapy, dialysis, or radiotherapy)
- Burial assistance
- Capital assistance for persons deprived of liberty (PDL) who have been released
- Capital assistance for livelihood
- Late registration of a birth
- Help for women in extremely difficult circumstances under Republic Act 9262

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Go to the CSWDO

- Tell the para-social worker what help you need.
- They check that your documents are complete. This takes about 5 minutes.

### Step 2: Have your interview

- If your documents are complete, the para-social worker interviews you using an intake sheet.
- The interview takes about 15 minutes.

### Step 3: Wait for approval

- A social worker prepares a referral slip for approval by the CSWDO head and the City Mayor or City Administrator.
- The city's budget, accounting, and treasury offices then process the payment papers.
- This takes about 1 month after you submit complete documents.

### Step 4: Claim your assistance

- Go to the City Treasurer's Office (CTO) and receive the cash from the disbursing officer.
- Release takes about 10 minutes.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

Everyone needs:

- [ ] Barangay certificate of residency and indigency - Office of the Barangay Captain

#### For medical assistance

- [ ] Medical certificate or medical abstract signed by the physician - attending physician or the hospital's medical records section
- [ ] Statement of account or hospital bill with the full name and signature of the billing staff, if the patient is still admitted - hospital billing clerk
- [ ] Hospital bill with promissory note, if the patient was discharged with a balance - hospital billing clerk
- [ ] Latest doctor's prescription, if the help is for medicine - attending physician
- [ ] Doctor's request, if the help is for a diagnostic procedure or for stainless or implant materials - attending physician
- [ ] Price quotation for chemotherapy, hemodialysis, or radiotherapy - authorized staff of the hospital or clinic

#### For burial assistance

- [ ] Death certificate with registry number - Local Civil Registrar (LCR), hospital, or funeral parlor
- [ ] Authorization letter, if the claimant is not an immediate family member - immediate family

#### For capital assistance after release from detention

- [ ] Copy of the release order - the trial or regional court
- [ ] Copy of the certification from the City of Lapu-Lapu Office for Substance Abuse Prevention (CLOSAP) - CLOSAP office

#### For women in extremely difficult circumstances (Republic Act 9262)

- [ ] Police report or blotter - the police station where the case was filed

Capital assistance for livelihood and help with late registration of a birth need only the barangay certificate.

### Eligibility

- You and the people you are asking help for must be residents of Lapu-Lapu City.
- You must be an individual or family in a crisis.
- A social worker assesses whether you qualify.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long before I receive the money?**
A: About 1 month after you submit complete documents. The charter's total is 1 month and 30 minutes.

**Q: Where do I get the cash?**
A: At the City Treasurer's Office, from the disbursing officer.

**Q: Who decides if I qualify?**
A: The area social worker assesses your situation. Both you and the person you are helping must be residents of Lapu-Lapu City.
