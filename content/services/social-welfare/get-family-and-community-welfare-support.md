# Get family and community welfare support — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) helps women and children who are victims of violence or abuse, overseas Filipino workers (OFWs) who come home displaced or abused, and persons deprived of liberty (PDL) who are on probation. All three services are free.

## How to Apply

### If you or your child suffered violence or abuse

1. Come to the CSWDO, or be referred by your barangay. A social worker talks with you, interviews you, and asks for your permission to proceed (20 to 30 minutes).
2. The social worker assesses your situation and needs (about 30 minutes).
3. Together you make a plan for care and treatment (about 30 minutes).
4. The social worker gives direct help, such as psychosocial support, and connects you to partner agencies if needed. This continues until the goals of your plan are reached, and then the case is closed.

If you walk in without the documents, the office will advise you how to get them.

### If you are a returning OFW

1. Ask or visit the CSWDO. Staff find out what help you need (about 10 minutes).
2. Submit your documents. Staff check that they are complete (about 10 minutes).
3. A para-social worker interviews you using an intake sheet (about 15 minutes).
4. Your request for financial assistance is prepared and approved. Approval of the payment papers takes about 10 days.
5. Claim your financial assistance from the disbursing officer at the City Treasurer's Office (CTO) (about 10 minutes).

### If you are on probation and need after-care

1. Come to the CSWDO to enroll in the after-care program. A social worker interviews you (about 15 minutes).
2. Submit your documents. The social worker checks them (about 5 minutes).
3. The social worker tells you when to start reporting and which activities to attend. They also check whether you need a referral to the Public Employment Service Office (PESO), skills training, or financial assistance.
4. Report to the CSWDO on schedule and attend all required activities, such as home visits, family sessions, values formation, the parent effectiveness seminar, and the marriage enrichment seminar. The program runs 6 months to 1 year and 2 months.
5. The social worker sends progress reports to the court. At the end, they send a final report and certificate of completion to the court, and the after-care service ends.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

#### Women and children who suffer violence or abuse

- [ ] Referral letter from the barangay Violence Against Women and Children (VAW-C) officer - barangay VAW-C office
- [ ] Police report or blotter - police station
- [ ] Medico-legal result - hospital, or the Pink Room at Vicente Sotto Memorial Medical Center (VSMMC)

#### Returning OFWs

- [ ] Valid ID - any government-issued ID
- [ ] Philippine passport - Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA)
- [ ] OWWA or NRCO certification - OWWA or NRCO
- [ ] Barangay certificate of residency - Office of the Barangay Captain

#### After-care for PDL on probation

- [ ] Referral and certificate of completion of the 6-month extensive rehabilitation - City of Lapu-Lapu Office for Substance Abuse Prevention
- [ ] Court release order - Hall of Justice

### Eligibility

- **Violence or abuse:** women and children who are victims of any form of abuse.
- **Returning OFWs:** distressed, displaced, or abused overseas Filipino workers.
- **After-care:** persons who were arrested, held in custody, detained, or imprisoned and are now on probation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I go to the CSWDO directly if I am being abused, without a barangay referral?**
A: Yes. The service covers victims who are referred and those who come forward on their own. Walk-in clients are advised on how to complete the requirements.

**Q: How long does help for returning OFWs take?**
A: The charter's total is 10 days, 1 hour and 45 minutes. Most of that is the approval of the payment papers.
