# Get livelihood capital assistance — Lapu-Lapu City

If you are an indigent resident with a small livelihood that needs more capital, the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) can help you get capital assistance. It works with partner agencies or, if needed, refers you to the city's crisis assistance program. The service is free.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO), livelihood staff                                                                                                  |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (except holidays)                                                                                                                 |
| **Requirements**    | Barangay certificate of residency and indigency, valid ID (or barangay clearance), one 2x2 photo, livelihood application form                                         |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Processing Time** | Through partner agencies: 2 to 3 months, 11 hours and 45 minutes. Through the city's Financial Assistance in Crisis Situations (AICS) program: 1 month and 25 minutes |

The CSWDO works with the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) Field Office VII, the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), and other partners.

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your application

- Fill out the livelihood profile form and submit your documents.
- Livelihood staff review them (about 10 minutes) and check that you are not already listed as a beneficiary (about 5 minutes).

### Step 2: Home and site visit

- Livelihood staff visit your home and your livelihood to assess and validate your application.
- This takes about 4 hours.

### Step 3: Attend the orientation

- If you are found eligible, attend a short orientation (about 3 hours).

### Step 4: Wait for your referral

- Staff prepare the list of qualified beneficiaries (about 30 minutes).
- They send it to a partner agency, or refer you to the AICS program for financial assistance (about 4 hours).

### Step 5: Receive your capital assistance

- **Through a partner agency:** wait for approval. This takes 2 to 3 months. The office will tell you when your application is approved.
- **Through AICS:** a para-social worker interviews you (about 15 minutes). Approval takes about 1 month after you submit complete documents. Then claim the cash from the disbursing officer at the City Treasurer's Office (about 10 minutes).

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Barangay certificate of residency and indigency - Office of the Barangay Captain
- [ ] Barangay clearance, if you have no valid ID - Office of the Barangay Captain
- [ ] Photocopy of a valid government-issued ID
- [ ] One 2x2 photo - photo studio
- [ ] Livelihood application form - CSWDO

### Eligibility

- Indigent individuals or groups with an existing livelihood and limited capital.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I apply if I don't have a business yet?**
A: The charter says the service is for indigent individuals or groups with an existing livelihood and limited capital. Ask the office about your situation.

**Q: Will someone visit my home?**
A: Yes. Livelihood staff visit your home and your livelihood to assess your application.
