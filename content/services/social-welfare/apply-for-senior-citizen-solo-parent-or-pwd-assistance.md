# Apply for a senior citizen, solo parent, or PWD ID — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) issues solo parent IDs, person with disability (PWD) IDs, and senior citizen IDs with purchase booklets to residents of Lapu-Lapu City. The service is free.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO), with the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) and the Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO) |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (except holidays)                                                                                                       |
| **Requirements**    | Depends on the ID: proof of birth, proof of residency, photos, and documents that show you are a solo parent or have a disability                           |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Processing Time** | 1 to 2 weeks and 20 minutes                                                                                                                                 |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Apply at the office

- Go to the office and fill out the application form.
- Submit the documents for the ID you need (see the lists below).
- Staff check that your documents are complete. This takes 10 to 15 minutes.

### Step 2: Wait for your ID to be approved

- Staff prepare your ID and purchase booklet.
- The office head and the City Mayor approve it.
- This takes 1 to 2 weeks.

### Step 3: Claim your ID and purchase booklet

- Return to the office to get your approved ID and purchase booklet.
- Release takes about 5 minutes.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

#### For a solo parent ID

- [ ] Photocopy of the birth certificate of each child below 18 years old - Local Civil Registrar or Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
- [ ] Death certificate of your spouse, if your spouse has died - Local Civil Registrar
- [ ] Affidavit of 2 disinterested persons who know you are a solo parent and state how you became one - Public Attorney's Office or City Legal Office
- [ ] Barangay certification that you are a solo parent - Office of the Barangay Captain
- [ ] Certification from the jail, if your spouse or common-law partner is in prison - Bureau of Corrections
- [ ] Court decision, if your marriage was annulled or you are legally separated - trial court
- [ ] 2 recent 1x1 photos - photo studio

#### For a senior citizen ID and purchase booklet

- [ ] Birth certificate or baptismal certificate - Local Civil Registrar, PSA, or the church where you were baptized
- [ ] Barangay certificate of residency or a Commission on Elections (COMELEC) certificate - Office of the Barangay Captain or COMELEC office
- [ ] 2 pieces 1x1 photos - photo studio

#### For a PWD ID and purchase booklet (adults and children)

- [ ] Certificate of disability from a government physician - government hospital
- [ ] Voter's ID of the parents or guardian - COMELEC office
- [ ] Barangay certificate of residency - Office of the Barangay Captain
- [ ] Birth certificate - Local Civil Registrar or PSA
- [ ] 2 recent 1x1 photos and 1 recent 2x2 photo - photo studio

### Eligibility

- You must be a resident of Lapu-Lapu City.
- The charter lists senior citizens and persons with disabilities. Solo parents apply through the same process with the solo parent documents above.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I have to pay for the ID or the purchase booklet?**
A: No. The charter lists no fees for this service.

**Q: How long until I get my ID?**
A: About 1 to 2 weeks after you submit complete documents.

**Q: Why do I need a senior citizen or PWD ID?**
A: The city's midyear and year-end cash aid for senior citizens and PWDs asks for this ID. Therapy and special education for children with disabilities at the CSWDO also requires a PWD ID issued in Lapu-Lapu City.
