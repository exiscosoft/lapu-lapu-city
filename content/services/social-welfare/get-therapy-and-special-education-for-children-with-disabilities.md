# Get therapy and special education for children with disabilities — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) offers free physical therapy, occupational therapy, special education (SPED), and social rehabilitation for children with disabilities aged 0 to 14. The sessions help children build skills so they can join regular classes and community life.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) therapy center (called STAC in the charter)                         |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (except holidays)                                                                  |
| **Requirements**    | PWD ID issued in Lapu-Lapu City, recent X-ray, medical certificate, and specialist referrals or test results if needed |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                                                                   |
| **Processing Time** | 3 hours and 25 minutes; each therapy session lasts 45 minutes                                                          |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Fill out the intake form

- Fill out the intake interview form.
- A social worker interviews you and recommends the right services for your child (about 10 minutes).

### Step 2: Have your child assessed

- A physical therapist, an occupational therapist, and a SPED teacher assess your child.
- This takes 30 minutes to 1 hour.

### Step 3: Attend the parents' orientation

- Attend an orientation on the center's policies, rules, and regulations (about 1 hour).

### Step 4: Join your child's therapy sessions

- Take part in each therapy session with your child.
- Each session lasts 45 minutes.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Person with disability (PWD) ID issued in Lapu-Lapu City - Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO)
- [ ] X-ray result taken within the last 6 months (all children) - City Health Office (CHO) or Lapu-Lapu City Hospital
- [ ] Medical certificate stating that your child is free from any communicable or contagious disease and fit for therapy - CHO, Vicente Sotto Memorial Medical Center (VSMMC), or any private clinic or specialist
- [ ] Physiatrist referral and recommendation, for children with mobility and transfer problems - VSMMC or any private clinic or specialist
- [ ] Neuro-developmental pediatrician referral and recommendation, for children with a suspected behavioral or developmental problem - VSMMC or any private clinic or specialist
- [ ] Developmental assessment and IQ test results, for children with a suspected cognitive, sensory, or learning problem - VSMMC or any private clinic or specialist

### Eligibility

- Children with disabilities aged 0 to 14.
- Your child needs a PWD ID issued in Lapu-Lapu City.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My child does not have a PWD ID yet. What should I do?**
A: Apply for one first. The PWD ID must be issued in Lapu-Lapu City.

**Q: Do I need all the specialist referrals?**
A: No. The physiatrist referral, the neuro-developmental pediatrician referral, and the developmental assessment and IQ test are only for children with those specific needs. All children need the PWD ID, the X-ray, and the medical certificate.

**Q: Do parents stay during therapy?**
A: Yes. The charter asks parents to take part in every therapy session.
