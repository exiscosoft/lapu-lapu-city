# Get Married by the City Mayor or at the Kasalan ng Bayan — Lapu-Lapu City

Couples living in Lapu-Lapu City can ask the City Mayor's Office to schedule their wedding with the City Mayor, or, if they qualify, join the city's free yearly Kasalan ng Bayan run by the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO).

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Wedding with the City Mayor: City Mayor's Office. Kasalan ng Bayan: City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)           |
| **When**            | Kasalan ng Bayan applications: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM (except holidays). Ask the City Mayor's Office for its hours |
| **Requirements**    | Your marriage license from the Local Civil Registrar (LCR), plus other documents depending on the option (see below)             |
| **Cost**            | No fees listed for either option                                                                                                 |
| **Processing Time** | Wedding with the City Mayor: about 1 day to get a tentative schedule. Kasalan ng Bayan: see below                                |

You need a marriage license for both options. See [Apply for a marriage license](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-a-marriage-license).

### Compare your options

| Option                          | Who can apply                                                                                               | Cost        | What to expect                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Wedding with the City Mayor** | Couples, or a spouse, living in Lapu-Lapu City                                                              | None listed | Tentative schedule in about 1 day. Schedules may change depending on the City Mayor's availability          |
| **Kasalan ng Bayan**            | Couples who have lived together for at least 5 years and are bona fide indigent residents of Lapu-Lapu City | Free        | Interview and document check, a 4-hour orientation, then the yearly Kasalan ng Bayan itself (about 5 hours) |

## How to Apply

### Option A: Wedding with the City Mayor

#### Step 1: Submit your requirements

- Bring your marriage license and voter's certificate to the City Mayor's Office.
- The staff review your documents and accept your application (about 4 hours).

#### Step 2: Wait for your schedule

- The Appointment Secretary calls or texts you with a tentative schedule (about 4 hours).
- This process only covers applications for the marriage ceremony (solemnization).
- **Schedules may change** depending on the City Mayor's availability. The office will confirm your final schedule.

### Option B: Kasalan ng Bayan

#### Step 1: Talk to the Kasalan ng Bayan focal person

- Go to the CSWDO and ask for the Kasalan ng Bayan focal person.
- The focal person interviews you and checks whether you qualify (about 10 minutes).

#### Step 2: Have your documents checked

- Hand in your complete documents. The focal person checks them and returns them to you (about 10 minutes).

#### Step 3: Submit your documents to the LCR

- Bring your complete documents to the Local Civil Registrar (LCR).

#### Step 4: Attend the orientation

- Attend the orientation and briefing for the Kasalan ng Bayan (about 4 hours).

#### Step 5: Attend the Kasalan ng Bayan

- Attend the wedding itself (about 5 hours). It is run by the CSWDO with other city offices.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Wedding with the City Mayor

- [ ] Marriage license - from the Local Civil Registrar
- [ ] Voter's certificate - from the Commission on Elections (COMELEC)

### Documents Needed: Kasalan ng Bayan

- [ ] Marriage license - from the Local Civil Registrar
- [ ] Birth certificates of the couple - from the Local Civil Registrar
- [ ] CENOMAR (Certificate of No Marriage) of the couple - from the Local Civil Registrar
- [ ] Cedula (community tax certificate) of the couple - from the City Treasurer's Office
- [ ] Barangay certificate of residency and indigency - from the Office of the Barangay Captain
- [ ] Photocopy of a valid government-issued ID
- [ ] Pre-Marriage Orientation (PMO) Certificate of Compliance - from the CSWDO

### Eligibility

- **Wedding with the City Mayor:** couples, or a spouse, living in Lapu-Lapu City.
- **Kasalan ng Bayan:** couples who have lived together for at least five years and are bona fide indigent residents of Lapu-Lapu City.

## For Solemnizing Officers: Certification for CRASM

Religious leaders and heads of religious organizations who live in Lapu-Lapu City can get a certification from the City Mayor's Office for their application for a Certificate of Registration of the Authority to Solemnize Marriage (CRASM).

| Information         | Details                                                                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Mayor's Office                                                             |
| **Who can apply**   | Religious leaders or heads of religious organizations living in Lapu-Lapu City  |
| **Cost**            | No fee                                                                          |
| **Processing Time** | 3 days. Submit your request **at least 3 working days before your target date** |

### How to apply

1. Submit a request letter addressed to the City Mayor, with your supporting documents. The staff check that they are complete and accept your application (about 30 minutes).
2. The staff prepare your certification (about 4 hours), and the City Mayor signs it (about 2 days). The staff call or text you when it is ready.
3. Claim your certification (about 30 minutes).

### Documents needed

- [ ] Request letter addressed to the City Mayor - prepared by you
- [ ] Photocopy of a valid government-issued ID - prepared by you
- [ ] Certificate of Membership from your church or religious group - from your religious organization
- [ ] Police clearance or NBI (National Bureau of Investigation) clearance - from the PNP (Philippine National Police) or the NBI
- [ ] SEC registration of your religious organization - from the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)
- [ ] Certificate of Residency - from your barangay

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the Kasalan ng Bayan really free?**
A: Yes. The charter lists no fees for any step. You still need your own documents, such as the marriage license and cedula.

**Q: Will my wedding with the City Mayor happen on the date I am given?**
A: The schedule you receive is tentative. It may change depending on the City Mayor's availability, and the office will confirm the final schedule.

**Q: How early should a solemnizing officer request the CRASM certification?**
A: At least 3 working days before your target date.
