# Apply for a Marriage License — Lapu-Lapu City

Get a marriage license from the Local Civil Registrar (LCR) of Lapu-Lapu City if you plan to marry and at least one of you lives in the city. You first attend a Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counselling seminar at the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO).

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Attend the Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counselling (PMO/C)

- Go to the CSWDO with a photocopy of your valid government-issued ID.
- Fill out the Marriage Expectation Inventory (MEI) form (about 10 minutes). The staff then give you a seminar schedule.
- Attend the seminar together (about 4 hours).
- The CSWDO prepares your certificate of compliance and has it signed by the resource speaker. Claim it when it is ready.

### Step 2: Fill out the application and submit your requirements at the LCR

- Fill out the application form and hand it in with your requirements (about 10 minutes).
- If your application is complete, the staff ask you to pay at the cashier or the City Treasurer's Office (CTO).

### Step 3: Show your official receipt and attend a short interview

- Present your official receipt (about 5 minutes).
- The City Civil Registrar or an authorized officer interviews you and signs your application, and the parental consent or advice form if needed (about 10 minutes).

### Step 4: Log your application and get a claim stub

- Return the application form to the Marriage Section and sign the logbook.
- The staff log your application, tell you when to collect your license, and give you a claim stub (about 10 minutes).
- A Notice of Application for Marriage License is posted on the bulletin board for 10 days. After that, the license is signed and approved.

### Step 5: Claim your marriage license

- Return on the date given and claim your license (about 5 minutes).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Certificate of Live Birth - PSA (Philippine Statistics Authority) copy. If entries are blurred, also attach a recent copy from the local civil registrar
- [ ] CENOMAR (Certificate of No Marriage) - valid for 3 months
- [ ] Certificate of Residency stating how many months or years you have lived there - from the barangay where you have lived for at least 6 months
- [ ] Certificate of Marriage Counseling Seminar - from the Lapu-Lapu City CSWDO (Step 1)
- [ ] Valid ID showing an address in Lapu-Lapu City, and cedula (community tax certificate), for each applicant
- [ ] **If you are below 25:** parental consent or advice. Your parents must come to the LCR in person with a valid ID and sign the form during your application
- [ ] **If you are a widow or widower:** death certificate of your late spouse (from the local civil registrar or a PSA copy)
- [ ] **If your previous marriage was annulled:** your previous Certificate of Marriage with the annulment annotation, and a certified true copy of the annulment decision and its finality

**Additional requirements for foreign applicants**

- [ ] Legal Capacity to Marry or No Impediment to Marry - from your consulate or embassy in the Philippines
- [ ] Divorce decree (English version)
- [ ] Passport with the latest entry stamp in the Philippines
- [ ] CENOMAR issued by the PSA
- [ ] Birth certificate (English version)

**For the Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counselling**

- [ ] Photocopy of a valid government-issued ID
- [ ] Completed Marriage Expectation Inventory (MEI) form - from the CSWDO

You can order PSA copies of your birth certificate and CENOMAR through the LCR. See [Get a copy of a birth, marriage, or death certificate](/services/social-welfare/get-a-copy-of-a-birth-marriage-or-death-certificate).

### Eligibility

- A man and a woman who want to marry each other
- Both applicants are 18 or older
- Each applicant is single, widowed, annulled, or divorced
- At least one applicant lives in Lapu-Lapu City

1. Male and Female persons desirous of getting married

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do we need to attend the counselling seminar before applying?**
A: Yes. The Certificate of Marriage Counseling Seminar from the CSWDO is one of the LCR's requirements.

**Q: Why do we have to wait before we get the license?**
A: The LCR posts a Notice of Application for Marriage License on its bulletin board for 10 days before the license is signed and released.

**Q: Can my parents just sign the consent form at home?**
A: No. If you are below 25, your parents must come to the LCR in person with a valid ID and sign the form during your application.

**Q: Is there a fee for the seminar?**
A: The charter lists no fee for the seminar, but one step tells couples to "pay immediately the corresponding fee". Ask the CSWDO.
