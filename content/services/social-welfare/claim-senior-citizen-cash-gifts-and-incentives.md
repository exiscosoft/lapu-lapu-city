# Claim senior citizen cash gifts and incentives — Lapu-Lapu City

Registered senior citizens and persons with disabilities (PWDs) in Lapu-Lapu City can receive cash from the city government: a birthday cash gift, midyear and year-end cash aid, and a cash award for residents who reach 100. If you missed your birthday cash gift, you can ask the City Mayor's Office to put you back on the payroll.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Make sure you are registered

- You need a senior citizen ID (from OSCA) or a PWD ID (from PDAO).
- If you do not have one yet, [apply for your ID first](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-senior-citizen-solo-parent-or-pwd-assistance).

### Step 2: Receive your birthday cash gift

- OSCA staff prepare the payroll and get it approved by the city's budget, accounting, and treasury offices and the City Mayor or City Administrator.
- When the cash gift is ready, OSCA tells the Barangay Mayor's Office, whose staff deliver it to you.
- Delivery takes 1 to 2 hours.

### Step 3: Claim your midyear and year-end cash aid

- OSCA and PDAO staff prepare and process the payroll. This takes 1 to 2 weeks.
- They send a letter of distribution to each barangay (1 to 3 days).
- Go to the payout in your barangay and bring your senior citizen or PWD ID. Distribution runs for 2 to 3 days.

### Step 4: For a centenarian, have the family submit proof of age

- A family member brings the documents to the OSCA office (10 minutes).
- OSCA staff visit the centenarian at home for an interview (10 minutes).
- Staff call the family with the payout schedule.
- The City Mayor hands over the cash award, with the disbursing officer and OSCA staff.

### Step 5: If you missed your birthday cash gift, ask for a re-payroll

- Write a request letter addressed to the City Mayor and attach a photocopy of your OSCA ID.
- Submit it to the City Mayor's Office within the same calendar year as your missed birthday cash gift.
- Approval takes about 3 days. The request then goes to OSCA for payroll processing.
- Payroll may take 1 to 3 months. The City Treasurer's Office will tell you the payout schedule.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Senior citizen ID or PWD ID, for midyear and year-end cash aid - OSCA or PDAO office
- [ ] ID card from OSCA, for the birthday cash gift - OSCA office
- [ ] Request letter addressed to the City Mayor, for a re-payroll - you write it
- [ ] Photocopy of your OSCA ID, for a re-payroll - you prepare it

For the centenarian cash award, bring one of these as proof of age:

- [ ] Senior citizen ID from OSCA that shows the year of birth - OSCA
- [ ] Other Philippine government ID that shows the year of birth - the issuing government office
- [ ] Baptismal certificate - the church where the centenarian was baptized
- [ ] Marriage certificate of the centenarian - Local Civil Registrar (LCR) or Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
- [ ] Birth certificates of the centenarian's children - LCR or PSA

### Eligibility

- Birthday cash gift: registered senior citizens of Lapu-Lapu City.
- Midyear and year-end cash aid: registered senior citizens and PWDs of Lapu-Lapu City.
- Centenarian cash award: residents of Lapu-Lapu City who are 100 years old or older.
- Re-payroll: registered OSCA senior citizens who could not claim their birthday cash gift during the scheduled distribution. You must file within the same calendar year.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I missed my birthday cash gift. Can I still get it?**
A: Yes, if you apply within the same calendar year. Send a request letter to the City Mayor with a photocopy of your OSCA ID. After approval, payroll may take 1 to 3 months, and the City Treasurer's Office will tell you when to claim it.

**Q: Do PWDs get the midyear and year-end cash aid?**
A: Yes. Registered senior citizens and registered PWDs of Lapu-Lapu City can both claim it.

**Q: Who can I ask about my cash gift?**
A: Contact your Senior Citizen Chapter or the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA).
