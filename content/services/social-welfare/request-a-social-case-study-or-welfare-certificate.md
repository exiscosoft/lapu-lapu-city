# Request a social case study or welfare certificate — Lapu-Lapu City

If another office or agency asks you for a social welfare document, the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) can prepare it. This includes a parental capability assessment report, a social case study or social case summary report, a certificate of indigency, a letter of authority, and a referral or endorsement letter. The service is free.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (except holidays)                          |
| **Requirements**    | Request letter or list of requirements from the office asking for the document |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                           |
| **Processing Time** | 2 to 6 days and 6 minutes                                                      |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Bring your request letter to the CSWDO

- Present the request letter or list of requirements from the office or agency that needs the document.
- A social worker reviews it (about 3 minutes).

### Step 2: Home visit

- A social worker visits your home (about 1 day).

### Step 3: Wait while your document is prepared

- The social worker prepares the document and gets it approved.
- This takes 2 to 5 days.

### Step 4: Receive your document

- The social worker gives you the approved document (about 3 minutes).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a fee for a certificate of indigency or a case study?**
A: No. The charter lists no fees for these documents.

**Q: Will a social worker visit my home?**
A: Yes. The charter includes a home visit before the document is prepared.
