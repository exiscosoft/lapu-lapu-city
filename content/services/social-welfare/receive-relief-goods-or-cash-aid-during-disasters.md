# Receive relief goods or cash aid during disasters — Lapu-Lapu City

If you or your family are hit by a disaster such as a fire, the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) can give you food and non-food relief items and financial assistance. The service is free and available 24 hours a day, every day.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | The area social worker assigned to your barangay (City Social Welfare and Development Office); cash is released at the City Treasurer's Office (CTO)                                                                          |
| **When**            | Monday to Sunday, 24 hours, including holidays                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Requirements**    | Barangay certificate that you were affected by the disaster; more documents for cash aid                                                                                                                                      |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Processing Time** | Relief goods are released on the same visit. The charter's total is 11 days and 32 minutes, but it also says approval of cash aid takes 1 month after you submit complete documents. Ask the office for the current timeline. |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Go to your area social worker

- Approach the area social worker assigned to your barangay.
- They do a short intake interview (about 2 minutes).

### Step 2: Get food and non-food items

- Submit your barangay certificate. The social worker prepares a relief slip (about 5 minutes).
- Claim your food and non-food items (about 15 minutes).

### Step 3: Apply for financial assistance

- Submit the documents for cash aid (see the list below).
- The social worker prepares a referral slip for approval by the CSWDO head and the City Mayor or City Administrator.
- The city's budget, accounting, and treasury offices then process the payment papers.
- The charter says this takes 1 month after you submit complete documents.

### Step 4: Claim your cash aid

- Go to the City Treasurer's Office (CTO) and receive the cash from the disbursing officer.
- Release takes about 10 minutes.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

#### For food and non-food items

- [ ] Barangay certificate stating that you are a victim of, or were affected by, the disaster - Office of the Barangay Captain

#### For financial assistance

- [ ] Barangay certificate stating that you are a victim of, or were affected by, the disaster - Office of the Barangay Captain
- [ ] Barangay certificate of residency and indigency - Office of the Barangay Captain
- [ ] Certificate from the Bureau of Fire, for fire victims - Bureau of Fire office

### Eligibility

- Any individual or family who is a victim of a disaster.

## Contact Information

- **Office**: City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO), through the area social worker assigned to your barangay
- **Hours**: Monday to Sunday, 24 hours, including holidays

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I get help on a weekend or holiday?**
A: Yes. Emergency disaster relief is available 24 hours a day, Monday to Sunday, including holidays.

**Q: I lost my home in a fire. What else do I need for cash aid?**
A: Besides the two barangay certificates, bring a certificate from the Bureau of Fire.
