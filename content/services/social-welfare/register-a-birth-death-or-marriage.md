# Register a Birth, Death, or Marriage — Lapu-Lapu City

Record a birth, marriage, or death with the Local Civil Registrar (LCR) of Lapu-Lapu City. Registering on time is free; late registration needs more documents and has fees.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Local Civil Registrar (LCR), 2nd Floor, City Library Bldg., Lapu-Lapu City Government Center                                                                                                      |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Requirements**    | On-time registration: the completed certificate (four original copies for a birth or marriage). Late registration: a Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) negative result plus supporting papers |
| **Cost**            | On-time registration is free. Late registration: ₱100, plus ₱80 for the Certificate of No Record. Other fees may apply (see below)                                                                |
| **Processing Time** | On time: about 3 days after you pay. Late: your application is posted for 10 consecutive days before release                                                                                      |

All documents you submit must be **originals**.

### Fees at a glance

| Type                                                              | Fee  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- |
| On-time (timely) registration                                     | Free |
| Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father (AUSF), if you use one | ₱100 |
| Late registration                                                 | ₱100 |
| Certificate of No Record (late registration)                      | ₱80  |
| LCR service fee for out-of-town applications                      | ₱100 |

You pay fees at the cashier inside the LCR office or at the City Treasurer's Office (CTO).

### On time or late?

The charter does not state the deadline for on-time registration. It does say that you need a PSA negative result when the person is 1 year old or older (for a birth), or when 1 year or more has passed since the marriage or death. **Ask the LCR whether your registration counts as on time or late.**

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your requirements

- Bring your complete requirements to the LCR. There are separate staff for births, marriages, and deaths.
- The staff check that your papers are complete and correct. This takes about 10 minutes for on-time registration and 20 minutes for late registration.
- If everything is in order, the staff send you to pay at the cashier inside the LCR office or at the CTO.

### Step 2: Hand in your official receipt

- Give your official receipt to the LCR staff.
- **On-time registration:** the staff process your registration. This takes about 3 days.
- **Late registration:** your application is posted for 10 consecutive days before it is processed.
- For a death certificate, the LCR staff sign it and stamp the registry number, then the City Civil Registrar reviews and signs it.

### Step 3: Claim your documents

- Return to the LCR on the date the staff give you.
- Show your claim stub and/or a valid ID. Release takes about 5 minutes.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: On-time Registration

**Birth**

- [ ] Four original copies of the Certificate of Live Birth
- [ ] Muslim Form, for Muslim registrants
- [ ] **If the parents are not married and the child will use the father's surname:**
  - The "Paternity" portion on the back of the Certificate of Live Birth, signed by both parents and notarized. If the father is a minor, his parent also signs the certificate.
  - A notarized Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father (AUSF), signed by the mother. If the mother is a minor, her parent also signs the AUSF.
  - Valid IDs of the parents, and of a grandparent if a parent is a minor

**Marriage**

- [ ] Four original copies of the Certificate of Marriage, signed by the solemnizing officer, the husband and wife, and all witnesses
- [ ] A copy of your marriage license, if it was issued by another city or municipality

**Death**

- [ ] Certificate of Death, filled in by the funeral home or hospital and signed by the doctor who attended the deceased and by the City Health Office (CHO) officer
- [ ] The informant's certification, filled in and signed by the relative(s) of the deceased

### Documents Needed: Late Registration of a Birth

- [ ] PSA negative result (for a person 1 year old or older)
- [ ] Any **two** of these: baptismal certificate, school record, income tax return, insurance policy, medical or immunization record (certified true copy from the issuing health center), voter registration record, senior citizen record, SSS E-1 / GSIS / Pag-IBIG record, PhilHealth Member Data Record (MDR), or the birth certificate of the person's child or children
- [ ] Marriage contract of the parents
- [ ] Marriage contract of the person being registered, if married
- [ ] National ID of the person being registered
- [ ] Barangay certification as proof of residence
- [ ] Unedited, front-facing 2x2 color photo with a white background
- [ ] Valid IDs of the parents. For a foreign parent: passport, plus a Bureau of Immigration (BI) Clearance Certificate or an Alien Certificate of Registration (ACR) I-Card
- [ ] Birth certificates of the parents
- [ ] Affidavit of Delayed Registration (on the back of the Certificate of Live Birth)

The charter lists these as **additional requirements when the parents are not married**:

- [ ] Affidavit of Paternity, or the paternity portion on the back of the Certificate of Live Birth
- [ ] Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father
- [ ] For a child aged 7 to 17: an Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father signed by the child, with the child's valid ID, and the attestation of the mother or guardian
- [ ] Affidavit of two disinterested persons on the facts of birth
- [ ] Affidavit of Discrepancy
- [ ] Affidavit for out-of-town application for late registration, with two corroborators

Ask the LCR which of these apply to your case. **A person 18 or older who is being registered late must appear in person.**

### Documents Needed: Late Registration of a Marriage

- [ ] PSA negative result (1 year or more after the date of marriage)
- [ ] Original Certificate of Marriage, or the record issued by the church or civil wedding
- [ ] Affidavit on the facts of the marriage, stating why it was not registered. It may be signed by:
  - the couple;
  - one spouse, stating where the other spouse is, with a supporting document; or
  - the surviving children, with a birth certificate showing the parents' names
- [ ] Valid ID of the couple, spouse, or informant
- [ ] Affidavit of Kinship (if the couple is absent)
- [ ] Affidavit of two disinterested persons on the facts of the marriage, with photocopies of the witnesses' IDs
- [ ] Affidavit for out-of-town application for late registration, with two corroborators

### Documents Needed: Late Registration of a Death

- [ ] PSA negative result (1 year or more after the date of death)
- [ ] Original Certificate of Death signed by the City Health Office
- [ ] Medical certificate from the hospital, or a burial or cremation certificate from the church, cemetery, or crematorium
- [ ] Affidavit on the facts of the death, stating why it was not registered, signed by the nearest kin (spouse, children, parents, or sibling), with documents showing their relationship to the deceased
- [ ] Affidavit of Kinship
- [ ] Valid ID of the nearest kin (informant)
- [ ] Affidavit of two disinterested persons on the facts of the death and why it was not registered, with the IDs of both witnesses
- [ ] Affidavit for out-of-town application for late registration of death, with two corroborators

### Eligibility

You can apply if you are:

- the person named in the document, aged 18 or older;
- a parent or the spouse of that person; or
- a child, brother, sister, or attorney-in-fact, if that person is absent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I have to pay to register a birth, marriage, or death on time?**
A: No. On-time registration is free. If you file an Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father (AUSF), that costs ₱100.

**Q: Why does late registration take longer?**
A: After you pay, your application is posted for 10 consecutive days before the LCR processes it.

**Q: Does the person being registered need to come to the office?**
A: For a late birth registration, yes, if that person is 18 or older.
