# Correct or Update a Civil Registry Record — Lapu-Lapu City

Fix an error, add a missing entry, or record a legal change (such as legitimation, use of the father's surname, or a court decision) on a birth, marriage, or death record at the Local Civil Registrar (LCR) of Lapu-Lapu City.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Where**           | Local Civil Registrar (LCR), 2nd Floor, City Library Bldg., Lapu-Lapu City Government Center                                               |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM                                                                                                       |
| **Requirements**    | Depends on the service. Most need a Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) copy and an LCR certified true copy of the record, plus valid ID |
| **Cost**            | From free (foundling registration) to ₱3,000 (change of first name or RA 10172 correction), plus related fees                              |
| **Processing Time** | Filing takes under an hour. Corrections filed online add posting, publication, and approval time (see below)                               |

All documents must be the **original or a certified true copy** from the issuing office, **with two photocopies**.

## Which Service Do You Need?

The LCR handles several kinds of changes. Find your situation below.

| Your situation                                                                               | Service                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| An entry on a birth, marriage, or death certificate has a clerical (typing or writing) error | Republic Act (RA) 9048 Correction of Clerical Error (CCE) |
| You want to change your first name                                                           | RA 9048 Change of First Name (CFN)                        |
| Your sex, or the day and month of your birth, is wrong on your birth certificate             | RA 10172 correction                                       |
| A child of unmarried parents will use the father's surname                                   | RA 9255 (Use of the Surname of the Father)                |
| The child's parents have since married each other and want the child legitimated             | Legitimation                                              |
| Same as above, and one or both parents were minors                                           | RA 9858 (Legitimation to Minor Parents)                   |
| An entry was left out (blank) on a birth, marriage, or death certificate                     | Supplemental Report                                       |
| A court has decided a case that affects the record                                           | Registration of a court decree                            |
| You need to register a foundling (an infant, child, or adult)                                | Foundling registration                                    |
| You signed a pre-nuptial agreement (marriage settlement) before your wedding                 | Registration of a pre-nuptial agreement                   |

The court decrees the LCR registers are: correction of entries, declaration of nullity of marriage, presumptive death, recognition of foreign judgment, and adoption.

### About corrections filed online (APCAS)

Clerical error corrections, first name changes, and RA 10172 corrections are filed **online through APCAS**, the online system the LCR uses to submit these petitions. The charter lists this online filing for people born in Lapu-Lapu City. If you were born somewhere else, the charter lists a separate "migrant" fee; ask the office whether it applies to you.

For a **first name change** or an **RA 10172 correction**, your petition must also be published in a newspaper once a week for two consecutive weeks: a local newspaper if you were born in Lapu-Lapu City, or a national newspaper if you were born elsewhere.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your requirements

- Bring your complete requirements to the LCR's legal and court order registration staff.
- The staff check that your papers are complete and correct (about 20 minutes).
- If everything is in order, the staff send you to pay at the cashier inside the LCR office or at the City Treasurer's Office (CTO).

### Step 2: Hand in your official receipt

- Give your official receipt to the staff. They process your request (about 30 minutes).
- **For a clerical error correction:** the staff prepare your Petition for Correction and Verification. Have it notarized, then bring the notarized papers back to the staff.

### Step 3: Wait for the online petition (CCE, CFN, and RA 10172 only)

- The LCR submits your documents online through APCAS (about 3 days).
- Your petition is posted for 10 consecutive days.
- For a first name change or RA 10172 correction, your petition is also published in a newspaper for two consecutive weeks. You submit the publisher's Affidavit of Publication and the newspaper clippings as proof.
- Once your petition is approved, the LCR annotates your civil registry record within 10 working days of receiving the approved petition.

### Step 4: Claim your documents

- Return to the LCR on the date the staff give you. Release takes about 5 minutes.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

You will usually need a **PSA copy** and an **LCR certified true copy** of the record. You can request both through the LCR (see [Get a copy of a birth, marriage, or death certificate](/services/social-welfare/get-a-copy-of-a-birth-marriage-or-death-certificate)).

**Legitimation and RA 9858** (place of birth is Lapu-Lapu City)

- [ ] PSA copy and LCR certified true copy of the Certificate of Live Birth
- [ ] Certificate of CEMAR (Advisory on Marriages) of both parents
- [ ] Parents' marriage contract or certificate (certified true copy or PSA copy)
- [ ] Death certificate, if one parent has died
- [ ] Valid IDs of the parents
- [ ] Birth document of the child acknowledged by the father
- [ ] Affidavit of Acknowledgement by the father
- [ ] Affidavit of Legitimation by the parents
- [ ] For RA 9858: Joint Supplemental Affidavit of the parents stating that one or both were minors
- [ ] Out-of-town application with two corroborators (if the child was born outside Lapu-Lapu City)
- [ ] Mailing fee to PSA, Quezon City, or to another municipality

**RA 9255 (Use of the Surname of the Father)**

- [ ] PSA copy and certified true copy of the Certificate of Live Birth
- [ ] Valid IDs of the parents and/or the child
- [ ] Birth document of the child acknowledged by the father
- [ ] Affidavit of Acknowledgement by the father
- [ ] Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father by the mother
- [ ] Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father by the child, and Affidavit of Attestation by the mother or guardian
- [ ] Out-of-town application with two corroborators (if the child was born outside Lapu-Lapu City)
- [ ] Mailing fee to PSA, Quezon City, or to another municipality

**Supplemental Report**

- [ ] PSA copy and certified true copy of the birth, marriage, or death certificate
- [ ] Supporting documents for the missing entry, such as the baptismal or birth certificate of the child, mother, or father; the birth certificate of a sibling; the elementary school record (Form 137); the voter registration record from the Commission on Elections (COMELEC); or your own or your parents' marriage certificate
- [ ] Supplemental Affidavit signed by the mother, father, the person named in the record, the spouse, or the children
- [ ] Out-of-town application with two corroborators (if the child was born outside Lapu-Lapu City)
- [ ] Valid ID of the parent and/or the person named in the record
- [ ] Mailing fee to another municipality

**RA 9048 Correction of Clerical Error**

- [ ] PSA copy and certified true copy of the birth, marriage, or death certificate
- [ ] Supporting documents for the data to be corrected, such as the birth or baptismal certificate of the person or parents; school record (Form 137 or transcript of records); voter registration record (COMELEC); National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) certificate; police clearance; marriage certificate of the parents or the person (if married); or the birth certificate of a sibling
- [ ] Valid ID of the petitioner
- [ ] Mailing fee to another municipality

**RA 9048 Change of First Name and RA 10172 (sex, or day and month of birth)**

- [ ] PSA copy and certified true copy of the Certificate of Live Birth
- [ ] Birth certificate and baptismal certificate of the person
- [ ] Elementary school record (Form 137)
- [ ] Voter registration record (COMELEC)
- [ ] Marriage certificate of the person (if married)
- [ ] For a correction of sex: medical record, and a medical certificate from an accredited government physician stating that the petitioner has not undergone sex change or sex transplant
- [ ] Proof of no pending administrative, civil, or criminal case or criminal record: clearance from your employer (if employed) or an Affidavit of Unemployment (if unemployed), NBI certificate, and police clearance
- [ ] Proof of newspaper publication: the publisher's Affidavit of Publication and clippings of the published petition
- [ ] Valid ID of the petitioner
- [ ] Mailing fee to another municipality

**Registration of a court decree**

What you bring depends on where the court hearing was held and where the event (birth, marriage, or death) happened:

| Hearing and event                                       | Bring                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hearing outside Lapu-Lapu City, event in Lapu-Lapu City | 3 sets of the Certificate of Finality, Court Decision, and Petition (certified true copies from the Clerk of Court of the RTC branch); 3 copies each of the Certificate of Authenticity and Certificate of Registration from the LCR where the court is; PSA copy of the record |
| Hearing in Lapu-Lapu City, event outside Lapu-Lapu City | 4 sets of the Certificate of Finality, Court Decision, and Petition (certified true copies from the Clerk of Court of the RTC branch)                                                                                                                                           |
| Hearing and event both in Lapu-Lapu City                | 3 sets of the Certificate of Finality, Court Decision, and Petition (certified true copies from the Clerk of Court of the RTC branch); PSA copy of the record                                                                                                                   |

RTC means Regional Trial Court.

**Pre-nuptial agreement**

- [ ] Pre-nuptial agreement or marriage settlement, signed by the couple and notarized **before the wedding day**
- [ ] Submit it together with the Certificate of Marriage to the registering LCR (see [Register a birth, death, or marriage](/services/social-welfare/register-a-birth-death-or-marriage))
- [ ] Valid IDs of the couple

**Foundling found in the Philippines**

- [ ] Four copies of the completed Certificate of Live Birth (OCRG Form No. 101)
- [ ] Affidavit of the finder
- [ ] For an infant or child: certification or blotter from the barangay captain or police on how the foundling was found (if the child was found in a different barangay from where the finder lives, both barangays issue one), and a report from the National Authority for Child Care (NACC) signed by the authorized officer
- [ ] For an adult foundling: a report from the local social welfare and development office on the foundling's background, estimated age, and qualifications as a foundling
- [ ] Valid IDs of the finder and the NACC or social welfare officer

### Eligibility

You can apply if you are:

- the person named in the document (petitioner), aged 18 or older;
- a parent or the spouse of that person; or
- a child, brother, sister, or attorney-in-fact, if that person is absent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why does a first name change take longer than a clerical error correction?**
A: Both are posted for 10 consecutive days, but a first name change (and an RA 10172 correction) must also be published in a newspaper for two consecutive weeks.

**Q: How long after approval will my record be updated?**
A: The LCR annotates your record within 10 working days of receiving the approved petition.

**Q: Is there a fee to register a foundling?**
A: No. Foundling registration is free.
