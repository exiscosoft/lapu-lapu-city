# Get a Copy of a Birth, Marriage, or Death Certificate — Lapu-Lapu City

Request a certified copy of a birth, marriage, or death record from the Local Civil Registrar (LCR) of Lapu-Lapu City, order a Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) copy through the LCR, or have the LCR endorse a record to the PSA when the PSA has no copy.

**What is BREQ?** BREQ is the service the LCR uses to order PSA copies for you. The LCR takes your request and the PSA fee, encodes the request, sends it to the PSA, and releases the PSA security paper copies to you once they arrive.

**CENOMAR** is the Certificate of No Marriage. **CEMAR** is the Advisory on Marriages.

## How to Apply

### Option A: Local copy from the LCR

1. Get a request form from the LCR staff and fill it out. Pay the fee and keep your official receipt.
2. Hand in the completed form with your official receipt. The staff check your ID and any authorization letter (about 5 minutes).
3. The records section searches for and prints your record (about 30 minutes).
4. Claim your document (about 5 minutes).

### Option B: PSA copy through BREQ

1. Hand in a completed request form to the LCR staff in charge and pay the PSA fee. You get an official receipt and a BREQ temporary receipt (about 5 minutes per copy).
2. The LCR encodes your request, deposits the fees, and submits your request to the PSA.
3. The LCR collects the processed copies from the PSA after about 5 working days. This can vary case to case.
4. Return to the LCR to claim your PSA security paper copies (about 10 minutes).

### Option C: Electronic endorsement to the PSA

1. Present your official receipt and requirements to the LCR staff (about 5 minutes).
2. **If the PSA has no record but the LCR does:** the LCR schedules mailing of your record to the PSA in Manila.
3. **If your record was registered on time:** the staff will advise you to follow up one month later for an update.
4. Return to the LCR on the date the staff give you to claim your documents. The staff will explain the mailing procedure (about 10 minutes).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

**For a local copy or a PSA copy (BREQ)**

- [ ] Completed request form - from the LCR staff in charge
- [ ] A valid ID with your photo and signature, such as a driver's license, school ID, office ID, GSIS, SSS, or passport
- [ ] **If you are a representative:** an authorization letter or notarized special power of attorney (SPA) signed by the document owner, stating the type of document requested, plus the valid IDs (original or photocopy) of both the owner and the representative
- [ ] **If you are requesting for a public office:** a letter from the office requesting a copy for administrative purposes

**For an electronic endorsement**

- [ ] The registered Certificate of Live Birth, Marriage, or Death
- [ ] **Newly registered record:** a letter requesting electronic endorsement, addressed to the PSA Regional Director, and proof of urgency
- [ ] **No PSA record but the LCR has one:** a PSA negative certificate, and the mailing fee
- [ ] A valid ID with your photo and signature, such as a driver's license, school ID, office ID, GSIS, SSS, or passport
- [ ] **If you are a representative:** an authorization letter, plus the valid IDs of both the owner and the representative
- [ ] **If you are requesting for a public office:** a letter from the office requesting a copy for administrative purposes

### Eligibility

**Local copy or PSA copy (BREQ):** parents, the person named in the record (owner), an authorized representative, or a court with a court order.

**Electronic endorsement:** the person named in the document (aged 18 or older), a parent or the spouse, or a child, brother, sister, or attorney-in-fact if that person is absent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can someone else request my certificate for me?**
A: Yes. They need an authorization letter or notarized special power of attorney signed by you that names the type of document, plus valid IDs for both of you.

**Q: How long does a PSA copy take?**
A: The LCR collects PSA copies after about 5 working days, depending on the case. You then claim them at the LCR.

**Q: The PSA says it has no record of my birth, but the LCR has it. What do I do?**
A: Ask the LCR for an electronic endorsement. Bring a PSA negative certificate and pay the endorsement, issuance, documentary stamp, and mailing fees. The LCR mails your record to the PSA in Manila.
