# Join livelihood or skills training programs — Lapu-Lapu City

The city government offers free skills training for residents who want to find work or start a small business. The City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) runs practical skills training with partner agencies, and the Manpower and Human Development Training Center (MHDTC) runs sewing and dressmaking courses.

---

## What You Need to Know

| **Where**           | City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO), or Manpower and Human Development Training Center (MHDTC) - Mainland     |
| **When**            | CSWDO: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (except holidays). MHDTC: you will get a text or call with your training schedule |
| **Requirements**    | Proof of birth, barangay certificate or clearance, photos, and school records; MHDTC also asks for a voter's certification   |

### Compare the programs

| Program                                                        | Who can join                                                                                 | Training length                                 | Cost |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---- |
| CSWDO practical skills development training                    | Ages 18 to 60, high school graduates who want to be trained                                  | 15 to 30 days of classes                        | Free |
| MHDTC industrial high-speed sewing machine operations          | Registered voters and residents of Lapu-Lapu City, ages 18 to 40, at least elementary level  | 1-day orientation and 14 or 20 days of training | Free |
| MHDTC community-based dressmaking leading to Dressmaking NC II | General public, registered voters of Lapu-Lapu City, of legal age, at least elementary level | 1-day orientation and 22 days of training       | Free |

The CSWDO training is run with the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) and other partners. The charter gives a total time of 15 to 30 days, 12 hours and 35 minutes. The MHDTC charter gives no total time.

NC II stands for National Certificate II. The sewing course prepares you for jobs in factories and tailoring shops, or for starting your own sewing business. The dressmaking course covers industrial sewing machines and pattern drafting. Both MHDTC courses are open to men, women, and LGBTQIA+ applicants.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your registration and documents

- **CSWDO:** fill out the registration (learner's profile) form and submit it with your documents. The focal person checks them (about 15 minutes).
- **MHDTC:** submit the complete documents required by both MHDTC and the partner company. Staff check your documents and record your name and contact details.

### Step 2: Wait for your training schedule

- **CSWDO:** the focal person sends the list of qualified applicants to the partner agencies, then tells you your training schedule.
- **MHDTC:** wait for a text or call from the MHDTC office with your schedule.

### Step 3: Attend the orientation

- **CSWDO:** attend the launching and orientation (about 4 hours).
- **MHDTC:** be on time for the orientation (1 day).

### Step 4: Complete the training

- **CSWDO:** attend all classes for 15 to 30 days. The office checks for full attendance.
- **MHDTC:** sewing training runs 14 or 20 days; dressmaking runs 22 days.

### Step 5: Finish and get your certificate

- **CSWDO:** attend the graduation (about 5 hours).
- **MHDTC:** take the final assessment and receive your certificate at the culmination (4 to 5 hours).
- **MHDTC sewing only:** you are then endorsed to a company in the Mactan Economic Zone (MEZ) for its final assessment (1 day).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

#### CSWDO practical skills training

- [ ] Photocopy of your marriage certificate, if married - Local Civil Registrar (LCR) or Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
- [ ] Photocopy of your birth certificate - LCR or PSA
- [ ] Barangay certificate of residency and indigency - Office of the Barangay Captain
- [ ] 3 passport-size photos with white background, and 3 1x1 photos wearing a shirt with a collar - photo studio
- [ ] Photocopy of your high school diploma - the school you graduated from or attended

#### MHDTC sewing course: MHDTC requirements

- [ ] Diploma or certificate - the school you attended or graduated from
- [ ] NSO/PSA certificate - LCR, PSA office, or online
- [ ] Barangay clearance - your barangay
- [ ] Voter's certification - Commission on Elections (COMELEC) office, 2nd floor, Lapu-Lapu City main building
- [ ] 2 pieces 1x1 ID photos - nearest photo studio

#### MHDTC sewing course: company requirements

- [ ] Resume or biodata - you prepare it
- [ ] NSO/PSA certificate (4 copies) - LCR, PSA office, or online
- [ ] Social Security System (SSS) E1 form or SSS ID, with a clear SSS number - SSS, ground floor, Annex Bldg., Gaisano Mactan Mall, Pajo
- [ ] PhilHealth - Mactan Economic Zone II, Pueblo Verde, SM Hypermart
- [ ] Pag-IBIG - 2nd floor, Gaisano Mactan Convention Center, Pajo
- [ ] National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) clearance - 3rd floor, Island Central Mall, Pusok
- [ ] Marriage certificate or contract, if married (4 copies) - LCR, PSA office, or online
- [ ] Birth certificates of your children, if you are married or a single parent (4 copies) - LCR, PSA office, or online
- [ ] Diploma (elementary, high school, or college), or Form 138-A report card if you have no diploma - your school
- [ ] Transcript of records, for college graduates - your school
- [ ] Certificate of employment, if you have worked before - your previous company
- [ ] Certificate of training, seminar, or on-the-job training, if any - your school
- [ ] Application letter - you prepare it

#### MHDTC dressmaking course

- [ ] NSO/PSA certificate - LCR, PSA office, or online
- [ ] Barangay clearance - your barangay
- [ ] Voter's certification - COMELEC office, 2nd floor, Lapu-Lapu City main building
- [ ] One 1x1 ID photo - nearest photo studio

The charter's dressmaking list starts at item 2, so one item may be missing. Ask MHDTC for the complete list before you apply.

### Eligibility

- **CSWDO training:** ages 18 to 60, high school graduate, and willing to be trained.
- **MHDTC sewing:** registered voter and resident of Lapu-Lapu City, ages 18 to 40, elementary level or higher.
- **MHDTC dressmaking:** open to the general public; registered voter of Lapu-Lapu City, of legal age, elementary level or higher.

### Industrial High-Speed Sewing Machine Operations

This training program is designed to equip participants with the knowledge, skills, and work discipline required for employment in the garment and manufacturing industry. It focuses on the operation of industrial high-speed sewing machines, proper handling of materials, and the execution of basic to advanced sewing techniques used in mass production.

Trainees will learn machine parts and maintenance, threading, stitching methods, quality control standards, and workplace safety. The program also emphasizes speed, accuracy, and efficiency to meet industry production targets.

Upon completion, participants are expected to be job-ready and capable of working in factories, tailoring shops, or starting their own small sewing-related business.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I have to pay for the training?**
A: No. The CSWDO lists no fees, and MHDTC lists "No Fees / Charges".

**Q: Will the sewing course help me get a job?**
A: After the course, MHDTC endorses trainees to a company in the Mactan Economic Zone for its final assessment. That is why the sewing course also lists company requirements.
