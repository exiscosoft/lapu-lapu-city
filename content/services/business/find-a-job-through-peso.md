# Find a Job Through PESO — Lapu-Lapu City

The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) helps jobseekers find local and overseas work. PESO screens you based on your skills and refers you to job vacancies from private employers, for free. Schools can also ask PESO to hold career guidance sessions for their students.

## How to Apply

### Get a Job Referral

#### Step 1: Look at the Job Vacancies

- Check the job vacancies PESO has posted from private companies.

#### Step 2: Fill Out the NSRP Form 1

- Get the NSRP Form 1 from PESO and fill it out (about 5 minutes).
- Write the position and company you want to apply to.

#### Step 3: Submit Your Form and Resume

- Give PESO staff your completed NSRP Form 1 and your updated bio-data or resume.
- Staff interview you (about 15 minutes) and record your profile (about 5 minutes).

#### Step 4: Bring Your Referral Letter to the Company

- PESO gives you a referral letter to bring to the company, and endorses you to the employer.

### Request a Career Guidance Session (for Schools)

#### Step 1: Send a Letter Request

- The school sends a letter request to the PESO Manager, with the program flow.
- PESO acknowledges the request and schedules the activity (about 3 days).

#### Step 2: Gather the Students

- The school organizes and gathers the students for the career talk and the Career Development and Support Program (CDSP) orientation.
- PESO staff give the talk and have the students fill out NSRP Form 1 (about 1 hour), then collect attendance sheets (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 3: Give a Certificate of Appreciation

- The school gives PESO a Certificate of Appreciation confirming the activity was held (about 30 minutes).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Job Referral

- [ ] Updated bio-data, resume or curriculum vitae - prepare it yourself
- [ ] National Skills Registration Program (NSRP) Form 1 - PESO

### Documents Needed: Career Guidance (for Schools)

- [ ] Letter request from the school addressed to the PESO Manager - the school
- [ ] Program flow - the school

### Eligibility

- Job referral: jobseekers, senior high school graduates, college-level students, college graduates, displaced workers and returning Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs)
- Career guidance: Grade 10, Grade 12 and graduating college students, through their school

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does PESO charge for job referrals?**
A: No. The service is free.

**Q: Can PESO help me find work overseas?**
A: Yes. PESO gives referrals for both local and overseas employment.

**Q: Who can use PESO's referral service?**
A: Jobseekers, senior high school graduates, college-level students, college graduates, displaced workers and returning OFWs.

**Q: How do I give feedback or file a complaint?**
A: Fill out a feedback survey and drop it in the PESO drop box. For complaints, write a letter to the PESO Manager or ask for a schedule of dialogue.
