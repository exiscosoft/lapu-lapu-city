# Rent Stalls in Public Markets — Lapu-Lapu City

The Market and Slaughterhouse Division runs the city's public market. This page is for stall lessees paying stall rent and electricity or getting a market clearance, and for vendors who need cash tickets for extra space or goods brought in for sale.

---

| Information         | Details                                                                                    |

| **Where**           | Public market, through the market collectors and the Market and Slaughterhouse Division    |
| **When**            | Ask the office                                                                             |

## How to Apply

### Pay Your Stall Rent and Electricity

#### Step 1: Show Your Previous Receipt or Bill

- Give the market collector your previous Official Receipt or your bill.
- If you don't have your previous receipt, tell the collector the lessee's name, section and stall number.

#### Step 2: Pay the Amount Due

- The collector computes the amount you owe and issues an Official Receipt (3 minutes with a receipt or bill, about 5 minutes without).

### Get a Market Clearance

#### Step 1: Update Your Payments

- Pay any unpaid rent or electricity at the cashier. Staff check the records for unpaid bills.

#### Step 2: Pay the Clearance Fee and Fill Out the Form

- Pay the ₱150 market clearance fee. You receive an Official Receipt and the market clearance form.
- Fill out the form.

#### Step 3: Get the Form Signed

- The staff member who posts rent payments signs first, to confirm your rent is updated.
- The staff member who handles electricity accounts signs second.
- The market supervisor signs last.
- The whole process takes 5 to 7 minutes.

### Buy Cash Tickets

#### If You Display Goods in an Extra Space

- Set up your goods in the area. The market collector issues you a cash ticket. The fee depends on the size of the space you use (about 30 seconds).

#### If You Bring Goods Into the Market for Sale

- The market collector issues a cash ticket for every delivery of goods brought in for sale. The fee depends on the kind and number of goods (1 to 3 minutes).

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Previous Official Receipt (optional) - from your last payment
- [ ] Your stall rent or electricity bill
- [ ] Receipts showing your payments are updated (for a market clearance) - from your past payments

### Eligibility

- Stall rent, electricity and market clearance: stall lessees
- Cash tickets: all vendors who occupy extra space or bring goods into the market for sale

The charter does not cover how to lease a new stall. Ask the Market and Slaughterhouse Division about available stalls and how to apply.
