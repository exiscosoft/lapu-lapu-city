# Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes — Lapu-Lapu City

Every year you must renew your Business Mayor's Permit and pay your local business taxes to the City Treasurer's Office. This page is for business owners, companies and organizations paying business tax, other local taxes, fees and charges to the city.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Where**           | Cashiers, City Treasurer's Office                                                                      |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, except holidays                                                  |
| **Due Dates**       | 1st quarter: January 20; 2nd quarter: April 20; 3rd quarter: July 20; 4th quarter: October 20          |
| **Requirements**    | The basis for your payment (such as your Tax Order of Payment) and the payor's written or printed name |
| **Cost**            | Based on the city's Schedule of Taxes, Fees and Charges                                                |
| **Processing Time** | About 6 minutes at the cashier                                                                         |

If a quarterly due date falls on a weekend or holiday, the office opens that day only to accept payments.

---

## How to Renew and Pay

### Step 1: File Your Renewal Application

- Renew your Business Mayor's Permit at the Business One-Stop-Shop (BOSS) or online at online.lapulapucity.gov.ph.
- Bring or upload the renewal requirements listed below.
- Staff check your business for unpaid amounts from past years, then compute your taxes and fees.
- You receive a Business Tax Order of Payment.
- See [Apply for a business permit](/services/business/apply-for-a-business-permit-in-lapu-lapu-city) for the full step-by-step process and processing times.

### Step 2: Get a Queue Number

- At the City Treasurer's Office, get a priority number from the queuing machine. Staff can help you use it (about 1 minute).

### Step 3: Pay at the Cashier

- When your number is called, give your Tax Order of Payment to the cashier and pay.
- You receive an Official Receipt (OR) or Community Tax Certificate (CTC) (about 5 minutes).
- Keep your Official Receipt. You need it to claim your permit and as proof of payment.

### Step 4: Pay the Next Quarters on Time

- If you pay quarterly, pay each quarter on or before its due date: April 20, July 20 and October 20.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed to Pay

- [ ] Basis for the payment - your Tax Order of Payment or Business Tax Order of Payment from the assessor
- [ ] Written or printed name of the payor

### Documents Needed to Renew Your Permit

The office may ask for more documents depending on the nature of your business.

- [ ] Filled-out Unified Application Form - BOSS or the online portal
- [ ] Basis for computing your taxes, fees and charges (for example, your declared gross sales or receipts for the previous year)
- [ ] Contract of Lease - your lessor
- [ ] Community Tax Certificate (CTC) - City Treasurer's Office
- [ ] Sanitary Permit - see [Secure a Sanitary Permit](/services/health-services/secure-a-sanitary-permit-for-your-business)
- [ ] Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) - Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)

### Who Pays

- The general public
- Public or private companies
- Public or private organizations

### Tips

- Prepare your documents in December so you can file early in January.
- Bring copies of everything, plus soft copies on your phone.
- If you renovated or added equipment, bring the related permits and invoices.
- For food and health businesses, schedule your sanitary inspection early and make sure your staff have updated health cards.
- If you pay quarterly, keep a calendar of the April, July and October due dates.
- Late payment leads to surcharges and interest. Ask the City Treasurer's Office how much applies to you.
- If you stop operating, close your business formally so taxes stop adding up. See [Close or retire your business](/services/business/close-or-retire-your-business).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When are business taxes due?**
A: Quarterly, on January 20, April 20, July 20 and October 20.

**Q: What if the due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or holiday?**
A: The City Treasurer's Office opens that day only to accept payments, so you can still pay on time.

**Q: What do I need to bring to pay?**
A: The basis for your payment, such as your Tax Order of Payment, and the payor's name written or printed.
