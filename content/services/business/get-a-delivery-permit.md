# Get a Delivery Permit — Lapu-Lapu City

Businesses with trucking or delivery services need a Delivery Permit from the City Mayor's Office for their vehicles. You get the permit and its sticker in about 3 days.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Mayor's Office; pay at the City Treasurer's Office; claim at the Business One-Stop-Shop (BOSS) |
| **When**            | Apply at least 3 working days before you need the permit                                            |
| **Requirements**    | Booking form, vehicle registration and official receipt for each vehicle, and your business permit  |
| **Cost**            | Set by the City Treasurer's Office                                                                  |
| **Processing Time** | 3 days                                                                                              |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit Your Requirements

- Get a booking form from the City Mayor's Office and fill it out.
- Submit it with your other requirements. Staff check your documents and receive your application (about 30 minutes).

### Step 2: Wait for Approval

- Your application goes to the staff in charge for approval (about 1 hour).
- Staff call or text you once it is approved and refer you to the City Treasurer's Office for payment.

### Step 3: Pay the Fees

- Pay at the City Treasurer's Office and get your Official Receipt (OR) (allow up to 4 hours).

### Step 4: Submit Your Receipt

- Bring your OR to the City Mayor's Office.
- Staff prepare your Delivery Permit (about 1 day), and it is signed (about 1 day).

### Step 5: Claim Your Permit and Sticker

- Claim your Delivery Permit with its sticker at the Business One-Stop-Shop (about 30 minutes).

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Accomplished booking form - City Mayor's Office
- [ ] Clear copy of the Certificate of Registration (COR) of each vehicle - prepare it yourself
- [ ] Clear copy of the Official Receipt (OR) of each vehicle - prepare it yourself
- [ ] Clear copy of your Business Permit, if your business address is in Lapu-Lapu City - see [Apply for a business permit](/services/business/apply-for-a-business-permit-in-lapu-lapu-city)

### Eligibility

- Business establishments with trucking or delivery services

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My business is outside Lapu-Lapu City. Do I need a business permit copy?**
A: The business permit copy is required only if your business address is in Lapu-Lapu City.

**Q: Do I need separate documents for each truck?**
A: Yes. Bring a clear copy of the Certificate of Registration and the Official Receipt for every vehicle.

**Q: Where do I get the sticker?**
A: You claim the Delivery Permit and its sticker at the Business One-Stop-Shop.
