# Hire Workers or Join a Job Fair as an Employer — Lapu-Lapu City

Companies and establishments can get accredited by the Public Employment Service Office (PESO) to post job vacancies and find workers, and can join job fairs that PESO organizes. Both services are free.

---

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

## How to Apply

### Get Your Company Accredited

#### Step 1: Fill Out the NSRP Form 2

- Get the National Skills Registration Program (NSRP) Form 2 from PESO or the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) website and fill it out (about 15 minutes).

#### Step 2: Email Your Form and Documents

- Email the completed NSRP Form 2 with the required documents to PESO.
- PESO checks them. If anything is missing, PESO tells you what to complete. If your documents are complete, PESO confirms receipt and gives you the name and position of the staff member who received them, and the date and time (about 1 day).

### Join a PESO Job Fair

#### Step 1: Send a Letter of Intent

- Send PESO a letter of intent to join the job fair.
- PESO replies and asks you to complete the simple accreditation process: submit the requirements and a filled-out NSRP Form 2 (about 3 days).

#### Step 2: Wait for Confirmation

- PESO checks your documents and tells you if anything is missing.
- If slots are still open, PESO confirms your participation. If all slots are filled, your request is declined (about 3 days).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

The same documents apply to both accreditation and job fair participation.

- [ ] Letter request addressed to the PESO Manager - your company
- [ ] Brief company profile - your company
- [ ] Valid Business Permit for the current year - see [Apply for a business permit](/services/business/apply-for-a-business-permit-in-lapu-lapu-city)
- [ ] BIR Form 2303 (Certificate of Registration) - Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)
- [ ] SEC Registration - Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)
- [ ] Screenshot of your PhilJobNet registration - PhilJobNet website (free online registration)
- [ ] List of job vacancies, with the number of openings, qualifications and job descriptions - your company
- [ ] Your recruitment process - your company
- [ ] NSRP Form 2 - PESO or the DOLE website

**Additional documents for some companies:**

- [ ] Local manpower agencies: DOLE Department Order 174 Certificate, and a Certificate of No Pending Case (valid within 6 months from the date of issue) - DOLE
- [ ] Construction companies: Philippine Contractors Accreditation Board (PCAB) license - PCAB office
- [ ] Overseas recruitment agencies: Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) License to Operate - DMW

### Eligibility

- Establishments and companies

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to be accredited to join a job fair?**
A: Yes. When you send your letter of intent, PESO asks you to complete the simple accreditation process first.

**Q: Is my job fair slot guaranteed?**
A: No. PESO confirms your participation only if slots are still open.

**Q: Can I submit the accreditation documents online?**
A: Yes. You can email the completed NSRP Form 2 and your documents to PESO.
