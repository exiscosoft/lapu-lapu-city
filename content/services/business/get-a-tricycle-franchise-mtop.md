# Get or Renew a Tricycle Franchise (MTOP) — Lapu-Lapu City

A Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP) is the city franchise you need to operate a tricycle for hire. The City Attorney's Office issues new MTOPs for Olango Island, renews existing ones, and updates them when the owner or the motorcycle changes.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Where**           | City Attorney's Office; fees are paid at the City Treasurer's Office (CTO)                                                                                                                                                           |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, except holidays and government-sanctioned activities                                                                                                                                           |
| **Requirements**    | Valid government ID, motorcycle registration or existing MTOP, and the official receipts for the fees                                                                                                                                |
| **Cost**            | New, renewal or change of ownership: ₱50 Mayor's permit, ₱40 supervision fee, ₱60 franchise fee, ₱450 filing fee (first 5 units) and ₱100 Persons with Disability (PWD) sticker; change of ownership adds ₱100. Change of unit: ₱200 |
| **Processing Time** | About 1 day and 1 to 2 hours, depending on the service                                                                                                                                                                               |

### Which Service Do You Need?

| Service                       | Who it is for                                                               | Main fees                                                                                         | Processing time                   |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| New MTOP (Olango Island only) | Filipino citizens; partnerships or corporations at least 60% Filipino-owned | Mayor's permit ₱50 per unit, supervision fee ₱40, franchise fee ₱60, filing fee, PWD sticker ₱100 | About 1 day and 1 hour 10 minutes |
| Renewal                       | Holders of an old or existing MTOP                                          | Same as a new MTOP                                                                                | About 1 day and 2 hours           |
| Change of ownership           | MTOP holders transferring the franchise                                     | Same as a new MTOP, plus ₱100 transfer fee                                                        | About 1 day and 1 hour 55 minutes |
| Change of motorcycle unit     | MTOP holders replacing their motorcycle                                     | ₱100 amendment fee plus ₱100 transfer fee                                                         | About 1 day and 1 hour 15 minutes |

The filing fee is ₱450 for the first 5 units and ₱50 for each additional unit. The charter also lists a City Traffic Management Office (CTMS) certification fee, tricycle stickers and a body plate fee among the receipts you need, but does not give their amounts. Ask the office.

## How to Apply

### New MTOP (Olango Island)

#### Step 1: Submit Your Requirements

- Bring your requirements to the City Attorney's Office.
- Staff check them, assign a body number and tell you what to pay (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 2: Pay the Fees

- Pay at the City Treasurer's Office and get your Official Receipt (OR) (about 5 minutes).

#### Step 3: Fill Out the Application Form

- Return to the City Attorney's Office with your OR and your motorcycle's Certificate of Registration (CR).
- Fill out the MTOP application form and the PWD sticker form. Staff help you (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 4: Wait for Approval

- The City Attorney's Office reviews and signs your application (about 1 day).

#### Step 5: Claim Your MTOP

- Claim your MTOP at the City Attorney's Office (about 5 minutes).

### Renewal or Change of Ownership

#### Step 1: Submit Your Requirements

- Bring the original of your old or existing MTOP and your other requirements to the City Attorney's Office.
- Staff confirm your MTOP against office records. For a change of ownership, they also check your Deed of Sale or other proof of transfer (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 2: Pay the Fees

- Pay at the City Treasurer's Office and get your Official Receipt (about 5 minutes).

#### Step 3: Fill Out the Application Form

- Submit your OR and CR, and fill out the application form and the PWD sticker form (about 20 minutes).
- Staff tell you how to get your clearance from the City Traffic Management Office (CTMS).

#### Step 4: Get Your CTMS Clearance

- Bring your tricycle to the CTMS for inspection, with a copy of your MTOP and your OR.
- CTMS staff inspect the tricycle and issue a clearance (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 5: Submit the CTMS Clearance

- Give the clearance to the City Attorney's Office. Staff prepare your renewed MTOP, in the new owner's name for a change of ownership (about 25 to 30 minutes).

#### Step 6: Wait for Approval

- The City Attorney's Office reviews and signs your MTOP (about 1 day).

#### Step 7: Claim Your Renewed MTOP

- Claim it at the City Attorney's Office (about 5 minutes).

### Change of Motorcycle Unit

#### Step 1: Submit Your Requirements

- Bring the original MTOP, the new motorcycle's CR, a valid ID and your Affidavit of Change of Unit to the City Attorney's Office (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 2: Pay the Fees

- Pay the change of unit fees at the City Treasurer's Office (about 5 minutes).

#### Step 3: Submit Your Receipt

- Give your OR to the staff in charge (about 5 minutes).
- Staff update your MTOP record to show the new motorcycle (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 4: Wait for Approval and Claim

- The City Attorney's Office reviews and signs your MTOP (about 1 day).
- Claim your updated MTOP (about 5 minutes).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: New MTOP (Olango Island)

- [ ] Certificate of Registration (CR) of the motor vehicle - Land Transportation Office (LTO)
- [ ] Certificate of Residency - your Barangay Chairman's office
- [ ] 1 valid government ID
- [ ] Official Receipts for the annual franchise fee, filing fee, CTMS certification fee, Mayor's permit, supervision fee, tricycle stickers and body plate fee - City Treasurer's Office

### Documents Needed: Renewal

- [ ] Original of your old or existing MTOP
- [ ] 1 valid government ID
- [ ] Official Receipts for the annual franchise fee, filing fee, CTMS certification fee, Mayor's permit, supervision fee and tricycle stickers - City Treasurer's Office

### Documents Needed: Change of Ownership

- [ ] Original of the old or existing MTOP
- [ ] Original notarized Deed of Sale, Extrajudicial Settlement, Waiver of Rights or other proof of transfer
- [ ] Certificate of Residency - your barangay
- [ ] 1 valid government ID
- [ ] Official Receipts for the annual franchise fee, transfer fee, filing fee, CTMS certification fee, Mayor's permit, supervision fee and tricycle stickers - City Treasurer's Office

### Documents Needed: Change of Motorcycle Unit

- [ ] Original of the old or existing MTOP
- [ ] Certificate of Registration of the new motorcycle - Land Transportation Office (LTO)
- [ ] 1 valid government ID
- [ ] Affidavit of Change of Unit
- [ ] Official Receipt for the change of unit fee - City Treasurer's Office

### Eligibility

- New MTOP (Olango Island): Filipino citizens, or partnerships and corporations with at least 60% Filipino equity or ownership
- Renewal: holders of an old or existing MTOP
- Change of ownership: MTOP holders
- Change of motorcycle unit: bona fide MTOP holders

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I get a new MTOP outside Olango Island?**
A: The charter lists new MTOP issuance for Olango Island only. Ask the City Attorney's Office about other areas.

**Q: How much is the filing fee if I have more than 5 tricycles?**
A: ₱450 covers your first 5 units. Each additional unit costs ₱50.

**Q: Do I need to bring my tricycle?**
A: For a renewal or change of ownership, yes. You bring it to the City Traffic Management Office for inspection before you get your clearance.

**Q: I bought a tricycle with an existing franchise. What do I do?**
A: Apply for a change of ownership. Bring the original MTOP and the original notarized Deed of Sale or other proof of transfer.
