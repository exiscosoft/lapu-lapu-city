# Get an Occupational Permit for Employment — Lapu-Lapu City

An Occupational Permit, also called a Mayor's Permit for Employment, shows that you are allowed to work or practice your profession in Lapu-Lapu City. It is for Filipino citizens of legal age working in the city, and for Filipino tour guides whose tour packages include the city.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Office of the City Administrator; pay at the City Treasurer's Office                                                   |
| **When**            | Ask the office                                                                                                         |
| **Requirements**    | Original Police or National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) Clearance, original City Health Card, and one 2x2 ID picture |
| **Cost**            | ₱100; tour guides pay ₱600 (₱100 plus ₱500); free for first-time jobseekers with an Oath of Undertaking                |
| **Processing Time** | 1 hour                                                                                                                 |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit Your Requirements

- Bring your documents to the Office of the City Administrator.
- Staff check that they are complete and tell you to pay (about 10 minutes).

### Step 2: Pay the Fee

- Pay at the cashier of the City Treasurer's Office and get your Official Receipt (OR).
- The fee is ₱100. Tour guides pay an additional ₱500.

### Step 3: Submit Your Documents Again With the Receipt

- Return to the Office of the City Administrator with all your documents and the OR.
- Staff process your permit (about 15 minutes) and the City Administrator approves it (about 30 minutes).

### Step 4: Claim Your Permit

- Sign the logbook and claim your signed permit (about 5 minutes).

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Police Clearance or National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) Clearance (original) - any police or NBI office that issues them
- [ ] City Health Card (original) - Lapu-Lapu City Health Office; see [Apply for a Health Card](/services/health-services/apply-for-a-health-card-for-work-or-study)
- [ ] 1 piece 2x2 ID picture

### Additional for First-Time Jobseekers (Free Permit)

Under Republic Act 11261, the First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act, first-time jobseekers can get the permit for free.

- [ ] Oath of Undertaking (original) - the barangay where you live

### Additional for Tour Guides

- [ ] Certificate of Training (photocopy) - Department of Tourism (DOT)

### Eligibility

- Filipino citizens of legal age who practice their profession or occupation in Lapu-Lapu City
- Filipino tour guides with tour packages that include Lapu-Lapu City

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I am applying for my first job. Do I have to pay?**
A: No. Bring an original Oath of Undertaking from your barangay and the permit fee is waived under the First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act (Republic Act 11261).

**Q: How much do tour guides pay?**
A: ₱600 in total: the ₱100 permit fee plus an additional ₱500 tour guide fee.

**Q: Can I bring a photocopy of my clearance and health card?**
A: No. The Police or NBI Clearance and the City Health Card must be originals.
