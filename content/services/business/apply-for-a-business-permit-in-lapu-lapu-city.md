# Apply for a Business Permit — Lapu-Lapu City

You need a Business Mayor's Permit to operate a business legally in Lapu-Lapu City. This guide is for proprietors, corporations, partnerships, cooperatives and associations applying for a new permit or renewing one, either at the Business One-Stop-Shop (BOSS) or online.

## How to Apply

You can apply in person at the BOSS or through the city's online portal. Both follow the same three stages: apply, pay, then claim.

### Option A: Apply in Person

#### Step 1: Apply

- Fill out the Unified Application Form and attach the documents listed under Requirements below.
- Submit them at the BOSS. Staff receive and check your application (about 30 minutes).
- If you are renewing, staff check your business for unpaid amounts from past years. If there are any, they print a Notice to Comply (about 1 hour).
- An assessor computes your taxes and fees and prints your Business Tax Order of Payment (BTOP) (about 1 hour).
- Your application is then routed to the other offices that must review it: the Real Property Tax Division, the city health office, the City Planning and Development Office, the Office of the Building Official and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) (about 1 hour).

#### Step 2: Pay

- Pay the amount on your BTOP to an authorized collector at the City Treasurer's Office.
- You receive an Official Receipt (OR) (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 3: Submit and Claim

- Submit your OR and any remaining requirements. Staff check your documents for the Business Mayor's Permit (about 1 hour).
- The permit is approved (about 1 day).
- Claim your Business Mayor's Permit (about 30 minutes).

### Option B: Apply Online

#### Step 1: Create an Account and Apply

- Go to online.lapulapucity.gov.ph and sign up for a user account.
- Select **Business Permit** in the dashboard menu.
- Fill out the Business Application Form and upload your documents.
- Staff check your documents and give you an assessment (5 to 10 minutes).

#### Step 2: Pay Online

- Choose a payment gateway and pay.
- Staff confirm your payment and approve your permit (2 to 5 minutes).
- If you want the paper Official Receipt, claim it at the cashier. Show a screenshot or the confirmation of your successful payment.

#### Step 3: Print Your Permit

- Print your Business Permit from the portal (1 to 2 minutes).

Courier delivery of the Official Receipt is available on a case-to-case basis. Contact the BOSS releasing staff at 0927-2002-821.

## Requirements

The office may ask for more documents depending on the nature of your business.

### Documents Needed: New Business

- [ ] Filled-out Unified Application Form - BOSS or the online portal
- [ ] DTI, SEC or CDA registration (Department of Trade and Industry, Securities and Exchange Commission, or Cooperative Development Authority, depending on your type of business)
- [ ] Occupancy Permit (if you are the lessor) - see [Get an occupancy permit](/services/housing-land-use/get-an-occupancy-permit)
- [ ] Contract of Lease (if you rent or lease your space) - your lessor
- [ ] Community Tax Certificate (CTC) - City Treasurer's Office
- [ ] Sanitary Permit - see [Secure a Sanitary Permit](/services/health-services/secure-a-sanitary-permit-for-your-business)
- [ ] Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) - Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)

### Documents Needed: Renewal

The renewal checklist is on the [Renew permits and pay local business taxes](/services/business/renew-permits-and-pay-local-business-taxes) page.

### Eligibility

- Proprietors (sole proprietorships)
- Corporations
- Partnerships
- Cooperatives
- Associations

### Tips

- Your taxes and fees depend on the size, nature and location of your business.
- Incomplete documents delay your permit. Check the list above before you go.
- Renew on time to avoid penalties.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does a business permit cost?**
A: It depends on your type of business. The office computes your taxes and fees using the city's Schedule of Taxes and Fees, and lists them on your Business Tax Order of Payment.

**Q: Do I need a contract of lease if I own the building?**
A: For a new application, no. The contract of lease is only needed if you rent or lease your space. If you are the lessor, bring your Occupancy Permit instead.

**Q: Can I apply without going to City Hall?**
A: Yes. You can apply, pay and print your permit through the online portal at online.lapulapucity.gov.ph.

**Q: How long does a walk-in application take?**
A: The approval step alone takes about 1 day. Add a few hours for evaluation, assessment, payment and release, so plan for at least 1 to 2 working days if your documents are complete.
