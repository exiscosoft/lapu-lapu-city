# Get a Permit for Banners, Streamers or Signage — Lapu-Lapu City

Businesses and companies need a special permit from the City Mayor's Office to put up temporary banners, streamers and other promotional materials, or permanent directional signs. The City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) issues the certification you need to get your tarpaulin cash bond refunded.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Mayor's Office (permits); City Environment and Natural Resources Office (cash bond refund certification) |
| **When**            | Apply at least 7 working days ahead for banners, and at least 20 working days ahead for permanent signs       |
| **Requirements**    | Request letter to the City Mayor, sample layout with dimensions and location photo, and office endorsements   |
| **Cost**            | Permit fees as set by the City Treasurer's Office; ₱50 for the cash bond refund certification                 |
| **Processing Time** | Banners: 7 days. Directional signs: 15 days. Cash bond refund certification: 3 days                           |

## How to Apply

### Banners, Streamers or Directional Signs (City Mayor's Office)

#### Step 1: Get Your Endorsements

- Your request must first be approved by the offices listed above. For banners, get endorsements from CENRO and the CTMS. For permanent directional signs, also get endorsements from the City Planning and Development Office and the Office of the City Engineer. These offices may inspect your request.

#### Step 2: Submit Your Request

- Bring your request letter, sample layout and endorsements to the City Mayor's Office.
- Staff check your documents and receive your application (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 3: Wait for Approval

- Your request goes to the City Mayor or the approving authority.
- Approval takes about 3 days for banners and about 7 days for directional signs.
- Staff call or text you about the approval. Once approved, they refer you to the City Treasurer's Office for payment (about 1 hour for banners, up to 3 days for directional signs).

#### Step 4: Pay the Fees

- Pay at the City Treasurer's Office and get your Official Receipt (OR) (allow up to 4 hours).

#### Step 5: Submit Your Receipt

- Bring your OR to the City Mayor's Office. Staff prepare your special permit and have it signed (about 2 days for banners, about 3 days for directional signs).
- Staff call or text you when it is ready.

#### Step 6: Claim Your Special Permit

- Claim your special permit at the City Mayor's Office (about 30 minutes).

### Cash Bond Refund Certification for Tarpaulins (CENRO)

#### Step 1: Submit Your Permit and Photos

- Bring your approved permit to advertise and photos of your advertising materials (tarpaulins, standees, posters, placards, banners and similar items) to CENRO.
- Staff check the permit's expiration date and your photos (about 10 minutes).

#### Step 2: Wait for the Site Check

- A CENRO inspector visits the site to verify (about 2 hours) and submits an inspection report.
- Staff prepare your Certification for Release of Advertisement Bond, and the head of CENRO signs it.

#### Step 3: Claim Your Certification

- Sign the receiving copy and claim your certification. The whole process takes about 3 days.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Banners, Streamers and Promotional Materials (Temporary)

- [ ] Request letter addressed to the City Mayor, stating the number of pieces and other details - prepare it yourself
- [ ] Sample layout with dimensions and proposed location, with a photo - prepare it yourself
- [ ] Endorsement - City Environment and Natural Resources Office
- [ ] Endorsement - City Traffic Management Office

### Documents Needed: Directional Signs (Permanent)

- [ ] Request letter addressed to the City Mayor - prepare it yourself
- [ ] Sample layout with dimensions and proposed location, with a photo - prepare it yourself
- [ ] Endorsement - City Planning and Development Office
- [ ] Endorsement - Office of the City Engineer
- [ ] Endorsement - City Environment and Natural Resources Office
- [ ] Endorsement - City Traffic Management Office

### Documents Needed: Cash Bond Refund Certification

- [ ] Approved permit to advertise
- [ ] Photos of your advertising materials (tarpaulins, standees, posters, placards, banners and similar items)

### Eligibility

- Banner and sign permits: business establishments and companies (sole proprietorships, partnerships and corporations)
- Cash bond refund certification: individual residents and public or private entities

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How early should I apply?**
A: At least 7 working days before your target date for banners and streamers, and at least 20 working days before for permanent directional signs.

**Q: How much is the permit?**
A: The City Mayor's Office refers you to the City Treasurer's Office, which charges the applicable taxes and fees. The charter does not list the amounts.

**Q: How do I get my tarpaulin cash bond back?**
A: After your display, bring your approved permit to advertise and photos of your materials to CENRO. After a site check, CENRO issues a Certification for Release of Advertisement Bond within about 3 days, for a ₱50 fee.
