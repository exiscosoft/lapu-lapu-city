# Apply for the Summer Job Program — Lapu-Lapu City

The Summer Job for the Youth Program gives poor but deserving students, out-of-school youth (OSY) and children of displaced workers a paid temporary job for 3 days. It runs every summer and at other times of the year, to add to the family's income and help the young person stay in school.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Your barangay and the Public Employment Service Office (PESO)                                                                                                                                  |
| **When**            | Every summer and other times of the year; ask PESO or your barangay about the next schedule                                                                                                    |
| **Requirements**    | National Skills Registration Program (NSRP) Form 1, Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) birth certificate, grades, Certificate of Indigency, and other documents depending on your situation |
| **Cost**            | Free. You are paid for your work                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Processing Time** | About 33 days and 30 minutes from registration to receiving your pay                                                                                                                           |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Register With Your Barangay

- Register for the program at your barangay. PESO staff and barangay officials assess registered applicants (about 3 days).

### Step 2: Fill Out the NSRP Form 1

- Get the National Skills Registration Program (NSRP) Form 1 application form and fill it out.

### Step 3: Submit Your Documents

- Submit the completed NSRP Form 1 and your documents.
- PESO checks them. If anything is missing, PESO tells you what to complete. If your documents are complete, PESO gives you a schedule for the orientation (about 3 days).

### Step 4: Attend the Orientation

- Attend the program orientation, where PESO explains the rules and the dos and don'ts.
- Your attendance is recorded, and PESO announces when the program starts.

### Step 5: Work for 3 Days

- Do your assigned work for 3 days. PESO and barangay officials document the activity.

### Step 6: Receive Your Pay

- PESO prepares the payroll and tells your barangay officials when salaries are ready.
- Your salary is released with the help of the City Treasurer's Office (about 20 days after your work).

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] NSRP Form 1 - PESO or your Barangay Chairman
- [ ] Birth Certificate - Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
- [ ] Previous and current grades - your school
- [ ] Certificate of Indigency - your barangay
- [ ] Notice of Termination or Retrenchment (if your parent was displaced or terminated) - your parent's company
- [ ] Certification of Out-of-School Youth (for OSY only) - your barangay

### Eligibility

- You are a student or out-of-school youth aged 15 to 25.
- Your parents' combined net income, including your own income if any, does not exceed the regional poverty threshold.
- If you are a student, you have a passing general weighted average (GWA).
- If you are not in school, your barangay certifies that you are an out-of-school youth.
- Children of displaced workers can also apply.

## Official Citizen's Charter

### D.) Summer Job For the Youth Program

This is the City's youth employment-bridging program that aims to provide temporary jobs for a period of three (3) days to poor but deserving students, out-of-school youth, and dependents of displaced or to be displaced workers every summer and/or any time of the year to ensure that the family's income is increased to help the beneficiary continue his/her education.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long is the job?**
A: 3 days.

**Q: How old do I have to be?**
A: At least 15 and not more than 25 years old.

**Q: I am not in school. Can I still apply?**
A: Yes. Get a Certification of Out-of-School Youth from your barangay and include it with your documents.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: PESO processes the payroll after your 3 days of work. Releasing salaries takes about 20 days.
