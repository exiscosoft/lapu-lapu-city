# Apply for the Government Internship Program — Lapu-Lapu City

The Government Internship Program (GIP) gives young people aged 18 to 30 with no work experience a chance to work on government projects and programs. It is part of the national KABATAAN 2000 program, and the Public Employment Service Office (PESO) handles applications in Lapu-Lapu City.

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Send a Letter of Intent

- Apply by sending PESO a letter of intent. PESO assesses registered applicants (about 1 day).

### Step 2: Fill Out the GIP Application Form

- PESO gives you the application form and the list of requirements (about 1 day).

### Step 3: Submit Your Documents

- Submit your completed application form and documents.
- PESO checks them. If anything is missing, PESO lists what you need to complete. If your documents are complete, PESO gives you a schedule for the orientation (about 3 days).

### Step 4: Attend the Orientation

- Attend the GIP orientation with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) Regional Office 7 and PESO, where they explain the rules and the dos and don'ts (about 1 hour).
- Receive your GIP uniform and ID (about 30 minutes).

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] GIP Application Form - PESO
- [ ] Birth Certificate - Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
- [ ] One proof of schooling - your school. Any of these:
  - Transcript of Records (TOR)
  - Form 137 or Form 138
  - Diploma or Certificate of Graduation
  - Certification from your school or institution, or other proof
- [ ] Certificate of Indigency - your barangay

### Eligibility

- You are 18 to 30 years old.
- You finished at least high school or senior high school, its equivalent under the Alternative Learning System, or a technical-vocational course.
- You have no work experience. Exceptions apply to people hit by disasters, affected by armed conflict, or displaced by government regulations, as determined by the DOLE Regional Office.

A component of KABATAAN 2000 under Executive Order (EO) no. 139 s. 1993, and DOLE Administrative Order No. 260-15, which aims to provide opportunities and engage young workers to serve general public in government agencies/entities projects and programs at the national and local level.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I already have work experience. Can I still apply?**
A: Generally no. The program is for people with no work experience, except those hit by disasters, affected by armed conflict, or displaced by government regulations, as determined by the DOLE Regional Office.

**Q: Do I need a college degree?**
A: No. You need to have finished at least high school or senior high school, its Alternative Learning System equivalent, or a technical-vocational course.

**Q: Do I get a uniform?**
A: Yes. PESO gives out GIP uniforms and IDs at the orientation.
