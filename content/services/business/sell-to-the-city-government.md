# Sell to the City Government — Lapu-Lapu City

This page is for suppliers, contractors and creditors doing business with the Lapu-Lapu City government. It covers buying bidding documents and claiming purchase orders at the City General Services Office (CGSO), and getting your claims processed and certifications issued at the City Accountant's Office.

## How to Apply

### Get Bidding Documents (CGSO)

#### Step 1: Request an Order of Payment

- Ask the BAC Secretariat at the CGSO for an order of payment for the bidding documents (about 2 minutes).

#### Step 2: Pay the Fee

- Pay at the cashier of the City Treasurer's Office. The fee depends on the Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC) of the project you are bidding on (about 5 minutes).

#### Step 3: Show Your Receipt

- Present your receipt to the BAC Secretariat (about 2 minutes).

#### Step 4: Receive Your Bidding Documents

- Once staff confirm your documents are complete, they print and release the bidding documents (about 5 minutes).

### Claim Your Purchase Order (CGSO)

#### Step 1: Show Your Company ID or Authorization Letter

- Present it to the staff in charge of releasing purchase orders (about 5 minutes).

#### Step 2: Submit the Requirements

- Submit any other required documents, such as a performance bond if it applies to your contract (about 5 minutes).

#### Step 3: Receive Your Purchase Order

- Staff release your purchase order (about 5 minutes).

### Get Paid: Disbursement Voucher Processing (City Accountant's Office)

#### Step 1: Submit Your Claim and Supporting Documents

- Submit your disbursement voucher and supporting documents. See Requirements below for the list that applies to your type of procurement.
- Staff log your documents, assign a voucher number and send them for pre-audit (about 10 minutes for supplier and contractor vouchers).

#### Step 2: Comply With Any Missing Requirements

- Staff review your documents for completeness and check that the amount claimed is correct (about 4 hours for supplier and contractor vouchers).
- If something is missing, staff contact you right away. Submit what is needed.

#### Step 3: Approval and Forwarding

- The City Accountant or an authorized signatory signs the pre-audited documents (about 5 minutes).
- Your documents are logged and forwarded to the City Treasurer's Office for payment (about 5 minutes).

### Get a Certification (City Accountant's Office)

#### Step 1: Submit Your Request

- **BIR tax credit forms (2306/2307) and business tax:** ask for the forms you need.
- **Availability of funds:** submit a letter request.
- **City clearance:** submit a filled-out city clearance form.
- **Net take-home pay, premium payments or loan repayments:** submit your Official Receipt from the City Treasurer's Office.

#### Step 2: Wait While It Is Prepared and Signed

- Staff prepare your certification or forms, and the City Accountant or an authorized signatory signs them.

#### Step 3: Claim It

- Sign the logbook and claim your certification (about 5 minutes).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Bidding Documents

- [ ] Order of payment - BAC Secretariat, CGSO

### Documents Needed: Purchase Order

- [ ] Company ID or authorization letter
- [ ] Performance bond (if applicable)

### Documents Needed: Payment Processing for Goods or Services Bought Through Alternative Modes

- [ ] Approved Obligation Request (OBR)
- [ ] Statement of VAT, Expanded Withholding Tax (EWT) and business tax
- [ ] Purchase request
- [ ] Purchase order
- [ ] Project Procurement Management Plan (PPMP) for the year, and any supplement
- [ ] BAC resolution or minutes
- [ ] Filled-out Request for Quotation (RFQ) forms from at least 3 suppliers or bidders
- [ ] Abstract of canvass or bids as quoted
- [ ] Business permit
- [ ] Delivery receipt or sales invoice
- [ ] Inspection and acceptance report

### Documents Needed: Payment Processing for Competitive Bidding

You need the first three documents above (approved OBR; statement of VAT, EWT and business tax; purchase request), plus the Annual Procurement Plan, PPMP, BAC minutes on the mode of procurement, invitation to bid, Philippine Government Electronic Procurement System (PhilGEPS) registration, newspaper publication, BAC minutes on opening and post-qualification, bid form, eligibility and financial envelope checklists, abstracts of bids as read and as calculated, and the post-qualification evaluation report. See the full list in the Official Citizen's Charter section below.

### Documents Needed: Payment Processing for Infrastructure Projects

Infrastructure contractors submit a longer list of 56 documents, including the statement of work accomplished, certificate of project completion, pictures, contractor's affidavit, surety bond, contract, notice of award, notice to proceed and performance bond. See the full list in the Official Citizen's Charter section below.

### Documents Needed: Certifications

- [ ] Availability of funds: letter request
- [ ] Net take-home pay, premium payments or loan repayments: Official Receipt from the City Treasurer's Office (₱50 fee)
- [ ] City clearance: filled-out city clearance form
- [ ] BIR tax credit forms and business tax: no documents needed

### Eligibility

- Bidding documents: interested bidders and suppliers
- Purchase orders: winning bidders and suppliers
- Payment processing: creditors, suppliers and contractors; national agencies; government financial institutions (GFIs) and government-owned or -controlled corporations (GOCCs); private financial institutions; city officials and employees; and anyone with a business interest with the city government
- Certifications: creditors, suppliers and contractors; city officials and employees; and anyone with a business interest with the city government

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much do bidding documents cost?**
A: The fee depends on the Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC) of the project. The BAC Secretariat gives you an order of payment showing the amount.

**Q: What do I bring to claim a purchase order?**
A: Your company ID or an authorization letter, and a performance bond if your contract requires one.

**Q: How long does it take the City Accountant's Office to process my payment?**
A: For supplier and contractor vouchers, the office's steps take about 4 hours 20 minutes if your documents are complete. Your documents then go to the City Treasurer's Office for payment.

**Q: What happens if my documents are incomplete?**
A: The staff member reviewing your claim contacts you right away so you can submit what is missing.

**Q: Can I get my BIR Form 2307 from the city?**
A: Yes. Request it at the City Accountant's Office. No documents are needed, and it takes about 40 minutes.
