# Close or Retire Your Business — Lapu-Lapu City

If you are closing a business registered in Lapu-Lapu City, you need a Certificate of Closure from the City Treasurer's Office. This is for proprietors, corporations, partnerships, cooperatives and associations that want to retire their business and stop city business taxes.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Treasurer's Office                                                                                                                        |

| **Requirements**    | Letter to the City Treasurer, latest Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) returns or affidavit of no operation, location sketch, proof of payments |
| **Cost**            | ₱50 certification fee plus ₱30 documentary stamp, plus any business tax you still owe                                                          |
| **Processing Time** | About 3 days and 18 minutes (most of it is the ocular inspection)                                                                              |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit Your Requirements

- Bring your letter and documents to the City Treasurer's Office.
- Staff check your documents and send them to the business assessment section.
- The assessment section checks your latest payment and bills you for any remaining business tax (about 10 minutes).

### Step 2: Pay What You Owe

- Pay any remaining business tax and the certification fee at the cashier.
- You receive an Official Receipt (OR) (about 5 minutes).

### Step 3: Wait for the Inspection

- City Treasurer's Office staff visit your business location to confirm it has closed (about 3 days).

### Step 4: Claim Your Certificate of Closure

- Return to the office to claim your printed Certificate of Closure (about 3 minutes).

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Letter addressed to the City Treasurer saying that your business is closing - prepare it yourself
- [ ] Latest Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) returns, or any other basis for computing the tax you still owe, or an Affidavit of No Operation/No Income
- [ ] Proof of payment of any remaining business tax due
- [ ] Board Resolution or Secretary's Certificate approving the closure (for corporations, partnerships and cooperatives)
- [ ] Sketch of your business location - prepare it yourself
- [ ] Official Receipt for the certification fee - City Treasurer's Office cashier

### Eligibility

- Proprietors
- Corporations or partnerships
- Cooperatives or associations

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My business earned nothing this year. What do I submit instead of BIR returns?**
A: You can submit an Affidavit of No Operation/No Income.

**Q: Do I still have to pay business tax when I close?**
A: Yes, if you still owe any. The office checks your latest payment and bills you for the remaining tax before you can get your Certificate of Closure.

**Q: Why does it take 3 days?**
A: Staff inspect your business location to confirm that it has closed. The inspection takes about 3 days.
