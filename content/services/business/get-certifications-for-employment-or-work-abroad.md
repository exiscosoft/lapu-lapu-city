# Get Certifications for Employment or Work Abroad — Lapu-Lapu City

The city issues several certifications that residents often need for jobs, school, visas and work abroad: a Certificate of Unemployment, a Certification of Good Moral, a Certification for Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA) Apostille, and a Mayor's Clearance for applicants to the uniformed services.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Mayor's Office (Unemployment, Good Moral, DFA Apostille); Office of the City Administrator (Mayor's Clearance) |
| **When**            | Apply at least 3 working days before you need a City Mayor's Office certification                                   |
| **Requirements**    | A request letter or clearances, a valid ID or passport copy, and a barangay document                                |
| **Cost**            | No fee listed for the City Mayor's Office certifications; ₱80 for the Mayor's Clearance                             |
| **Processing Time** | 3 days for City Mayor's Office certifications; 1 hour for the Mayor's Clearance                                     |

### Which Certification Do You Need?

| Certification                                     | Who it is for                                                                               | Fee         | Processing time |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | --------------- |
| Certificate of Unemployment                       | Parents and residents of Lapu-Lapu City (often needed by schools)                           | None listed | 3 days          |
| Certification for DFA Apostille                   | Residents processing a visa (work, tourist and others) and Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) | None listed | 3 days          |
| Certification of Good Moral                       | Residents processing a visa (work, tourist and others) and OFWs                             | None listed | 3 days          |
| Mayor's Clearance for AFP, BFP, PNP, BJMP and PCG | Residents of legal age applying to the uniformed services                                   | ₱80         | 1 hour          |

The uniformed services are the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP), Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), Philippine National Police (PNP), Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP) and Philippine Coast Guard (PCG).

---

## How to Apply

### Certificate of Unemployment, Good Moral or DFA Apostille (City Mayor's Office)

#### Step 1: Submit Your Request

- Bring your request letter addressed to the City Mayor and your supporting documents to the City Mayor's Office.
- Staff check your documents and receive your request (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 2: Wait for Your Certification

- Staff prepare your certification and have it signed. This takes about 2 days.
- Staff call or text you when it is ready.

#### Step 3: Claim Your Certification

- Claim your certification at the City Mayor's Office (about 30 minutes).

### Mayor's Clearance for the Uniformed Services (Office of the City Administrator)

#### Step 1: Submit Your Requirements

- Bring your clearances and ID picture to the Office of the City Administrator.
- Staff check that they are complete and tell you to pay (about 10 minutes).

#### Step 2: Pay the Fee

- Pay ₱80 at the cashier of the City Treasurer's Office and get your Official Receipt (OR).

#### Step 3: Submit Your Documents Again With the Receipt

- Return with all your documents and the OR. Staff process your clearance (about 15 minutes) and the City Administrator approves it (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 4: Claim Your Clearance

- Sign the logbook and claim your signed clearance (about 5 minutes).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Certificate of Unemployment

- [ ] Request letter addressed to the City Mayor - prepare it yourself. If a school requires the certificate, include the student's name, year level and school.
- [ ] Photocopy of a valid government-issued ID
- [ ] Certificate of Indigency - your barangay

### Documents Needed: Certification for DFA Apostille

- [ ] Request letter addressed to the City Mayor - prepare it yourself
- [ ] Photocopy of your Philippine passport
- [ ] The document to be apostilled (Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Residency, or Community Tax Certificate/cedula) - your barangay or the City Treasurer's Office

### Documents Needed: Certification of Good Moral

- [ ] Request letter addressed to the City Mayor - prepare it yourself
- [ ] Photocopy of a valid government-issued ID
- [ ] Certificate of Good Moral - your barangay

### Documents Needed: Mayor's Clearance

- [ ] Regional Trial Court (RTC) Clearance (original) - Lapu-Lapu City Hall of Justice
- [ ] Municipal Trial Court in Cities (MTCC) Clearance (original) - Lapu-Lapu City Hall of Justice
- [ ] Police Clearance - Lapu-Lapu City Police Office (LLCPO)
- [ ] 1 piece 2x2 ID picture

### Eligibility

- Certificate of Unemployment: parents and residents of Lapu-Lapu City
- DFA Apostille and Good Moral certifications: Lapu-Lapu City residents processing a visa, and OFWs
- Mayor's Clearance: Filipino citizens of legal age living in Lapu-Lapu City who are required by the AFP, BFP, PNP, BJMP or PCG to get the clearance

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My child's school asked for a Certificate of Unemployment. What should my letter say?**
A: Address it to the City Mayor and include the student's name, year level and school.

**Q: Which documents can the City Mayor's Office certify for DFA apostille?**
A: The charter lists the Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Residency, and Community Tax Certificate (cedula).

**Q: Is there a fee for the City Mayor's Office certifications?**
A: The charter lists no fee for the Certificate of Unemployment, Good Moral or DFA Apostille certifications. The Mayor's Clearance from the Office of the City Administrator costs ₱80.
