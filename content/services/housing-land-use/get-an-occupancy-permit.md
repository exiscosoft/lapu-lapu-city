# Get an Occupancy Permit — Lapu-Lapu City

Before you use or move into a new, renovated, or repaired building, you need a Certificate of Occupancy from the Office of the Building Official (OBO) at the City Engineering Office. This is for owners of residential and commercial buildings, developers, contractors, and government agencies.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Office of the Building Official (OBO), City Engineering Office                                                         |
| **When**            | Ask the office                                                                                                         |
| **Requirements**    | Your building permit and building plans, a Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC), and a certificate of completion  |
| **Cost**            | An occupancy fee and an inspection fee. The charter does not list the amounts; ask the office.                         |
| **Processing Time** | The charter does not give a total. Office steps take 3 to 30 minutes each, plus the wait for your inspection schedule. |

**Why you need it:** No building or structure may be used or occupied, and no change in its use may be made, until the Building Official issues a Certificate of Occupancy.

**Before you start:** You must already have a [building permit](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-building-permit).

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your requirements and request an inspection

- Submit your documents to the receiving staff. They set a schedule for your site inspection (about 5 minutes).

### Step 2: Pay the occupancy and inspection fees

- Pay the occupancy fee and the inspection fee, then show your official receipt.
- The office prepares your Certificate of Occupancy and the inspection order.

### Step 3: Wait for your inspection schedule

- The office tells you the inspection date by call or text message.

### Step 4: Be present during the inspection

- Inspectors (line and grade, architectural, sanitary/plumbing, electrical, and electronics and communication) check that the building follows your approved plans and specifications (about 30 minutes).
- They sign the Certificate of Final Inspection if it complies.

### Step 5: Submit the inspection report and fix any deficiencies

- Submit the occupancy inspection report. Staff tell you what needs to be corrected, if anything.
- Correct the deficiencies listed in the report.

### Step 6: Submit your official receipts

- Submit your official receipts of payment. Staff record them and prepare your occupancy permit, and the office prepares a transmittal to the City Assessor's Office.
- The Building Official reviews and signs your occupancy permit.

### Step 7: Claim your Certificate of Occupancy

- Sign for and receive your Certificate of Occupancy, together with the owner's copy of your plans and other documents (about 5 minutes).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Copy of the complete set of your building permit and building plans - you (the applicant or owner)
- [ ] Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) - Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)
- [ ] Filled-in Certificate of Completion - your supervising professionals

### Eligibility

- Developers
- Contractors
- Individual project proponents
- Government agencies

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How will I know when the inspection is?**
A: The office tells you the inspection schedule by call or text message.

**Q: What happens after I get my Certificate of Occupancy?**
A: The office prepares a transmittal to the City Assessor's Office. To declare your new building for real property tax, see [Get a tax declaration for your property](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-tax-declaration-for-your-property). It lists the building permit and Certificate of Occupancy as requirements.
