# Get an Electrical Permit Certificate for Urban Poor Families — Lapu-Lapu City

Urban poor residents who belong to an accredited urban poor association can get a free certificate for their electrical permit from the Urban Poor Affairs Office (UPAO).

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                           |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Urban Poor Affairs Office (UPAO)                                                                                  |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM                                                                               |
| **Requirements**    | Membership certification from your association president, barangay electrical permit, cedula, and tax declaration |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                                                              |
| **Processing Time** | Not stated in the charter; ask the office                                                                         |

---

## How to Apply

The charter does not list the steps for this service. In general:

### Step 1: Prepare your requirements

- Get a certification of membership from your association president, and your barangay electrical permit, cedula, and tax declaration.

### Step 2: Go to the UPAO

- Bring your requirements to the UPAO from Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- If you live in the Olango area, the charter names a separate Olango area coordinator for this service.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Certification of membership from your association president - your urban poor association
- [ ] Barangay electrical permit - your barangay
- [ ] Cedula (Community Tax Certificate)
- [ ] Tax declaration - [Office of the City Assessor](/services/housing-land-use/request-property-records-from-the-assessor)

### Eligibility

- Urban poor residents who are members of an accredited urban poor association. If your association is not accredited yet, see [Accredit an urban poor association](/services/housing-land-use/accredit-an-urban-poor-association).
