# Update or Cancel a Tax Declaration — Lapu-Lapu City

If your property has changed, the Office of the City Assessor can re-assess it, cancel its tax declaration (TD), or add an annotation such as a sale of an unsegregated portion or a mortgage. This page is for declared property owners and their authorized representatives.

---

## Which One Applies to You?

| Your situation                                                                                                                                                 | What to request                         | Charter section |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| You renovated or extended your building, part of it was damaged, or you want the property's classification or taxability reviewed                              | Re-assessment and revalidation          | C               |
| Your building was demolished or totally damaged, or the TD should be cancelled for another reason                                                              | Cancellation of the tax declaration     | D               |
| You sold, donated, or transferred a portion of a property that has not been segregated (split off) yet                                                         | Annotation of the conveyance on the TD  | K               |
| An adverse claim, real estate mortgage, lis pendens (notice of pending case), tax lien, levy, court order, or other encumbrance needs to be recorded on the TD | Annotation of the encumbrance on the TD | L               |

Need a new TD instead (transfer, new building, first-time declaration, or subdivision)? See [Get a tax declaration for your property](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-tax-declaration-for-your-property).

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your request and documents

- Bring your request and documents to the receiving counter of the Office of the City Assessor (about 30 minutes per property or per entry).
- For re-assessment (C) and cancellation (D), your request must be notarized.
- Keep your stamped "Received" copy of the request.

### Step 2: Pay the annotation fee (K and L only)

- Pay ₱20 per entry at the City Treasurer's Office and submit your official receipt.
- For L, staff first check whether your documents can be annotated and tell you the exact amount to pay.

### Step 3: Allow the site inspection (C and D only)

- Assessor staff visit your property. Let them in to take measurements, pictures, and videos (about 2 hours).

### Step 4: Claim your result

- **C**: Claim your new tax declaration and Notice of Assessment (NOA).
- **D**: The TD is cancelled. If you need proof, request a certified photocopy of the cancelled TD.
- **K**: Claim your new TD with the annotation after 2 to 3 days.
- **L**: The annotation is written on the original TD. If you need a copy, request a certified true machine copy of the TD with the annotation. See [Request property records from the Assessor](/services/housing-land-use/request-property-records-from-the-assessor).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Everyone

- [ ] Letter request with your contact details (notarized for C and D) - you
- [ ] Original and photocopy of a valid ID of the owner or requesting party and of the authorized person, if any - you
- [ ] If you are not the owner, declarant, or interested party: Special Power of Attorney (SPA) or letter of authorization or consent - you
- [ ] For companies and other juridical entities: board resolution or secretary's certificate, and the latest General Information Sheet (GIS) - you

### C. Re-assessment

- [ ] Current real property tax clearance - [City Treasurer's Office](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance-or-certification)
- [ ] Pictures of the property showing the renovated, added, or damaged part - you
- [ ] Supporting documents, if applicable (for example, a building permit for a renovation or extension, proof of exemption, approved plans, bill of materials, certifications, affidavits) - you or the Office of the Building Official

### D. Cancellation

- [ ] Current real property tax clearance - [City Treasurer's Office](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance-or-certification)
- [ ] Street-view pictures of the demolished or totally damaged property, if applicable - you
- [ ] Supporting documents, if applicable (for example, certifications, demolition permits, affidavits) - you
- [ ] Ocular inspection report - prepared by the Assessor's office

### K. Annotation of a Conveyance

- [ ] Certified true copy of the certificate of title showing the annotation of the transaction - Registry of Deeds (RoD)
- [ ] Deed of conveyance (for example, extrajudicial settlement of heirs, affidavit of adjudication, sale, donation, barter, exchange), certified true copy - Registry of Deeds
- [ ] Certificate Authorizing Registration (CAR), original or certified copy from the RoD - Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)
- [ ] Current real property tax clearance - City Treasurer's Office
- [ ] Photocopy of the official receipt for the transfer tax - City Treasurer's Office
- [ ] Official receipt for the annotation fee - City Treasurer's Office
- [ ] Street-view pictures of the buildings and other improvements on the property - you

### L. Annotation of an Encumbrance

- [ ] Certified true copy of the title showing the annotation, if applicable - Registry of Deeds
- [ ] Certified true copy of the document to be annotated (for example, affidavit of adverse claim, deed of real estate mortgage, court certification of a pending case for lis pendens, order of tax lien, levy, contract, court order) - Registry of Deeds
- [ ] Official receipt for the annotation fee - City Treasurer's Office

### Eligibility

- Declared owners of real property in Lapu-Lapu City and their authorized representatives

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does an annotation cost?**
A: ₱20 per entry or inscription, paid at the City Treasurer's Office.

**Q: My house was demolished. What should I do?**
A: Ask the Assessor to cancel its tax declaration. Bring a notarized request, a current real property tax clearance, and pictures or documents showing the demolition or damage. Staff inspect the site before cancelling the TD.
