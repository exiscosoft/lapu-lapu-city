# Pay Real Property Tax — Lapu-Lapu City

If you own land, a building, or machinery in Lapu-Lapu City, you pay real property tax (RPT) to the Office of the City Treasurer. Anyone with a legal interest in the property can pay.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Where**           | Real Property Tax Division, Office of the City Treasurer                 |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, except holidays                     |
| **Requirements**    | Copy of your latest tax declaration and your latest RPT official receipt |
| **Cost**            | Your tax as computed under the real property tax schedule                |
| **Processing Time** | About 12 minutes, or about 17 minutes if you have the payment posted     |

### Quarterly Due Dates

| Quarter     | Due date     |
| ----------- | ------------ |
| 1st quarter | March 31     |
| 2nd quarter | June 30      |
| 3rd quarter | September 30 |
| 4th quarter | December 31  |

If a due date falls on a weekend or holiday, the office opens that day to accept payments only.

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Go to the Real Property Tax Division

- Inside the Office of the City Treasurer, approach the Real Property Tax Division staff (about 2 minutes).

### Step 2: Get your bill

- Show your latest tax declaration and latest RPT official receipt. Staff compute what you owe and print your bill (about 5 minutes).

### Step 3: Pay

- Present the bill to the RPT collector and pay. Keep your official receipt (about 5 minutes).

### Step 4: Have the payment posted (optional)

- You can take your official receipt to the mezzanine floor to have the payment posted in the real property tax register (about 5 minutes).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if the due date falls on a weekend or holiday?**
A: The office opens on that day to accept payments only.

**Q: Can I pay for a property that isn't in my name?**
A: Yes, if you have a legal interest in the property. Bring the latest tax declaration and the latest RPT official receipt.

**Q: How much is my tax?**
A: The RPT staff compute your dues from the real property tax schedule when you present your tax declaration and last receipt.
