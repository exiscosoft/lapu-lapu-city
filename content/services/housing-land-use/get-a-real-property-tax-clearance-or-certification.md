# Get a Real Property Tax Clearance or Certification — Lapu-Lapu City

A real property tax clearance from the Office of the City Treasurer shows that the taxes on a property are paid. The office also issues other certifications on request.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Real property tax clearance                                                                        | Other certifications                                                                         |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Who can apply**   | Property owners and anyone with a legal interest in the property                                   | The general public, companies, and organizations                                             |
| **Where**           | Office of the City Treasurer                                                                       | License Division, Office of the City Treasurer                                               |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, except holidays                                               | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, except holidays                                         |
| **Requirements**    | Official receipt for the clearance fee and documentary stamp, and your latest RPT official receipt | Letter request to the City Treasurer, and official receipt for the fee and documentary stamp |
| **Cost**            | ₱20 per property, plus ₱30 documentary stamp per page                                              | ₱50 (for business establishments) or ₱20 per page, plus ₱30 documentary stamp per page       |
| **Processing Time** | About 21 minutes                                                                                   | About 29 minutes                                                                             |

You often need a current real property tax clearance to [get a tax declaration](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-tax-declaration-for-your-property), a [locational clearance](/services/housing-land-use/request-zoning-land-use-or-building-permits), or a [subdivision permit](/services/housing-land-use/apply-for-a-subdivision-development-permit).

## How to Apply

### Real Property Tax Clearance

#### Step 1: Submit your requirements

- Submit your requirements and tell staff what the clearance is for.
- Staff prepare and print your clearance and forward it to the City Treasurer or a deputy for signature (about 18 minutes).

#### Step 2: Claim your clearance

- Claim the signed tax clearance (about 3 minutes).

### Other Certifications

#### Step 1: Submit your letter request

- Submit your letter request and official receipt to the License Division.
- Staff prepare the certification or document you asked for, and the City Treasurer or a deputy signs it (about 28 minutes).

#### Step 2: Claim your certification

- Claim and sign for your certification (about 1 minute).

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Real Property Tax Clearance

- [ ] Official receipt for the tax clearance fee with documentary stamp
- [ ] Copy of the latest official receipt of real property tax payment for the tax declaration, showing the owner and location of the property - from your last [real property tax payment](/services/housing-land-use/pay-real-property-tax)

### Documents Needed: Other Certifications

- [ ] Letter request addressed to the City Treasurer - you
- [ ] Official receipt for the fee with documentary stamp

### Eligibility

- **Real property tax clearance**: real property owners and anyone with a legal interest in the property
- **Other certifications**: the general public, and public or private companies and organizations

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does a real property tax clearance cost?**
A: ₱20 per property, plus a ₱30 documentary stamp per page.

**Q: How long does it take?**
A: About 21 minutes for a tax clearance and about 29 minutes for other certifications, based on the charter's step times.
