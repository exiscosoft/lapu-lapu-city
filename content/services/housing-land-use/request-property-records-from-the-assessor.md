# Request Property Records from the Assessor — Lapu-Lapu City

The Office of the City Assessor keeps the records of all declared land, buildings, and machinery in the city. Property owners and their authorized representatives can request certified copies of tax declarations, certificates of land holdings, property history, certificates of no improvement, and lot or vicinity maps.

---

## Which One Applies to You?

| You need...                                              | What to request                                           | Charter section |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| A certified copy of your current tax declaration (TD)    | Certified true machine copy of the latest TD              | G               |
| Copies of old TDs or the documents behind them           | Certified copy of old TD and supporting documents         | H               |
| Proof of what property you own, or that you own none     | Certificate of land holdings or no land holding           | I               |
| The history of a property (past owners and transactions) | Real property verification, tracer, or trace-back history | J               |
| Proof that a lot has no buildings, or how many it has    | Certificate of no improvement or number of improvements   | M               |
| A lot plan or vicinity map of a property                 | Reproduction of vicinity map or lot location map          | N               |

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Fill in the request form

- Fill in the request form at the Office of the City Assessor and present your documents.
- For G, H, I, and J, staff check your documents and tell you the exact amount to pay. For H, staff first retrieve the old records to count the pages.
- For M and N, bring the official receipt for the fee when you submit your form.

### Step 2: Pay at the City Treasurer's Office

- Pay the fee at the City Treasurer's Office.
- Submit the official receipt with your form and documents to the Assessor's receiving section.

### Step 3: Allow the site inspection (M only)

- For a certificate of no improvement, staff check maps and online records, then may inspect the property. Let them in to take measurements, pictures, and videos (about 2 hours).
- If the inspection finds a building or improvement that was never declared, it must be declared for tax. The charter lists 2 days for the new tax declaration.

### Step 4: Claim your record

- Claim your certified copy, certificate, history summary, or map at the releasing counter.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Everyone

- [ ] Filled-in request form - Office of the City Assessor
- [ ] Original and photocopy of a valid ID of the property owner and of the authorized person, if any - you
- [ ] If you are not the owner or declarant: Special Power of Attorney (SPA) or letter of authorization or consent from the owner - you
- [ ] For companies and other juridical entities: board resolution or secretary's certificate naming the authorized representative - you
- [ ] If the owner has died or the property is part of an estate: proof that you are related to the owner or are an heir - you
- [ ] Official receipt for the fee - City Treasurer's Office

### Extra Documents for Some Records

- [ ] **I, J, M**: Current real property tax clearance - [City Treasurer's Office](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance-or-certification)
- [ ] **J, M**: Street-view pictures of the property showing the improvements - you

### Eligibility

- Declared owners of real property in Lapu-Lapu City and their authorized representatives

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I get a copy of a tax declaration for a property owned by a relative who has died?**
A: Yes, if you bring proof that you are related to the owner or are an heir, along with the other requirements.

**Q: What happens if the inspection for a certificate of no improvement finds a building?**
A: If the building was never declared, it is sent for declaration and you get a certification stating the number of improvements on the property instead.
