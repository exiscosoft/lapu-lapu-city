# Get a Tax Declaration for Your Property — Lapu-Lapu City

A tax declaration (TD) is the City Assessor's record of your land, building, or machinery for real property tax. Get one from the Office of the City Assessor when you buy or inherit property, finish a new building, install machinery, declare land for the first time, or split a lot.

---

## Which One Applies to You?

| Your situation                                                                                                                           | What to request                                   | Charter section |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| You bought, inherited, or received property (sale, donation, settlement of an estate, exchange, etc.) and need the TD transferred to you | Tax declaration for transfer of property          | A               |
| You finished a new house, building, structure, or other improvement                                                                      | Tax declaration for a new building or improvement | B.1             |
| You installed machinery or equipment                                                                                                     | Tax declaration for machinery and equipment       | B.2             |
| Your land has never been declared and has no title                                                                                       | First-time declaration of untitled land           | E.1             |
| Your land is titled but has never been declared                                                                                          | First-time declaration of titled land             | E.2             |
| You partitioned or subdivided a lot                                                                                                      | New tax declarations for partition or subdivision | F               |

Need to change, annotate, or cancel an existing TD instead? See [Update or cancel a tax declaration](/services/housing-land-use/update-or-cancel-a-tax-declaration). Need a copy of your TD? See [Request property records from the Assessor](/services/housing-land-use/request-property-records-from-the-assessor).

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your request and documents

- Bring your letter request and documents to the receiving counter of the Office of the City Assessor (about 30 minutes per property).
- For a new building (B.1), your request must be notarized or a sworn declaration.
- Keep your stamped "Received" copy of the request.

### Step 2: Wait for the evaluation

- Staff check that your documents are complete and send your request to the right division.

### Step 3: Allow the site inspection (B.1, B.2, E.1, E.2, F)

- Assessor staff visit your property. Let them in to take measurements, pictures, and videos (about 2 to 3 hours).
- They prepare a Field Appraisal and Assessment Sheet (FAAS), which is then reviewed and approved.

### Step 4: Pay 10 years of back taxes (E.1, E.2, F)

- When your TD is approved, the office asks you to pay 10 years of back real property taxes.
- Pay at the City Treasurer's Office and submit your official receipt to the Assessor's receiving section. See [Pay real property tax](/services/housing-land-use/pay-real-property-tax).

### Step 5: Claim your tax declaration

- Claim your tax declaration and Notice of Assessment (NOA).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Everyone

- [ ] Letter request with your contact details (notarized request or sworn declaration for B.1) - you
- [ ] Original and photocopy of a valid ID of the property owner and of the authorized person, if any - you
- [ ] If you are not the owner or declarant: Special Power of Attorney (SPA) or letter of authorization or consent from the owner - you
- [ ] For companies and other juridical entities: board resolution or secretary's certificate, and the latest General Information Sheet (GIS) - you

### A. Transfer of Property

- [ ] Deed of conveyance (for example, extrajudicial settlement of heirs, affidavit of adjudication, sale, donation, barter, exchange), certified true copy - Registry of Deeds (RoD)
- [ ] Certificate Authorizing Registration (CAR), original or certified true copy from the RoD - Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)
- [ ] Current real property tax clearance - [City Treasurer's Office](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance-or-certification)
- [ ] Photocopy of the official receipt for the transfer tax - City Treasurer's Office
- [ ] Certified true copy of the certificate of title - Registry of Deeds
- [ ] Approved plan (subdivision, segregation, consolidation, etc.), if applicable - RoD or the Bureau of Lands of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR)
- [ ] Court order with certificate of finality, if applicable - trial courts
- [ ] Street-view pictures of the house, buildings, and other improvements - you

### B.1 New Building or Improvement

- [ ] Approved building permit and Certificate of Occupancy - Office of the Building Official ([building permit](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-building-permit), [occupancy permit](/services/housing-land-use/get-an-occupancy-permit))
- [ ] If there is no building permit: a certification from the Office of the Building Official that the structure does not need one
- [ ] Building plans (floor area plan, site development plan, etc.) - you
- [ ] Bill of materials - you
- [ ] Photocopy of the tax declaration of the land - you
- [ ] If you don't own the land: notarized consent, or the original deed of conveyance or contract of lease - you
- [ ] Street-view pictures of the property - you

### B.2 Machinery and Equipment

- [ ] Affidavit of ownership or sworn statement of the market value - you
- [ ] Tax declaration of the land, building, or structure where the machinery is installed - you
- [ ] Purchase receipts or sales invoices (original, to be certified by the office) - you
- [ ] Colored pictures of the machinery or equipment - you

### E.1 Untitled Land Declared for the First Time

- [ ] Certified true copy of the patent, homestead, Miscellaneous Sales Application (MSA), or similar - you or DENR
- [ ] Survey plan with technical description, prepared by a licensed geodetic engineer and approved by the Land Management Bureau (LMB) of the DENR
- [ ] Certification from the Community Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) that the land is within the alienable and disposable area
- [ ] Sworn statement of the market value of the property - you
- [ ] Affidavit of ownership stating your long, continuous, and open possession of the property - you
- [ ] Certification from the Punong Barangay that you are the present possessor and occupant
- [ ] Certification from the Punong Barangay and the City Mayor that the barangay and city have no objection to your declaration of ownership
- [ ] Affidavit of the adjoining landowners that you are the present possessor and occupant, sworn to before the Punong Barangay or the City Mayor
- [ ] Copy of the Index of Decrees, if applicable - Bureau of Lands
- [ ] Court order or decision, if applicable - local courts
- [ ] Ocular inspection and investigation report, and the FAAS - prepared by the Assessor's office
- [ ] Proof of payment of 10 years of back real property taxes - City Treasurer's Office

### E.2 Titled Land Declared for the First Time

- [ ] Certified true copy of the patent, homestead, Miscellaneous Sales Application (MSA), or similar - you
- [ ] Survey plan with technical description, prepared by a licensed geodetic engineer and approved by the LMB of the DENR
- [ ] Ocular inspection and investigation report, and the FAAS - prepared by the Assessor's office
- [ ] Proof of payment of 10 years of back real property taxes - you

### F. Partition or Subdivision of a Lot

- [ ] Partition survey plan with technical description, prepared by a licensed geodetic engineer and approved by the LMB of the DENR
- [ ] Certified true copy from the RoD of the registered deed of partition, extrajudicial partition, or other document subdividing the lot - you
- [ ] Court order or decision for a judicial partition, if applicable - local courts
- [ ] Certificate of tax clearance for the current year - [City Treasurer's Office](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance-or-certification)

### Eligibility

- Declared owners of real property in Lapu-Lapu City and their authorized representatives

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a fee for a new tax declaration?**
A: The charter lists no fee at the Assessor's office for these services. You may still pay other charges at the City Treasurer's Office, such as the transfer tax for a transfer, or 10 years of back taxes for land declared for the first time or a partitioned lot.

**Q: Why does the Assessor need to inspect my property?**
A: For new buildings, machinery, first-time land declarations, and partitions, staff measure and photograph the property to prepare its appraisal (the FAAS). Let them into the premises during the inspection.

**Q: Can someone else file for me?**
A: Yes. Your representative needs a Special Power of Attorney or a letter of authorization or consent from you, plus original and photocopied valid IDs for both of you.
