# Apply for a Subdivision Development Permit — Lapu-Lapu City

If you plan to subdivide a lot or develop a subdivision, you need approval from the City Planning and Development Office (CPDO) and the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Council). This page covers subdivision approval for lot owners and corporations, the locational clearance and development permit for land developers, and the full fee schedule.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Subdivision approval                                                                                                                     | Complex subdivision (land developers)                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Who can apply**   | Residents, lot owners (or anyone with the lot owner's consent), lessees, and corporations                                                | Land developers                                                                                                                                                    |
| **What you get**    | Subdivision approval                                                                                                                     | Preliminary Approval and Locational Clearance (PALC) and Development Permit (DP)                                                                                   |
| **Requirements**    | Notarized application form, subdivision scheme plan, land title, tax declaration, and realty tax clearance                               | Application form, subdivision scheme plan, roadway, drainage, and electrical plans, proof of ownership, land title, barangay endorsement, and realty tax clearance |
| **Cost**            | Processing and filing fees under the Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board (HLURB) 2013 Schedule of Fees                                 | PALC and DP fees under the HLURB 2013 Schedule of Fees                                                                                                             |
| **Processing Time** | About 23 working days plus about 6 hours: 20 days for City Council action, 3 days for the City Mayor's signature, and shorter CPDO steps | About 16 working days plus about 3.5 hours, plus City Council approval (the charter gives no time for it)                                                          |

- **Where**: City Planning and Development Office, Quezon National Highway, Pajo, Lapu-Lapu City
- **When**: Ask the office

## How to Apply

### Subdivision Approval

#### Step 1: Get the application form

- Ask CPDO staff for the application form and the list of requirements (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 2: Submit your application

- Submit the filled-in, notarized application form with your documents. Staff evaluate it (about 40 minutes) and bill the processing and filing fees (about 20 minutes).

#### Step 3: Pay the fees

- Pay at the cashier and keep your official receipt (about 1 hour).

#### Step 4: Wait for approval

- The CPDO prepares and reviews your subdivision approval. The City Planning and Development Coordinator and the City Mayor sign the recommendation (the Mayor's signature takes about 3 days).
- The City Council acts on it and passes a resolution (about 20 days).
- The City Planning and Development Coordinator signs the approval.

#### Step 5: Claim your subdivision approval

- Claim your approval at the CPDO (about 40 minutes).
- Next, get new tax declarations for the new lots. See [Get a tax declaration for your property](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-tax-declaration-for-your-property).

### Complex Subdivision: Locational Clearance and Development Permit

#### Step 1: Get the application form

- Ask CPDO staff for the application form and the list of requirements (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 2: Submit your application

- Submit the filled-in application form with your documents. The CPDO evaluates it (about 5 days) and endorses it to the City Mayor (about 1 day).

#### Step 3: Wait for City Council approval

- The City Mayor, through the Local Zoning Board, endorses the application to the City Council (about 5 days). The council then acts on it.

#### Step 4: Pay the fees

- After approval, the CPDO bills you for the PALC and DP fees (about 1 hour).
- Pay at the cashier and keep your official receipt (about 1 hour).

#### Step 5: Wait for signing

- The CPDO prepares your PALC and DP (about 1 day). The City Planning and Development Coordinator signs it (about 1 day), then the City Mayor (about 3 days).

#### Step 6: Claim your PALC and DP

- Claim your approved PALC and DP at the CPDO (about 1 hour).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Subdivision Approval

- [ ] Filled-in and notarized application form - CPDO
- [ ] 6 blueprints or whiteprints of the subdivision scheme plan (standard size)
- [ ] 1 certified true copy and 2 photocopies of your land title (Transfer Certificate of Title or Original Certificate of Title, TCT/OCT) - Registry of Deeds
- [ ] 1 certified true copy and 2 photocopies of the tax declaration - [City Assessor](/services/housing-land-use/request-property-records-from-the-assessor)
- [ ] If you are not the registered owner: 3 photocopies of documents proving your right, claim, or authority
- [ ] 3 photocopies of your current realty tax clearance - [City Treasurer's Office](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance-or-certification)
- [ ] 1 long brown envelope
- [ ] Billing slip

### Documents Needed: Complex Subdivision

- [ ] Filled-in application form - CPDO
- [ ] Subdivision scheme plan (standard size)
- [ ] Roadway layout
- [ ] Drainage plan
- [ ] Electrical plan
- [ ] Proof of lot ownership
- [ ] Certified true copy of the certificate(s) of title - Registry of Deeds
- [ ] Sangguniang Barangay (barangay council) endorsement - your barangay
- [ ] Current realty tax clearance - [City Treasurer's Office](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance-or-certification)

### Eligibility

- **Subdivision approval**: individual residents, lot owners, people with the lot owner's consent, lessees, and corporations
- **Complex subdivision**: land developers

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why does subdivision approval take so long?**
A: The City Council must act on it and pass a resolution, which the charter lists at about 20 days. The City Mayor's signature takes about 3 days.

**Q: Which fees apply to my project?**
A: It depends on the project type (for example, P.D. 957 subdivision, socialized or economic housing under B.P. 220, industrial/commercial, or farmlot) and its land area. The CPDO bills you after evaluating your application.
