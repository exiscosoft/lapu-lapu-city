# Request Zoning, Land Use, or Building Permits — Lapu-Lapu City

Planning to build, renovate, or find out how a lot is zoned? Start at the City Planning and Development Office (CPDO). It issues the locational clearance you need before you can get a building permit, and the zoning/land use certification that states how a lot is zoned.

---

## Which Permit Do You Need?

Building permits in Lapu-Lapu City come in this order:

1. **Locational clearance** from the CPDO. It confirms that your project fits the zoning of your lot. The building permit checklist lists it as the first requirement. (Explained on this page.)
2. **Building permit** from the Office of the Building Official at the City Engineering Office. You need it before you build, renovate, repair, or demolish a structure. See [Get a building permit](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-building-permit).
3. **Occupancy permit** (Certificate of Occupancy) from the same office. You need it before you use or move into the finished building. See [Get an occupancy permit](/services/housing-land-use/get-an-occupancy-permit).

## How to Apply

### Locational Clearance

#### Step 1: Get the application form

- Ask CPDO staff for the application form and the list of requirements (about 20 minutes).

#### Step 2: Submit your application

- Submit the filled-in, notarized application form with your documents. The steps are the same for residential, commercial, apartment, and institutional projects.
- Staff evaluate your application and bill the filing fee (about 40 minutes).

#### Step 3: Get an inspection schedule

- Submit your application for an inspection schedule (about 20 minutes).

#### Step 4: Be at the site for the inspection

- CPDO inspectors visit your lot (about half a day).
- If you can't be there, send an authorized representative with a Special Power of Attorney (SPA).

#### Step 5: Resubmit for re-evaluation and billing

- After the inspection, resubmit your application. Staff re-evaluate it and issue the bill (about 30 minutes).

#### Step 6: Pay the fees

- Pay at the cashier and keep your official receipt (about 1 hour).

#### Step 7: Wait for approval

- The CPDO prepares and encodes your clearance and inspection report. The department head approves and signs it, and the City Mayor signs it (about 2 days).

#### Step 8: Claim your locational clearance

- Claim your clearance at the CPDO (about 30 minutes). Then apply for your [building permit](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-building-permit).

### Zoning/Land Use Certification

#### Step 1: Submit your letter request

- Submit your letter request and documents. Staff evaluate it and work out the fee (about 80 minutes).

#### Step 2: Pay the fees

- Pay at the cashier and keep your official receipt (about 1 hour).

#### Step 3: Submit your official receipt

- The CPDO prepares the certification, reviews it, and the City Planning and Development Coordinator signs it (about 2 hours).

#### Step 4: Claim your certification

- Claim the certification at the CPDO (about 30 minutes).

## Requirements

### Documents Needed: Locational Clearance

- [ ] 1 certified true copy and 1 photocopy of your land title (Transfer Certificate of Title or Original Certificate of Title, TCT/OCT), or 1 certified true copy and 1 photocopy of the tax declaration - title from the Registry of Deeds; tax declaration from the [City Assessor](/services/housing-land-use/request-property-records-from-the-assessor)
- [ ] If you are not the registered owner: proof of your right, claim, or authority over the lot
- [ ] 2 photocopies of your current realty tax clearance - [City Treasurer's Office](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-real-property-tax-clearance-or-certification)
- [ ] 1 original and 1 photocopy of your barangay construction clearance - your barangay
- [ ] 2 photocopies of the lot plan
- [ ] 6 blueprints or whiteprints of the site plan, signed and sealed by a licensed architect and signed by the owner or representative
- [ ] 1 blueprint or whiteprint each of the plumbing plan with rainwater catchment detail, the floor plan, and the rainwater catchment detail of the plumbing plan (20 x 30 inches)
- [ ] 2 copies of the bill of materials
- [ ] For commercial, apartment, institutional, and special projects: 1 copy and 1 photocopy of any additional documents required - ask the CPDO
- [ ] Billing slip

### Documents Needed: Zoning/Land Use Certification

- [ ] Letter request
- [ ] 1 photocopy of the certified true copy of your land title (TCT/OCT) and 1 photocopy of the certified true copy of the tax declaration
- [ ] 1 photocopy of the vicinity map - the City Assessor can [reproduce a vicinity map](/services/housing-land-use/request-property-records-from-the-assessor)
- [ ] Billing slip

### Eligibility

- Individual residents, lot owners, people with the lot owner's consent, and lessees in Lapu-Lapu City
- Contractors
- Corporations

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a locational clearance before I apply for a building permit?**
A: Yes. The City Engineering Office's building permit checklist lists the locational clearance from the CPDO as its first requirement.

**Q: What if I can't attend the site inspection?**
A: An authorized representative with a Special Power of Attorney (SPA) may attend for you.

**Q: Why does the locational clearance take a few days?**
A: Most steps take under an hour, but the inspection takes about half a day and the City Mayor's signature takes about 2 days.
