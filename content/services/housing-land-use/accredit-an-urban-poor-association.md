# Accredit an Urban Poor Association — Lapu-Lapu City

Urban poor associations in Lapu-Lapu City can get accredited by the Urban Poor Affairs Office (UPAO). You apply through the area coordinator assigned to your barangay. Accreditation is free.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Urban Poor Affairs Office (UPAO), through the area coordinator for your barangay                                    |
| **When**            | Ask your area coordinator                                                                                           |
| **Requirements**    | List of officers and members, meeting minutes and schedule, history of the association, and copies of accreditation |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                                                                |
| **Processing Time** | Not stated in the charter; ask your area coordinator                                                                |

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Get the list of requirements

- Ask your area coordinator for the list of requirements.

### Step 2: Submit your requirements

- Submit all the requirements to your area coordinator, who checks that they are complete.

### Step 3: Receive your certification

- The head of the UPAO approves and signs the accreditation. Get your certification from your area coordinator.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] List of officers - your association
- [ ] List of legitimate members - your association
- [ ] Minutes of your monthly meetings, from the start of the association up to the present - your association
- [ ] Schedule of your monthly meetings - your association
- [ ] History of the association, stating the lot number and whether the lot owner is public or private - your association
- [ ] Copies of accreditation - your association
- [ ] Documents from the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), the Presidential Commission for the Urban Poor (PCUP), the Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board (HLURB), the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), and others to follow - ask your area coordinator what applies

### Eligibility

- Urban poor associations in Lapu-Lapu City

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does accreditation cost?**
A: Nothing. Accreditation is free of charge.

**Q: What can members of an accredited association apply for?**
A: The UPAO issues a certificate for an electrical permit to urban poor residents who are members of an accredited association. See [Get an electrical permit certificate for urban poor families](/services/housing-land-use/get-an-electrical-permit-certificate-for-urban-poor-families).
