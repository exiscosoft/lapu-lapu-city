# Turn Over Rescued Wildlife — Lapu-Lapu City

If you rescue a wild animal in Lapu-Lapu City, you can turn it over to the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO). The service is free, and you get a Wildlife Turn-over Receipt as the rescuer.

---

---

## How to Turn Over Wildlife

### Step 1: Bring the rescued animal to CENRO

- CENRO staff will help you with the animal.

### Step 2: Give details about the animal

- Staff ask you for the animal's length, weight, suspected species, and where you found it.
- They use this to prepare your Wildlife Turn-over Receipt.

### Step 3: Sign and get your receipt

- Sign the receipt as the wildlife rescuer.
- Staff give you the Wildlife Turn-over Receipt.

### Step 4: CENRO hands the animal over

- CENRO turns the animal over to the right agency: the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) or the Department of Environment and Natural Resources office in Cebu City (CENRO Cebu City).
- CENRO Cebu City issues a Wildlife Turnover Certificate to the city's CENRO.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I have to pay anything?**
A: No. Turning over rescued wildlife is free.

**Q: What happens to the animal?**
A: CENRO turns it over to the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) or to CENRO Cebu City of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources.
