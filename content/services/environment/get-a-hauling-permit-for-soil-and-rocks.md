# Get a Hauling Permit for Soil and Rocks — Lapu-Lapu City

Contractors and developers need a hauling permit to move common waste rocks and soil from a project site in Lapu-Lapu City. You apply at the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO), and the City Mayor's Office approves and issues the permit.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Who can apply**   | Contractors and developers                                                                               |
| **Where**           | City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)                                                    |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM                                                                     |
| **Requirements**    | Application form, permits and clearances for the project and the destination site, and a site inspection |

| **Processing Time** | Seven (7) days after you submit complete requirements                                                    |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Get interviewed and fill out the application form

- Tell CENRO staff you want to apply for a hauling permit. They will interview you briefly.
- Fill out the application form.

### Step 2: Submit your requirements

- Hand in the filled-out form with all the documents listed below.
- Staff check your documents.

### Step 3: Wait for the site inspection

- CENRO inspectors visit the site. The inspection takes about 2 hours.
- The inspectors then prepare an inspection report.

### Step 4: CENRO forwards your application to the City Mayor's Office

- If your requirements are complete, CENRO sends your application to the City Mayor's Office.

### Step 5: Pay the fee

- Staff at the City Mayor's Office tell you how much to pay.
- Pay at the City Treasurer's Office and hand in a copy of your official receipt (OR).

### Step 6: Get your permit

- The City Mayor's Office releases your approved Mayor's Permit.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Filled-out application form - from CENRO
- [ ] Site Development Permit
- [ ] Barangay Construction Clearance - from the barangay where the project is
- [ ] Zoning Certification for the destination site (commercial or residential) - see [zoning and land use certification](/services/housing-land-use/request-zoning-land-use-or-building-permits)
- [ ] Business Permit of all parties - see [business permit](/services/business/apply-for-a-business-permit-in-lapu-lapu-city)
- [ ] Affidavit of Undertaking from the hauler and from the destination site
- [ ] Approved Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) for the project
- [ ] Building Permit - see [building permit](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-building-permit)
- [ ] Certificate from the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) showing that the destination land is alienable or disposable
- [ ] Inspection report and photos of the inspection - CENRO inspectors do the site inspection and prepare the report (Step 3)

### Eligibility

- Contractors and developers

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much is the hauling permit fee?**
A: The charter does not list a fixed amount. The Office of the City Mayor sets the fee, and its staff tell you how much to pay once CENRO forwards your application.

**Q: Who issues the permit?**
A: CENRO receives and checks your application and inspects the site. The City Mayor's Office approves it and releases the Mayor's Permit.
