# Request an Environmental Seminar for Your Community — Lapu-Lapu City

Public or private groups in Lapu-Lapu City can ask the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) to give a free environmental seminar or talk. CENRO calls these information, education, and communication (IEC) activities, or Dalaw Turo.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Who can request** | Public or private groups                                                 |
| **Where**           | City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)                    |
| **When**            | Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM                                   |
| **Requirements**    | Letter of request with the proposed date, venue, participants, and topic |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                     |
| **Processing Time** | Seven (7) days after you submit your request                             |

---

## Topics You Can Request

CENRO assigns speakers by topic:

- Solid waste
- Coastal and marine
- Air and water
- Urban biodiversity, wildlife, and urban greening

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Send a letter of request

- Submit a letter to CENRO describing the activity you want (see the requirements below).
- Staff receive it and forward it to the Officer-in-Charge of CENRO (OIC-CENRO).

### Step 2: CENRO reviews your request

- The OIC-CENRO evaluates your request and assigns staff based on your topic.

### Step 3: Coordinate with CENRO staff

- The assigned staff contact you to ask for more details.

### Step 4: Hold the activity

- The assigned CENRO staff attend your activity.
