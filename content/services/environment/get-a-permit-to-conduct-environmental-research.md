# Get a Permit to Conduct Environmental Research — Lapu-Lapu City

Students, professors, researchers, and public or private organizations need a permit from the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) to conduct research in Lapu-Lapu City, including studies that collect species. You apply with a letter of request and pay ₱50 per item.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                          |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Who can apply**   | Students, professors, researchers, and private and public groups |
| **Where**           | City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)            |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM                             |
| **Requirements**    | Letter of request with details of your research study            |
| **Cost**            | Research fee of ₱50.00 per item                                  |
| **Processing Time** | Three (3) days after you submit your requirements                |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your letter of request

- Bring your letter of request to CENRO. Staff receive it and forward it to the Officer-in-Charge of CENRO (OIC-CENRO).
- The OIC-CENRO passes it to the staff member who handles research permits.

### Step 2: Get interviewed

- The staff member interviews you and checks your requirements.
- If everything is complete, they tell you how much to pay.

### Step 3: Pay the research fee

- Pay at the City Treasurer's Office.
- Bring a copy of your official receipt (OR) back to CENRO.

### Step 4: Wait for approval

- CENRO prepares your Permit to Conduct Research.
- The permit goes to the City Administrator's Office for approval and signature. How long this takes depends on the City Administrator's availability.

### Step 5: Claim your permit

- Sign the release document.
- You receive your Permit to Conduct Research with the OR attached.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Letter of request that includes:
  - Title of the research study
  - Location
  - Purpose of the research study
  - Duration of the research study
  - Species to be collected

### Eligibility

- Students, professors, and researchers
- Private and public entities
