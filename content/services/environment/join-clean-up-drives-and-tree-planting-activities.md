# Organize a Coastal Cleanup or Mangrove Planting Activity — Lapu-Lapu City

Groups and organizations can hold a coastal cleanup or a mangrove planting activity in Lapu-Lapu City with a permit from the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO). CENRO staff help during the activity, and you get a certificate afterward.

---

## How to Apply

### Coastal cleanup

#### Step 1: Submit a letter of intent

- Send CENRO a letter stating the proposed date, the proposed area, and the number of participants.
- Staff forward it to the Officer-in-Charge of CENRO (OIC-CENRO), who assigns a staff member to your request.

#### Step 2: Give CENRO more details

- CENRO staff contact you to ask for more details.

#### Step 3: Pay the fee and get your permit

- Pay the ₱50 certification fee at the City Treasurer's Office.
- Give CENRO a copy of your official receipt (OR). CENRO issues your permit.

#### Step 4: Hold the cleanup

- CENRO staff assist during the cleanup.

#### Step 5: Get your certificate

- Send CENRO photos of the cleanup.
- CENRO issues a Certificate of Accomplishment or Participation.

### Mangrove planting

#### Step 1: Submit a request letter

- Send CENRO a letter stating the number of participants, the number of propagules (mangrove seedlings) you will plant, the proposed schedule and site, and your contact details.
- The OIC-CENRO evaluates your request, and a staff member contacts you.

#### Step 2: Pay the fee

- Pay the ₱50 fee at the City Treasurer's Office. CENRO receives your official receipt (OR).

#### Step 3: Get your permit

- CENRO prepares the permit and the OIC-CENRO signs it.
- Sign the release document and claim your Mangrove Planting Permit.

#### Step 4: Hold the planting

- CENRO staff assist during the planting.

#### Step 5: Get your certification

- Submit proof of the activity, such as photos and the attendance sheet.
- CENRO issues your Mangrove Planting Certification.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

**For a coastal cleanup:**

- [ ] Letter of intent stating the proposed date, proposed area, and number of participants
- [ ] Photos of the cleanup - submitted after the activity, for your certificate

**For mangrove planting:**

- [ ] Request letter stating the number of participants, number of propagules, proposed schedule and site, and contact details
- [ ] Proof of the activity, such as photos and attendance - submitted after the activity, for your certification

### Eligibility

- Coastal cleanup: individuals or groups, and public or private entities
- Mangrove planting: public or private entities

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I organize a coastal cleanup as an individual?**
A: Yes. The coastal cleanup is open to individuals or groups. Mangrove planting is for public or private entities.

**Q: Do I get a certificate for the activity?**
A: Yes. For a coastal cleanup, send CENRO photos of the cleanup to get a Certificate of Accomplishment or Participation. For mangrove planting, submit proof of the activity to get a Mangrove Planting Certification.
