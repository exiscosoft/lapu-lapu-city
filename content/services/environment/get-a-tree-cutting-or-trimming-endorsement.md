# Get a Tree Cutting or Trimming Endorsement — Lapu-Lapu City

If you plan to cut or trim trees in Lapu-Lapu City, the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) inspects the trees and issues a certification and endorsement letter. Residents, lot owners, contractors, developers, and businesses can apply.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                         |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Who can apply**   | Residents and lot owners, contractors and developers, and establishments or corporations        |
| **Where**           | City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)                                           |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM                                                            |
| **Requirements**    | Two request letters, barangay clearance, project plan, photos and sketch, replacement seedlings |
| **Cost**            | ₱50.00 certification fee                                                                        |
| **Processing Time** | Seven (7) days after you submit complete requirements                                           |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your requirements

- Bring all the documents listed below to CENRO.
- If your requirements are complete, staff give you instructions for the inspection.
- If something is missing, staff give you the full list. The process continues once you submit everything.

### Step 2: Wait for the inspection order

- Staff forward your request to the Officer-in-Charge of CENRO (OIC-CENRO) for evaluation.
- The administrative officer prepares an inspection order and sends it to the CENRO inspectors.

### Step 3: Guide the inspector to the site

- Contact the inspector and show them the location of the trees.
- The inspector checks and validates your request on site. This takes about 2 hours.

### Step 4: Claim your certification and endorsement letter

- After the inspection, CENRO prepares your certification and tree cutting endorsement letter.
- Claim them at CENRO.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Request letter addressed to the OIC-CENRO of Lapu-Lapu City, stating the number and names of the trees
- [ ] Request letter addressed to the Community Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) in Cebu City, stating the number and names of the trees
- [ ] Barangay Clearance and Certification of No Objection - from the barangay where the trees are
- [ ] Project plan or as-built plan showing the structure to be built
- [ ] Photos of the area with the trees to be cut, your contact details, and a sketch of the location
- [ ] Five (5) seedlings for every tree to be cut

### Eligibility

- Individual residents and lot owners in Lapu-Lapu City
- Contractors and developers
- Establishments and corporations

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if I don't have all the requirements yet?**
A: You can still go to CENRO. Staff will give you the full list of requirements. The inspection is set up once you submit everything.

**Q: How many seedlings do I need to provide?**
A: Five (5) seedlings for every tree you will cut.

**Q: When do I pay the ₱50 certification fee?**
A: The charter lists the fee but not a payment step. Ask CENRO staff when and where to pay.
