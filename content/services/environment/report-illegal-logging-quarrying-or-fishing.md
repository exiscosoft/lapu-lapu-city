# Report Environmental Problems — Lapu-Lapu City

You can file a complaint with the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) about environmental problems in Lapu-Lapu City, such as illegal dumping and other garbage problems, damage to the coast and sea, or air and water pollution. Filing is free, and CENRO inspects the site and can issue citation tickets to violators.

## What You Can Report

CENRO sorts complaints into these groups and assigns staff to each:

| Type of complaint  | What it covers                              |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Solid waste        | Garbage problems, including illegal dumping |
| Coastal and marine | Problems along the coast and in the sea     |
| Air and water      | Air and water pollution                     |
| Household          | Complaints about a specific household       |

If your concern doesn't fit these groups, such as illegal tree cutting or quarrying, ask CENRO whether it handles it.

---

## How to File a Complaint

### Step 1: Send your complaint letter

- Write a letter to the Officer-in-Charge of CENRO (OIC-CENRO) stating the problem and where it is.
- Attach photos of the area, your contact number, and a sketch of the location.
- Submit it to CENRO. Staff receive your complaint and take your contact details.

### Step 2: CENRO evaluates your complaint

- Staff forward your complaint to the OIC-CENRO.
- The OIC-CENRO evaluates it and sends it to the staff who handle that type of complaint.
- The administrative officer issues an inspection order.

### Step 3: Guide the inspector to the site

- An inspector contacts you. Show them the location of the problem.
- The inspector checks and verifies your complaint. The inspection takes about 2 hours.

### Step 4: CENRO acts on violations

- If the inspector finds a violation, they issue a citation ticket to the violator.
- CENRO sets the penalty according to the violation.
- The violator pays at the City Treasurer's Office and submits a copy of the official receipt (OR) and an Affidavit of Undertaking.
- CENRO conducts a follow-up inspection to check whether the violator has complied.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I have to pay to file a complaint?**
A: No. Filing a complaint is free.

**Q: What happens if the inspector finds a violation?**
A: The violator gets a citation ticket, pays at the City Treasurer's Office, and submits an Affidavit of Undertaking. CENRO then does a follow-up inspection to check whether the violator has complied.
