# Get a Special Permit for a Procession, Fun Run or Dance Benefit — Lapu-Lapu City

Planning a dance benefit, procession, motorcade, fluvial parade, fun run or marathon? You need a special permit from the City Mayor's Office. Schools, organizations, companies, barangays and the general public can apply.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Mayor's Office (application and permit); City Treasurer's Office (payment)                               |
| **When**            | Apply at least 3 working days (dance benefit), 7 working days (procession) or 20 working days (fun run) ahead |
| **Requirements**    | Request letter to the City Mayor plus endorsements; see the table below                                       |
| **Cost**            | Fees and taxes are assessed and paid at the City Treasurer's Office; the charter lists no amounts             |
| **Processing Time** | 3 days (dance benefit); 7 days (procession); 16 days (fun run)                                                |

### Compare the Permits

| Activity                                             | Apply at least         | Processing time | What you need besides the request letter                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dance benefit**                                    | 3 working days before  | 3 days          | Endorsement from your barangay                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Procession, motorcade, fluvial parade or similar** | 7 working days before  | 7 days          | Route or sketch; endorsement from each affected barangay; endorsement from the City Traffic Management System (CTMS). A fluvial parade does not need the route or the traffic endorsement.            |
| **Fun run, marathon, ultramarathon or similar**      | 20 working days before | 16 days         | Route or sketch; contingency plan; endorsement from each affected barangay; CTMS endorsement; notice to proceed from affected private establishments, if any. You also attend a coordination meeting. |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your request

- Bring your request letter addressed to the City Mayor and the supporting documents for your activity to the City Mayor's Office.
- The receiving clerk checks that your documents are complete. Takes about 30 minutes.

### Step 2: Wait for approval

- The staff-in-charge takes your request to the City Mayor or approving authority. They will call or text you about the result.
- Approval takes about 30 minutes for a dance benefit, about 2 days for a procession, and about 3 days for a fun run.

### Step 3: Attend the coordination meeting (fun runs and marathons only)

- Once your fun run is approved, staff schedule a coordination meeting (about 1 day).
- At the meeting you coordinate with the city Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (DRRMO), the Lapu-Lapu City Police Office (PNP-LCPO) and CTMS. Allow up to 7 days for this step.

### Step 4: Pay at the City Treasurer's Office

- Staff refer you to the City Treasurer's Office for payment.
- Pay the fees there and get your Official Receipt. Allow up to 4 hours.

### Step 5: Submit your Official Receipt

- Bring the Official Receipt back to the City Mayor's Office.
- Staff prepare your Special Permit and send it for signing: about 2 days for a dance benefit or procession, and 3 days for a fun run.
- You will get a call or text when the permit is ready.

### Step 6: Claim your Special Permit

- Pick up your Special Permit at the City Mayor's Office. Takes about 30 minutes.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Request letter addressed to the City Mayor - you write this yourself (all activities)
- [ ] Endorsement from your barangay - your barangay (dance benefit)
- [ ] Endorsement from each affected barangay - the affected barangays (procession, fun run)
- [ ] Route or sketch - you prepare this yourself (procession except fluvial parade, fun run)
- [ ] Endorsement from the City Traffic Management System (CTMS) - City Traffic Management Office (procession except fluvial parade, fun run)
- [ ] Contingency plan - you prepare this yourself (fun run)
- [ ] Notice to proceed from affected private establishments, if applicable - the establishments concerned (fun run)

### Eligibility

- Schools
- Organizations
- Companies (sole proprietors, partnerships and corporations)
- Barangays
- The general public

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does a fluvial parade need a route sketch or a traffic endorsement?**
A: No. The charter says the route or sketch and the CTMS endorsement do not apply to fluvial parades. You still need the request letter and the endorsement from each affected barangay.

**Q: Why do I need to apply 20 working days before a fun run?**
A: A fun run or marathon takes the longest to process (16 days). It includes approval by the City Mayor and a coordination meeting with the DRRMO, the police and CTMS.

**Q: How much is the permit fee?**
A: The charter does not list amounts. The City Treasurer's Office assesses the fees and taxes when you pay.

**Q: Where do I get the traffic endorsement?**
A: From the City Traffic Management Office.
