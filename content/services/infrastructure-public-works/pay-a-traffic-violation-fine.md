# Pay a Traffic Violation Fine — Lapu-Lapu City

If you got a traffic citation ticket or your vehicle was clamped in Lapu-Lapu City, you pay the fine at the cashier of the City Traffic Management Office (CTMO). The violator, the vehicle owner, or someone they authorize can pay.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                         |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Cashier at the payment area, City Traffic Management Office                                     |
| **When**            | Office hours are not listed in the charter; ask the office                                      |
| **Requirements**    | Duplicate copy of your traffic citation ticket (blue-green) or clamp notice, and an ID if asked |
| **Cost**            | Depends on the violation; see the Schedule of Fees displayed at the payment area                |
| **Processing Time** | Up to 10 minutes to pay; up to 1 hour to remove a clamp                                         |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Go to the cashier

- Bring the duplicate copy of your traffic citation ticket (the blue-green copy) or your clamp notice.
- Bring your driver's license or a valid ID. The cashier may ask for it to check your identity.

### Step 2: Get your fine assessed

- The cashier checks your documents and tells you how much you owe.
- Fine amounts follow the Schedule of Fees posted at the payment area.

### Step 3: Pay and get your receipt

- Pay the fine.
- The cashier gives you an Official Receipt. Keep it as proof of payment.
- If your vehicle was clamped, the clamp is removed within 1 hour.

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Duplicate copy of the traffic citation ticket (blue-green) or clamp notice - the copy you were given when you were cited or clamped
- [ ] Driver's license or valid ID - only when the cashier asks for identity verification

- The traffic violator or their authorized representative
- The vehicle owner or their authorized representative

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much is my fine?**
A: It depends on the violation. The cashier assesses the amount using the Schedule of Fees displayed at the payment area.

**Q: Can someone else pay for me?**
A: Yes. The violator or the vehicle owner can send an authorized representative.

**Q: My vehicle was clamped. How long until the clamp is removed?**
A: Up to 1 hour after you pay.

**Q: I think my ticket is wrong. What can I do?**
A: You can ask for traffic dispute resolution. A desk officer or legal officer reviews your case for free.
