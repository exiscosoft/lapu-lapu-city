# Rent a City Venue or Gym — Lapu-Lapu City

You can rent the Hoops Dome, Court 1, or the Olango Hoops Dome, Gymnasium and Mini-City Hall for your event by getting a special permit from the City Mayor's Office. Schools, organizations, companies, barangays and the general public can apply.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Mayor's Office (booking and permit); City Treasurer's Office (payment)                              |
| **When**            | Apply at least 3 working days before your event (7 working days for the Hoops Dome)                      |
| **Requirements**    | Request letter addressed to the City Mayor and an accomplished booking form                              |
| **Cost**            | Rental fees and taxes are assessed and paid at the City Treasurer's Office; the charter lists no amounts |
| **Processing Time** | 3 days (Court 1 and Olango venues); 7 days (Hoops Dome)                                                  |

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Check if the venue is free

- Go to the City Mayor's Office and ask if your chosen venue is available on your dates.
- For the Hoops Dome, staff also check whether you have unpaid rental dues or other issues.
- Takes about 30 minutes.

### Step 2: Submit your request letter and booking form

- Once the date is confirmed, get the booking form from the City Mayor's Office and fill it out.
- Submit it with your request letter addressed to the City Mayor.
- The receiving clerk checks that your documents are complete. Takes about 30 minutes.

### Step 3: Wait for approval

- The staff-in-charge takes your request to the approving authority. They will call or text you about the result.
- Approval takes 1 day for Court 1 and Olango venues, and 2 days for the Hoops Dome.
- **Hoops Dome only:** After approval, staff refer you to the Hoops Dome officer-in-charge, who assesses your planned usage (about 2 days). Bring the assessment results back to the staff-in-charge at the City Mayor's Office.

### Step 4: Pay at the City Treasurer's Office

- Staff refer you to the City Treasurer's Office for payment.
- Pay the fees there and get your Official Receipt. Allow up to 4 hours.

### Step 5: Submit your Official Receipt

- Bring the Official Receipt back to the City Mayor's Office.
- Staff prepare your Special Permit and send it for signing (about 1 day).
- You will get a call or text when the permit is ready.

### Step 6: Claim your Special Permit

- Pick up your Special Permit at the City Mayor's Office. Takes about 30 minutes.
- **Hoops Dome only:** Attend the coordination meeting with the Hoops Dome officer-in-charge, the Lapu-Lapu City Police Office (PNP-LCPO) and the City Traffic Management System (CTMS).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How early should I book?**
A: Submit your request at least 3 working days before your event for Court 1 and the Olango venues, and at least 7 working days before for the Hoops Dome.

**Q: How much does it cost to rent a venue?**
A: The charter does not list amounts. The City Treasurer's Office assesses the fees when you pay. For the Hoops Dome, the fee follows the officer-in-charge's assessment of your usage.

**Q: Can I book a barangay hall through the City Mayor's Office?**
A: The charter covers only the Hoops Dome, Court 1, and the Olango Hoops Dome, Gymnasium and Mini-City Hall. For a barangay hall, ask your barangay office.

**Q: How will I know if my request is approved?**
A: The staff-in-charge will call or text you.
