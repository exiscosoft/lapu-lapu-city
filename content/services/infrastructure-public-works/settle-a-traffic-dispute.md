# Settle a Traffic Dispute — Lapu-Lapu City

If you disagree with a traffic citation ticket, you can have it reviewed for free by a desk officer or legal officer at the City Traffic Management Office (CTMO). The legal officer can also prepare affidavits and undertakings related to traffic matters, also for free.

## How to Apply

### To dispute a traffic citation ticket

#### Step 1: Go to the desk officer or legal officer

- Bring the duplicate copy of your traffic citation ticket (the blue-green copy).
- Bring your driver's license or a valid ID. The officer may ask for it to check your identity.

#### Step 2: Have your case reviewed

- The officer checks your documents.
- The officer then decides on your case.

#### Step 3: Get the result

- If the ticket stands, you are told to pay the fine at the cashier.
- If the officer agrees with you, they recommend that the ticket be cancelled.

### To get an affidavit or undertaking

#### Step 1: Bring your documents to the legal officer

- The legal officer reviews your requirements.

#### Step 2: Answer the legal officer's questions

- The legal officer interviews you about your request and prepares the document.

#### Step 3: Receive your document

- The legal officer issues the requested document.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

**For traffic dispute resolution:**

- [ ] Duplicate copy of the traffic citation ticket (blue-green)
- [ ] Driver's license or valid ID - only when asked for identity verification

**For affidavits and undertakings:**

- [ ] Photocopy of the vehicle's valid Certificate of Registration (CR)
- [ ] Photocopy of the Official Receipt (OR) of the current registration
- [ ] Original or photocopy of the driver's license, or a valid ID of the owner, business owner or operator of the vehicle
- [ ] Original or photocopy of the police report
- [ ] Duplicate copy of the citation ticket

The charter lists these for all affidavit and undertaking requests. Ask the legal officer which ones apply to your case.

### Eligibility

- Dispute resolution: the traffic violator and/or a witness
- Affidavits and undertakings: the traffic violator, or the vehicle's operator or owner

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a fee to dispute a ticket?**
A: No. Traffic dispute resolution is free.

**Q: What happens after the review?**
A: Either you are told to pay the fine at the cashier, or the officer recommends that your ticket be cancelled.

**Q: Can a witness take part?**
A: Yes. The charter lists the traffic violator and/or a witness as the people who can use this service.

**Q: Do I pay for an affidavit or undertaking?**
A: No. The legal officer prepares it for free.
