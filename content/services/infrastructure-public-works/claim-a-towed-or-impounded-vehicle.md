# Claim a Towed or Impounded Vehicle — Lapu-Lapu City

If your vehicle was towed or impounded in Lapu-Lapu City, you can get it back from the City Traffic Management Office (CTMO) impounding area. The driver or the owner/operator of the vehicle can claim it.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Desk officer and cashier at the City Traffic Management Office, then the impounding area                               |
| **When**            | Office hours are not listed in the charter; ask the office                                                             |
| **Requirements**    | Copies of the vehicle's registration papers, your driver's license, and a towing notice or a release order (see below) |
| **Cost**            | Assessed by the cashier; see the Schedule of Fees displayed at the payment area                                        |
| **Processing Time** | Up to 1 hour                                                                                                           |

### Towed or Impounded?

| Situation                                                                                                                                                                         | What you bring besides the registration papers and license                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Towed vehicle**                                                                                                                                                                 | The towing notice                                                                                                                                |
| **Impounded after an apprehension** by the Lapu-Lapu City Police Office (LLCPO), its Traffic Enforcement Unit (TEU), or the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) | A release order from the agency that made the apprehension. If the vehicle is involved in a criminal or civil case, you also need a court order. |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your documents to the desk officer

- Give all your requirements to the desk officer.
- The desk officer reviews them and sends you to the cashier.

### Step 2: Pay at the cashier

- The cashier assesses your fees using the Schedule of Fees displayed at the payment area.
- Pay the assessed amount and get your Official Receipt.

### Step 3: Get your release order

- Show the Official Receipt and your other documents to the desk officer.
- The desk officer prepares the release order and has it signed by the head of office.
- The desk officer gives you the signed release order.

### Step 4: Claim your vehicle

- Go to the impounding area and show the release order to the impounding staff.
- They release your vehicle.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Photocopy of the vehicle's valid Certificate of Registration (CR) - from your vehicle papers
- [ ] Photocopy of the Official Receipt (OR) of the current registration - from your vehicle papers
- [ ] Original or photocopy of the driver's license
- [ ] Towing notice - for towed vehicles
- [ ] Release order from the apprehending agency (LLCPO, LLCPO TEU or CENRO) - for impounded vehicles
- [ ] Court order - only for impounded vehicles involved in criminal or civil cases

### Eligibility

- The driver of the towed or impounded vehicle
- The operator or owner of the towed or impounded vehicle

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much will I pay to get my vehicle back?**
A: The cashier assesses the fees using the Schedule of Fees displayed at the payment area. The charter does not list the amounts.

**Q: My vehicle was apprehended by the police or CENRO. Can I claim it directly at the impounding area?**
A: No. First get a release order from the agency that made the apprehension. Bring it with your other requirements to the CTMO desk officer.

**Q: Can I bring photocopies instead of originals?**
A: For the Certificate of Registration and the registration Official Receipt, photocopies are what the charter asks for. For the driver's license, you can bring the original or a photocopy.
