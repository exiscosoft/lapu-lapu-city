# Enroll at Lapu-Lapu City College — Lapu-Lapu City

After you pre-enroll at Lapu-Lapu City College, you finish your enrollment on-site with the college's enrollment officer. This guide covers new students and transferee or returnee students.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Lapu-Lapu City College: Registrar's Office (new students) or the Dean's Desk (transferees and returnees) |
| **When**            | Ask the college for the on-site enrollment schedule                                                      |
| **Requirements**    | Completed pre-enrollment, plus any requirements you still owe from pre-enrollment                        |
| **Cost**            | The charter lists no fees for this service; ask the college                                              |
| **Processing Time** | About 13 to 18 minutes, plus 5 minutes if you ask for a printed study load                               |

| Student type           | Where you start    | What happens                                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| New student            | Registrar's Office | You get your portal login and register yourself in the student portal, with help from staff  |
| Transferee or returnee | Dean's Desk        | The Dean evaluates your subjects first; the enrollment officer encodes your subjects for you |

---

## How to Apply

### For new students

#### Step 1: Get your portal login

- Go to the Registrar's Office and approach the enrollment officer.
- If you were missing requirements during pre-enrollment, go to the College Dean first and submit them before you go to the enrollment area.
- The enrollment officer gives you your student username and password (about 3 minutes).

#### Step 2: Register in the student portal

- Log in to portal.llcc.edu.ph with your username and password.
- Complete each step until your registration is successful and your study load appears. The enrollment officer can help you fill in the details (about 10 to 15 minutes).
- Your enrollment is complete once your study load is visible.

#### Step 3 (optional): Get a printed study load

- Ask the registrar or records officer for a printed copy of your study load (about 5 minutes).

### For transferee and returnee students

#### Step 1: Get your evaluation of subjects

- Go to the Dean's Desk and ask for a copy of your evaluation of subjects.
- If you were missing requirements during pre-enrollment, submit them first before you go to the enrollment area.
- The Dean gives you your evaluation, tells you which subjects to enroll in, and gives you your portal username and password (about 3 minutes).

#### Step 2: Complete your registration

- The enrollment officer encodes your subjects in the portal.
- Review your study load and acknowledge it by signing online (about 10 to 15 minutes).
- Your enrollment is complete once your study load is visible.

#### Step 3 (optional): Get a printed study load

- Ask the registrar or records officer for a printed copy of your study load (about 5 minutes).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I was missing some requirements during pre-enrollment. What do I do?**
A: Submit the missing requirements to the College Dean first, then go to the enrollment area.

**Q: Do I need a printed copy of my study load?**
A: No. Your enrollment is complete once your study load shows in the portal. A printed copy is optional; ask the registrar if you want one.
