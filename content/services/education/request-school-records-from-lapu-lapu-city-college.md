# Request School Records from Lapu-Lapu City College — Lapu-Lapu City

Former and current students of Lapu-Lapu City College can request a transcript of records, certifications, a replacement diploma, certified true copies, and an endorsement for the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) authentication process. You request at the college's Office of the Registrar or online, and pay at the Cashier's Office in City Hall.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Go to the Office of the Registrar

- Show your school ID or a valid ID. If someone is requesting for you, they need an authorization letter.
- For a lost diploma, bring an affidavit of loss. For a damaged diploma, surrender the damaged copy.
- For an authentication, certified true copy, or CAV endorsement, bring the original documents. For CAV, that means your original transcript of records and diploma.
- The records officer checks your records and gives you a request form and an order of payment (about 3 to 5 minutes).

### Step 2: Pay at City Hall

- Fill out the request form and pay at the Cashier's Office in City Hall.
- The cashier gives you an Official Receipt (OR) (about 5 minutes).

### Step 3: Submit your form and receipt

- Go back to the registrar and hand in the request form and Official Receipt.
- For a certification, authentication, certified true copy, or CAV endorsement, wait while the document is prepared (about 5 to 15 minutes).
- For a transcript of records or a replacement diploma, you get a claim stub with your release date: within 5 to 7 working days for a transcript (except during enrollment), or 2 to 3 weeks for a diploma.

### Step 4: Claim your document

- On your release date, hand your claim stub to the releasing clerk at the Registrar's Office.
- Sign the logbook and receive your document.
- For a CAV endorsement, staff also show you how to apply for CAV through CHED's eCAV system.

### Requesting online instead

You can also request a transcript of records or certifications through the student portal:

1. Log in to portal.llcc.edu.ph, click "Online Form Request," and fill out the form. The registrar verifies your identity and records (about 20 minutes).
2. Wait for an email confirming your request, with a digital order of payment and payment instructions (about 10 minutes).
3. Pay at the cashier and send your proof of payment. The registrar sends you a claim stub with your release date (about 10 minutes).
4. The registrar processes your document (3 to 5 working days, depending on the type of request).
5. On your release date, go to the Office of the Registrar, hand in your claim stub, sign the logbook, and receive your document.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] School ID or valid ID - your own
- [ ] Authorization letter - only if someone else is requesting for you
- [ ] Affidavit of loss - only for a lost diploma
- [ ] Damaged diploma - only if you are replacing a damaged one
- [ ] Original school records - for an authentication or certified true copy
- [ ] Original transcript of records and diploma - for a CAV endorsement

### Eligibility

- You must have student records at Lapu-Lapu City College. The registrar checks your records before issuing a request form.
- A representative can request for you with an authorization letter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can someone else request my records for me?**
A: Yes. Your representative needs an authorization letter along with a school ID or valid ID.

**Q: Why does my transcript take longer during enrollment?**
A: The 5 to 7 working day release time does not apply during the enrollment period. Ask the registrar for your release date.

**Q: Does the college issue the CAV itself?**
A: No. The college gives you an endorsement to CHED. Staff then show you how to apply for CAV through CHED's eCAV system.

**Q: Certifications: same day or one working day?**
A: The charter's fee list says 1 working day, but its step table has you wait at the registrar for release, about 10 to 15 minutes after you hand in your receipt. Plan for up to 1 working day.
