# Enroll Your Child in City Daycare (Early Childhood Care and Development) — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) runs free Early Childhood Care and Development (ECCD) classes for children aged 3 to 4 years and 11 months. You only need your child's birth certificate to enroll.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO), through its child development workers                      |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM (except holidays)                                                         |
| **Who Can Enroll**  | Children aged 3 to 4 years and 11 months                                                                       |
| **Requirements**    | Your child's birth certificate and a filled-out registration form                                              |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                                                           |
| **Processing Time** | 11 hours and 12 minutes in total: enrollment, parent orientation, a 4-hour class session, and a parent seminar |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Register your child

- Fill out the registration form and hand it to the child development worker, together with your child's birth certificate.
- The child development worker reviews the form (about 2 minutes), enrolls your child, and gives you a schedule for the parent orientation (about 10 minutes).

### Step 2: Attend the parent orientation

- A parent attends a 4-hour orientation on ECCD services, run by the ECCD focal person.

### Step 3: Bring your child to class

- Your child attends 4-hour class sessions, Monday to Friday.
- Sessions support your child's physical, social, emotional, thinking, spiritual, and language development.

### Step 4: Attend the Parent Effectiveness Seminar

- A parent attends a 3-hour Parent Effectiveness Seminar (PES), run by social workers and the child development worker.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Your child's birth certificate - from the Local Civil Registrar (LCR) or the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
- [ ] Registration form - fill it out when you enroll

### Eligibility

- Children aged 3 to 4 years and 11 months.
- Parents are expected to attend the orientation and the Parent Effectiveness Seminar.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: Nothing. The charter lists no fees for any step.

**Q: My child is already 5. Can I still enroll?**
A: The city program is for children aged 3 to 4 years and 11 months. Five-year-olds go to public kindergarten, which the Department of Education (DepEd) runs in public elementary schools. Ask your nearest public elementary school about enrollment.
