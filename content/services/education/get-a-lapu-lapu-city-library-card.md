# Get a Lapu-Lapu City Library Card — Lapu-Lapu City

Lapu-Lapu City residents can register as members of the Lapu-Lapu City Library for free and then borrow books and magazines. You need a government-issued ID that shows your name and where you live.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Get a Member Registration Form

- Ask library staff for a Member Registration Form. They will explain how to fill it out (about 3 minutes).

### Step 2: Submit the form with your ID

- Fill out the form and hand it in with a government-issued ID that shows your name and place of residence.
- The head librarian interviews you to confirm that you live in the city and to identify your guarantor. A City Hall employee is preferred as guarantor (about 6 minutes).

### Step 3: Wait for approval

- The head librarian signs and approves your registration (about 1 minute). You are now a library member.

### Borrowing a book or magazine

1. Bring the book or magazine you want to borrow to library staff (about 1 minute).
2. Staff check your Member Registration Form on file (about 2 minutes).
3. Fill out the Borrower's Logbook. Staff review your entry (about 4 minutes).
4. Staff prepare your borrower's card and stamp the due date (about 2 minutes).
5. Sign the borrower's card. Staff check it and file it (about 2 minutes).

Return items by the due date. Overdue books and magazines cost ₱5 per day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does it cost anything to join or borrow?**
A: No. Registration and borrowing are free. You pay ₱5 per day only if you return a book or magazine late.

**Q: Who can be my guarantor?**
A: During your interview, the library identifies a guarantor for you. A City Hall employee is preferred.
