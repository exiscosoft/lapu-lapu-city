# Claim the Board or Bar Exam Passer Incentive — Lapu-Lapu City

Lapu-Lapu City residents who pass a Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) board exam or the bar exam can apply for an incentive from the Office of the City Mayor. You must have been a registered voter in the city on or before the date of your exam.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | Office of the City Mayor                                                                                                            |
| **When**            | Ask the office for its hours                                                                                                        |
| **Requirements**    | Request letter to the City Mayor, ID with a Lapu-Lapu City address, voter's certificate, certificate of residency, proof of passing |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                                                                                |
| **Processing Time** | 3 days to approve your application. Payroll processing then takes 1 to 3 months.                                                    |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Submit your application

- Write a request letter addressed to the City Mayor.
- Bring it to the Office of the City Mayor with your supporting documents (see the list below).
- Staff check that your documents are complete and receive your application (about 30 minutes).

### Step 2: Wait for approval

- The office reviews and approves your application.
- If staff have questions, they will call or text you. **If you hear nothing, your application is considered automatically approved.**
- Approval takes 3 days in total.

### Step 3: Wait for the payout

- After approval, payroll processing takes 1 to 3 months.
- The office will notify you a few days before the incentive is distributed.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Request letter addressed to the City Mayor - you prepare this
- [ ] Photocopy of a valid government-issued ID with your Lapu-Lapu City address - you prepare this
- [ ] Voter's Certificate - from the Commission on Elections (COMELEC)
- [ ] Certificate of Residency - from your barangay
- [ ] Board passers: Certificate of Passing - from the PRC
- [ ] Bar passers: Certificate of Membership - from the Philippine Bar Association (as listed in the charter)
- [ ] Topnotchers only: Certificate of Ranking - from the PRC

The charter's first step also mentions a "Barangay Endorsement" submitted with your letter. It is not on the checklist, so ask the office whether you need one besides your Certificate of Residency.

### Eligibility

- You passed a PRC board exam or the bar exam.
- You are a resident of Lapu-Lapu City.
- You were a registered voter on or before the date of your exam.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much is the incentive?**
A: The charter does not say. Ask the Office of the City Mayor.

**Q: Do I need to follow up after I apply?**
A: No. Staff will call or text you only if they have questions. If you hear nothing, your application is considered approved. The office will notify you a few days before the payout.

**Q: I registered as a voter after my exam. Can I still apply?**
A: The charter requires that you were a registered voter on or before the time of your exam.
