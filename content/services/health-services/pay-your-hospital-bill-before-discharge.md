# Pay Your Hospital Bill Before Discharge — Lapu-Lapu City

Before an admitted patient can go home from Lapu-Lapu City Hospital, the patient or a representative gets a Statement of Account (SOA) from the Billing Section, settles the bill and gets a signed clearance. This page explains how.

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Ask for your Statement of Account and billing clearance

- The patient or an authorized representative submits the documents listed below to the billing staff.
- The billing staff check your documents, prepare and sign your Statement of Account (SOA).
- **If you have no excess bill**, you get a copy of your SOA with a signed clearance. You are done.

### Step 2: Get help with your bill, if you need it

- If your bill shows an excess (an amount left to pay), whether or not you are a No Balance Billing (NBB) patient, go to the Malasakit Center. See [get help paying hospital bills at the Malasakit Center](/services/health-services/get-help-paying-hospital-bills-at-the-malasakit-center).
- If you will not use the Malasakit Center or a social worker, go straight to the cashier.

### Step 3: Pay at the cashier

- Pay your bill or the remaining balance.
- The cashier signs your clearance.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Charge slips - from the Laboratory or Radiology Department
- [ ] Clearance form - from the nurse station
- [ ] Patient Data Sheet (PDS) - from the nurse station
- [ ] PhilHealth claim forms (CF3, CF4) - from the nurse station
- [ ] Operative record, if you had an operation - from the Operating Room or Medical Records Department
- [ ] Laboratory and imaging results - from the Laboratory or Radiology Department

### Eligibility

- All patients admitted to Lapu-Lapu City Hospital

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if I cannot pay the full bill?**
A: Go to the Malasakit Center before paying. Staff there check whether your bill is covered by No Balance Billing and can give you an availment slip to present at the cashier.

**Q: Can a family member settle the bill for me?**
A: Yes. The patient or an authorized representative can request the Statement of Account and pay the bill.

**Q: I was confined at Sta. Rosa Community Hospital. Is the process the same?**
A: At Sta. Rosa, you pay at billing, get an official receipt and clearance, then show the clearance to the ward nurse and guard on duty. See [go to the local hospital for treatment or confinement](/services/health-services/go-to-the-local-hospital-for-treatment-or-confinement).
