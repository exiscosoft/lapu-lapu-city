# Request Hospital Medical Records and Certificates — Lapu-Lapu City

If you were admitted to or examined at a city hospital, you can request a medical certificate, medico-legal certificate, discharge summary, birth certificate or death certificate from the hospital's medical records section. This covers Lapu-Lapu City Hospital and Sta. Rosa Community Hospital.

## How to Apply

### At Lapu-Lapu City Hospital

#### Step 1: Get and fill out a request form

- Ask the medical records staff for a request form. They can help you fill it out.

#### Step 2: Submit your request

- Staff check your details and whether the record is available, then retrieve your chart.
- Ask for the date and time your document will be released.

#### Step 3: Claim your document

- Staff prepare your medical or medico-legal certificate, birth certificate or death certificate.
- The document is released to you or your authorized representative.

### At Sta. Rosa Community Hospital

#### Step 1: Present your request slip

- Give your request slip to the medical records officer, who processes your document.

#### Step 2: Pay at the cashier

- Pay the fee and get an official receipt (OR).

#### Step 3: Claim your document

- Return to the medical records section and show your OR. You get your document, sealed and signed by an authorized staff member.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Request form or request slip - from the Medical Records Section
- [ ] Valid ID (Lapu-Lapu City Hospital)
- [ ] Authorization letter - if a representative is claiming for you (Lapu-Lapu City Hospital)
- [ ] Other documents needed for your claim (Sta. Rosa) - ask the records section

### Eligibility

- Lapu-Lapu City Hospital: all patients admitted to or examined at the hospital
- Sta. Rosa: patients following up on the release of documents (birth certificate, medical certificate, PhilHealth claims) and walk-in patients

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can someone else claim my medical certificate?**
A: Yes. At Lapu-Lapu City Hospital, your representative brings an authorization letter and a valid ID.

**Q: How long does it take at Lapu-Lapu City Hospital?**
A: About 6 hours and 25 minutes. Preparing the certificate alone takes about 4 hours, so ask staff for the release date and time when you submit your request.

**Q: I was a medico-legal patient at Sta. Rosa. Where do I get my medical certificate?**
A: Present your official receipt at the medical records section. The certificate is released within 1 day. See [get emergency care at a city hospital](/services/health-services/get-emergency-care-at-a-city-hospital).
