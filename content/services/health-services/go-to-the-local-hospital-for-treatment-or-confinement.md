# Go to the Local Hospital for Treatment or Confinement — Lapu-Lapu City

The city government runs two hospitals: Lapu-Lapu City Hospital in Gun-ob and Sta. Rosa Community Hospital (SRCH-Olango). This page introduces both hospitals, explains what happens when you are admitted (confined), and links to a guide for each hospital service.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Get admitted

- You can be admitted through the emergency room. See [get emergency care at a city hospital](/services/health-services/get-emergency-care-at-a-city-hospital).
- If your doctor scheduled you for a procedure, go through the same emergency room steps and show your doctor's written orders for admission.

### Step 2: Stay in the ward

- Follow the doctor's orders and the ward rules.
- Ward nurses and nursing attendants look after you, give your medicines on time and follow up your laboratory results.
- The doctor on duty checks on you during regular ward rounds and changes your orders when needed.
- The pharmacist gives your medicines and medical supplies, and the dietary department serves the meals your doctor prescribed.

### Step 3: Get ready to go home

- Wait for the doctor's discharge orders, or ask to be discharged.
- The doctor and ward nurse explain your take-home medicines and your schedule for a follow-up check-up.

### Step 4: Pay at billing

- Go to the billing section or cashier. You pay and get an official receipt and a clearance.
- If you need help paying a bill at Lapu-Lapu City Hospital, ask the [Malasakit Center](/services/health-services/get-help-paying-hospital-bills-at-the-malasakit-center).

### Step 5: Show your clearance and go home

- Present your clearance to the ward nurse and the guard on duty. They release you.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

The charter lists no requirements for ward services. These items help with admission and PhilHealth processing:

- [ ] Doctor's written orders for admission - from your doctor, if your admission was planned
- [ ] PhilHealth ID or Member Data Record (MDR) - bring it if you have one
- [ ] Valid ID - any government-issued ID
- [ ] Personal items for your stay

### Eligibility

- Patients who are admitted to the hospital

### If you are not yet a PhilHealth member

The hospital staff in charge of Point of Service (POS) enrollment can enroll you and process your PhilHealth claim. You or your family need to provide the documents they ask for. At Lapu-Lapu City Hospital, POS enrollment is done at the Malasakit Center.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to bring anything to be confined?**
A: The charter lists no requirements for ward services. If your stay was planned, bring your doctor's written orders for admission. Bring your PhilHealth ID or MDR if you have one.

**Q: Can I ask to go home?**
A: Yes. You can wait for your doctor's discharge orders or ask to be discharged. The doctor and ward nurse will explain your home medicines and follow-up schedule before you leave.

**Q: What if I am not a PhilHealth member?**
A: The hospital staff in charge of Point of Service (POS) enrollment can process your PhilHealth claim if you or your family provide the needed documents.
