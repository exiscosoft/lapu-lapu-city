# Get Laboratory Tests at Lapu-Lapu City Hospital — Lapu-Lapu City

The Lapu-Lapu City Hospital laboratory does blood, urine, stool and other tests for admitted patients and walk-in patients. You need a laboratory request signed by a doctor, and the laboratory is open 24 hours.

---

| Information         | Details                                                                                                                                                      |

### When your results are ready

Turnaround time is counted from when your blood is taken.

| Patient type                     | Routine tests  | Other tests    | Chemistry tests that need fasting |
| -------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Outpatient department            | Within 3 hours | Within 4 hours | Next day, 7:00 AM                 |
| Emergency department or admitted | Within 2 hours | Within 3 hours | —                                 |

**STAT (urgent) tests** are faster: complete blood count (CBC), blood typing, Troponin I, sodium and potassium (Na & K) and pregnancy test within 1 hour; other tests within 2 hours.

- **Routine tests**: CBC, blood typing, urinalysis, fecalysis and pregnancy test
- **Other tests**: FBS (fasting blood sugar), lipid panel, BUN (blood urea nitrogen), creatinine, uric acid, SGPT and SGOT (liver tests), TBDB (bilirubin), albumin, Na & K, syphilis, Dengue Duo, HBsAg (hepatitis B), HIV, Troponin I, CRP (C-reactive protein), HbA1c and FOBT (fecal occult blood test)

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Present your laboratory request

- Hand your signed request to the laboratory receptionist. If you are admitted, the nurse logs your request.

### Step 2: Pay or have it charged to your bill

- **Outpatients**: pay at the cashier, or go to the Malasakit Center for help.
- **Emergency and admitted patients**: the test is charged to your hospital bill.

### Step 3: Go to the laboratory

- Show your official receipt (OR) or charge slip. Laboratory staff accept your request.

### Step 4: Give your sample

- A medical technologist or phlebotomist takes your blood, urine or other sample.
- The medical technologist checks that your sample is good enough to test.

### Step 5: Claim your results

- Wait for the turnaround time of your test.
- Bring a valid ID and your OR or availment slip to claim your official results.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Laboratory request form signed by a doctor - from the Outpatient Department (OPD patients), the Emergency Department (ER patients), or the laboratory itself (patients from other facilities or clinics)
- [ ] Official receipt, charge slip or availment slip - from the cashier, billing or the Malasakit Center
- [ ] Valid ID - to claim your results

### Eligibility

- The general public, including admitted and non-admitted patients

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I get a test with a request from a private clinic?**
A: Yes. Patients from other facilities or clinics bring a request form signed by a doctor. You can get the laboratory request form at the laboratory.

**Q: When will my results be ready?**
A: It depends on the test. For outpatients, routine tests take up to 3 hours, other tests up to 4 hours, and chemistry tests that need fasting are released at 7:00 AM the next day. See the table above.

**Q: What do I bring to claim my results?**
A: A valid ID and your official receipt or availment slip.
