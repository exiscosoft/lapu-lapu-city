# Get Emergency Care at a City Hospital — Lapu-Lapu City

If you or someone with you needs urgent medical care, go to the emergency department of Lapu-Lapu City Hospital or Sta. Rosa Community Hospital. Both hospitals are run by the city government, and their emergency rooms are open 24 hours a day.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Go to the triage area of the emergency room

- A triage nurse checks you, takes your vital signs and decides how urgent your case is.
- Patients who need immediate attention are served first.
- If another facility referred you, hand over your referral form.

### Step 2: Fill out the information sheet

- You get a personal information sheet (at Sta. Rosa, a registration data sheet).
- If the patient cannot fill it out or sign, the person with them signs instead.
- The nurse takes you to the right area: measles lane, medicine, pediatrics, isolation or minor surgery.

### Step 3: Get examined by the emergency doctor

- The nurse and emergency doctor interview you and do a physical examination.
- You may get a request form for medical supplies. Items the hospital does not have are written on a prescription.
- If you need laboratory or X-ray tests, the nurse fills out a request form and tells you where to go.

### Step 4: Get treatment

- The emergency doctor and nurse give your treatment and record the doctor's orders.
- Laboratory and radiology tests are charged based on their schedule of fees.

### Step 5: Go home or get admitted

- **If you do not need admission**: the doctor and nurse give you instructions for your take-home medicines and home care. At Sta. Rosa, patients who are treated in the emergency room and sent home are charged for the services they used.
- **If you need admission**: you (or the person with you, if you cannot sign) sign a consent form. The nurse prepares your chart and moves you to your ward. See [what to expect when you are confined](/services/health-services/go-to-the-local-hospital-for-treatment-or-confinement).
- **If you need a bigger hospital**: the doctor and nurse on duty arrange your referral and transfer.

### For medico-legal cases at Sta. Rosa

1. Seek medical attention at the emergency room. The doctor examines you and issues an order of payment.
2. Pay at billing and get an official receipt (OR).
3. Present the OR at the medical records section to claim your medical certificate. It is released within 1 day. Persons deprived of liberty (PDL) are served for free and skip this step.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Referral form - from the health facility that referred you, if any
- [ ] Registration data sheet or personal information sheet - given to you at the emergency room
- [ ] PhilHealth ID or Member Data Record (MDR) - bring it if you have one (asked by Sta. Rosa)
- [ ] Doctor's referral or admitting orders - from your doctor, if your doctor sent you for admission or a procedure (asked by Sta. Rosa)

### Eligibility

- Anyone can use the Lapu-Lapu City Hospital emergency department.
- At Sta. Rosa, the emergency room serves patients whose condition needs immediate medical attention, and medico-legal cases within 24 hours.

### Discounts on medico-legal fees at Sta. Rosa

- Persons with disability (PWD)
- Senior citizens
- Indigents with a certificate of indigency
- Persons deprived of liberty (PDL): free
- Sponsored members (4Ps)
- Kasambahay (household workers)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I go to the emergency room when the outpatient department is closed?**
A: Yes. At Sta. Rosa, the emergency room serves walk-in patients who need a consultation after the outpatient department closes at 5:00 PM. Patients who need immediate attention are still served first. At Lapu-Lapu City Hospital, go to the emergency department on weekends and holidays.

**Q: Will I pay if I am treated and sent home?**
A: At Sta. Rosa, yes. You are charged for the services you used. At both hospitals, medical supplies and tests are charged based on the hospital's fees.

**Q: What if I cannot afford the bill?**
A: At Lapu-Lapu City Hospital, the Malasakit Center can check your PhilHealth eligibility and help with medicines, tests and your hospital bill. See [get help paying hospital bills at the Malasakit Center](/services/health-services/get-help-paying-hospital-bills-at-the-malasakit-center).
