# Consult a Doctor at a Hospital Outpatient Department — Lapu-Lapu City

If you need to see a doctor for a problem that is not an emergency, go to the outpatient department (OPD) of Lapu-Lapu City Hospital or Sta. Rosa Community Hospital. The doctor checks you, prescribes medicines and orders tests if needed.

## How to Apply

### At Lapu-Lapu City Hospital

#### Step 1: Register

- **New patient**: fill out the OPD registration form.
- **Returning patient**: skip the form and hand over your index card. Staff pull out your record.
- You get a queuing number.

#### Step 2: Wait for your number

- A nurse calls your number, asks about your health history and takes your vital signs.

#### Step 3: Go to the Yakap desk

- Staff check whether you are a PhilHealth member and a Yakap member. Bring any government-issued ID or your PhilHealth Member Data Record (MDR).
- If you are not a PhilHealth member, staff guide you to the Medical Social Services/Malasakit Center, where PhilHealth Cares can enroll you.

#### Step 4: See the doctor

- The doctor checks you and prescribes medicines, home care or tests.
- If you need no tests, the nurse explains how to take your medicines or do your home care. You are done.

#### Step 5: Get your tests, if ordered

- The nurse fills out your laboratory or imaging request form.
- **Yakap members**: the Yakap desk encodes and approves your request, then sends you to the laboratory or radiology department.
- **Non-Yakap members**: go straight to the laboratory or radiology department. Tests are charged based on their schedule of fees.

### At Sta. Rosa Community Hospital

#### Step 1: Get a priority number

- Wait for your number. The nurse records your information, asks about your health problem and takes your vital signs.

#### Step 2: See the doctor or dentist

- When your name is called, go to the medical officer's, eye doctor's or dentist's table.
- The doctor examines you, prescribes medicines or orders laboratory tests, and tells you when to come back for a follow-up.
- If you need tests, the nurse gives you charge slips.

#### Step 3: Pay at the cashier

- Pay for your tests and get an official receipt (OR).

#### Step 4: Get your tests

- Present your OR at the laboratory.
- X-ray or ultrasound: about 1 hour. Complete blood count and urine test: about 1 hour. Blood chemistry: within 1 day.

#### Step 5: Show your results to the doctor

- The doctor reads your results, and the pharmacist gives you your medicines and supplies.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Referral - from your barangay health center (Sta. Rosa asks for one only if needed)
- [ ] Index card - from the Lapu-Lapu City Hospital OPD, if you are a returning patient
- [ ] Government-issued ID or PhilHealth Member Data Record (MDR) - for the Yakap desk at Lapu-Lapu City Hospital; Sta. Rosa prefers that you bring an ID

### Eligibility

- Lapu-Lapu City Hospital: the general public. The OPD serves Lapu-Lapu City residents and patients with non-emergency cases.
- Sta. Rosa: patients who need a medical or dental consultation, prenatal check-up, eye consultation or other services

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Where do I go if I need a doctor on a weekend or holiday?**
A: Go to the emergency department. Lapu-Lapu City Hospital sends weekend and holiday patients there. At Sta. Rosa, the emergency room also serves walk-in patients after the OPD closes at 5:00 PM.

**Q: Do I need a referral?**
A: Lapu-Lapu City Hospital lists a referral from a health center as a requirement. Sta. Rosa asks for a referral from your barangay health center only if needed.

**Q: Do I pay for the consultation?**
A: At Lapu-Lapu City Hospital, the charter lists no fee for registration or consultation. Tests are charged based on the laboratory and radiology schedule of fees, unless the Yakap desk approves them. At Sta. Rosa, ask for the schedule of fees at the OPD.
