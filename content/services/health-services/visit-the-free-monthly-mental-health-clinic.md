# Visit the Free Monthly Mental Health Clinic — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) holds a free mental health clinic once a month. If you or a family member has a mental health concern, you can see a psychologist or psychiatrist and get free medicine. You do not need to bring any documents.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                    |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | The venue announced for each month's clinic                |
| **When**            | Once a month; the schedule is announced before each clinic |
| **Requirements**    | None                                                       |
| **Cost**            | Free, including medicine                                   |
| **Processing Time** | About 50 minutes                                           |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Go to the clinic venue

- Go to the venue on the announced date.
- Staff from the CSWDO, the City Health Office (CHO) or a Barangay Health Worker (BHW) do a short intake interview with you.

### Step 2: Talk to a psychologist or psychiatrist

- Staff send you to a psychologist or psychiatrist for an assessment interview.
- This is run with staff from the Philippine Mental Health Association (PMHA) Cebu Chapter.

### Step 3: Get your free medicine

- If medicine is prescribed, PMHA and CHO staff give it to you for free.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] None

### Eligibility

- Patients of the City Health Office who have a mental illness or disorder
- People referred by a Barangay Health Worker
- Walk-in clients with mental health concerns

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a referral?**
A: No. Walk-in clients with mental health concerns are welcome. Clients identified by the City Health Office and those referred by a Barangay Health Worker can also attend.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: Nothing. The consultation and medicine are free.

**Q: When is the next clinic?**
A: The clinic is held monthly, and each schedule is announced ahead of time. Ask the CSWDO, the City Health Office or your Barangay Health Worker.
