# Get Help Paying Hospital Bills at the Malasakit Center — Lapu-Lapu City

The Malasakit Center at Lapu-Lapu City Hospital helps poor patients and patients who cannot afford their care. It can enroll you in PhilHealth, help with tests and medicines, help with your hospital bill, and refer you for free medicines or services the hospital does not offer. The service is free.

## How to Apply

### If you are admitted or in the emergency room

#### Step 1: PhilHealth check

- PhilHealth Cares (PCARES) staff check whether you are eligible for PhilHealth.

#### Step 2: Enroll in PhilHealth at the Point of Service, if eligible

- If you qualify, Malasakit Center staff enroll you so you can get free PhilHealth benefits.

#### Step 3: Ask for help with tests and medicines

- Show your doctor's request or prescription. Staff and the pharmacy check whether the test or medicine is available in the hospital and pre-approve it to be charged to your bill.
- **Free medicines from Rose Pharmacy**: if the hospital pharmacy does not have your medicine, the pharmacy stamps your prescription "not available". Malasakit staff interview you and give you a guarantee letter for Rose Pharmacy. This is only for Lapu-Lapu City voters. For a child patient, a parent must be a Lapu-Lapu City voter.

#### Step 4: Ask for a referral for services the hospital does not have

- For services such as a CT scan, endoscopy or colonoscopy, staff refer you to partner agencies such as the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO), the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), the city government and private partners.

#### Step 5: Ask for help with your hospital bill

- Staff check whether your Statement of Account (SOA) falls under No Balance Billing (NBB).
- **NBB with zero balance**: go to the cashier.
- **NBB with an excess**: staff collect your documents and give you an availment slip to present at the cashier.
- **Not NBB and not related to pregnancy**: staff give you an availment slip, and your bill is covered by Medical Assistance for Indigent and Financially Incapacitated Patients (MAIFIP) funds.

### If you are an outpatient

#### Step 1: Ask for help with tests and medicines

- Bring your laboratory request, radiology request or prescription together with your charge slip.
- Staff fill out a Medical Assistance Monitoring Form and a Certificate of Eligibility, then give you an availment slip.

#### Step 2: Free medicines from Rose Pharmacy (senior citizens)

- Staff check that you are a senior citizen and a Lapu-Lapu City voter.
- Check first with the hospital pharmacy. If your medicine is not available, staff refer you to Rose Pharmacy.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

**PhilHealth Point of Service enrollment**

- [ ] Patient Service Slip and Clinical Cover Slip
- [ ] PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF)
- [ ] NSO/PSA document of the patient
- [ ] Government-issued ID of the patient and the representative (3 copies)
- [ ] Marriage certificate with registry number, if applicable

**Tests and medicines available in the hospital**

- [ ] Doctor's request or prescription with complete details and the doctor's license number
- [ ] Photocopy of the government-issued ID of the patient and authorized representative (outpatients)

**Free medicines through Rose Pharmacy**

- [ ] Medical abstract (admitted patients) or medical certificate (outpatients)
- [ ] Prescription with the doctor's complete name, signature and license number
- [ ] Photocopy of the government-issued ID of the patient and representative (outpatients: Senior Citizen ID of the patient or government-issued ID of the representative)

**Referral for services the hospital does not have**

- [ ] Medical abstract
- [ ] Photocopy of the government-issued ID of the patient and representative
- [ ] Quotation for the procedure
- [ ] Referral letter - from the Malasakit Center staff

**Help with your hospital bill**

- [ ] Statement of Account (bill) - from the Billing Section
- [ ] Photocopy of the government-issued ID of the patient or representative
- [ ] Photocopy of the NBB portal

### Eligibility

- All admitted patients and outpatients at Lapu-Lapu City Hospital. The service is meant for patients who are poor (indigent) or cannot afford their care.
- Free medicines through Rose Pharmacy: Lapu-Lapu City voters (for admitted patients); senior citizens who are Lapu-Lapu City voters (for outpatients)
- **Not eligible for Malasakit help with the hospital bill**: patients who go home against medical advice (HAMA), medico-legal cases, and OB-Gyne cases without complications

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Who cannot get Malasakit help with a hospital bill?**
A: Patients who go home against medical advice (HAMA), medico-legal cases, and OB-Gyne cases without complications are not eligible.

**Q: Who can get free medicines from Rose Pharmacy?**
A: Only Lapu-Lapu City voters. For a child patient, a parent must be a Lapu-Lapu City voter. Outpatients must be senior citizens who are Lapu-Lapu City voters. You get the medicine from Rose Pharmacy only when the hospital pharmacy does not have it.

**Q: The hospital does not offer the procedure I need. Can the Malasakit Center help?**
A: Yes. Staff can refer you to partner agencies such as PCSO, DSWD, the city government and private partners. Bring your medical abstract, a photocopy of your ID and a quotation for the procedure.
