# Get an X-ray, Ultrasound, 2D Echo or ECG — Lapu-Lapu City

The Radiology Department of Lapu-Lapu City Hospital does X-rays, general and pregnancy (OB) ultrasounds, heart ultrasounds (2D echo) and electrocardiograms (ECG). You need a request from a doctor for each test.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Get a request from a doctor

- Get a signed request from the outpatient department, the emergency department, the nurse station (if you are admitted) or a private doctor's clinic.

### Step 2: Submit your request at the Radiology Department

- The radiology receptionist checks your request and tells you the cost.
- For an ultrasound or 2D echo, the receptionist also gives you a schedule for the procedure.

### Step 3: Pay or have it charged to your bill

- **Outpatients**: pay at the cashier, or go to the Malasakit Center for help.
- **Emergency and admitted patients**: the test is charged to your hospital bill.

### Step 4: Have the test done

- Return to the Radiology Department with your official receipt (OR) or charge slip. For an ultrasound or 2D echo, come back on your scheduled date.
- The procedure takes about 45 minutes.

### Step 5: Get a claim slip and ask when your result will be ready

- Staff tell you the day and time your official result will be released. X-ray, 2D echo and ECG results take about 3 days. Ultrasound results take about 1 day.

### Step 6: Claim your official result

- Bring a valid ID and your OR or availment slip.
- For X-ray, ultrasound and 2D echo, sign the logbook when you receive your result.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- [ ] Doctor's request or order - X-ray: from the Outpatient Department, Emergency Department, or the laboratory (for patients from other facilities or clinics). Ultrasound: from the nurse station, Outpatient Department or a doctor's clinic. 2D echo and ECG: 1 copy from the nurse's station (admitted patients), the Outpatient Department or a private doctor's clinic.
- [ ] Official receipt, charge slip or availment slip - from the cashier, billing or the Malasakit Center
- [ ] Valid ID - to claim your result

### Eligibility

- X-ray: the general public
- Ultrasound: admitted patients and outpatients with a doctor's request for a general or OB ultrasound
- 2D echo and ECG: patients with a doctor's order or request

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does a 2D echo cost?**
A: ₱2,000: a ₱1,500 procedure fee and a ₱500 reader's fee. This applies if you do not use the Malasakit program or have no PhilHealth coverage.

**Q: Can I use a request from my private doctor?**
A: Yes. For 2D echo and ECG, a request from a private doctor's clinic is accepted. For ultrasound, a request from a doctor's clinic is accepted. For X-ray, patients from other facilities or clinics get their request form at the laboratory.

**Q: When can I get an OB ultrasound?**
A: OB-Gyne ultrasounds are done on Tuesdays, 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM. You get a schedule when you submit your request.
