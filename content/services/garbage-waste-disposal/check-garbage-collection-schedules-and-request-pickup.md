# Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) collects garbage for free from households along national roads and city streets in Lapu-Lapu City, every day of the week. You need to segregate your garbage before it is collected.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information      | Details                                                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Who it's for** | Residents and households along national roads and city streets            |
| **Where**        | Put your garbage at the designated area or holding station on your street |
| **When**         | Monday to Sunday, 6:00 PM to 12:00 AM                                     |
| **Requirements** | Segregate your garbage at the source                                      |
| **Cost**         | Free                                                                      |

---

## How It Works

### Step 1: Put out your segregated garbage

- Place your garbage at the designated area or holding station.
- The truck waits about 10 minutes at each designated area, so have your garbage out before it arrives.

### Step 2: The truck collects along its route

Garbage trucks follow assigned routes in shifts:

| Shift     | Hours               |
| --------- | ------------------- |
| 1st shift | 6:00 PM to 2:00 AM  |
| 2nd shift | 2:00 AM to 10:00 AM |
| Striker   | 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM  |

### Step 3: Garbage goes to the Material Recovery Facility

- The trucks bring the garbage to the city's Material Recovery Facility (MRF) for further segregation.
- Trucks are weighed on a computerized scale when full and again after unloading, then washed for the next shift.

### Step 4: Residual waste goes to the sanitary landfill

- Private haulers take residual waste (garbage that can't be recycled or composted) from the MRF to the sanitary landfill. See the [City Environment and Natural Resources Office](/government/departments/city-environment-and-natural-resources-office) page for details.

---

## Where to Check Your Schedule

The charter lists the collection hours and shifts above but not the route for each street. To find out when the truck passes your area:

- Ask at your **Barangay Hall**. Barangays post collection schedules and also run their own garbage trucks.
- Follow your **barangay's Facebook page** and city government announcements for delays, holiday changes, and typhoon disruptions.

---

## Bulk or Special Pickup

The Citizen's Charter does not describe a process for requesting pickup of bulk items such as furniture, appliances, construction debris, or yard trimmings. Ask your Barangay Hall or CENRO how to arrange it. For hazardous household waste and electronics, see [request special waste collection](/services/garbage-waste-disposal/request-special-waste-collection-hazardous-materials-electronics).

---

## Tips for Residents

- Use durable, closed containers so animals don't scatter your garbage.
- For recyclables, check if your barangay has a Material Recovery Facility (MRF) or a partner junk shop.
- Don't burn garbage. Open burning is prohibited under Republic Act 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act) and the Clean Air Act.
- Businesses and establishments must segregate their waste and may need to contract their own private hauler for bulk or industrial waste. Keep your receipts and hauling contracts for CENRO compliance inspections.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a fee for garbage collection?**
A: No. City garbage collection is free.

**Q: Will the truck take unsegregated garbage?**
A: The charter lists proper segregation at the source as the requirement for collection. Separate your garbage before putting it out.

**Q: How long does the truck wait at my street?**
A: About 10 minutes at each designated area.
