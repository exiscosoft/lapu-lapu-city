# Report Illegal Dumping & Waste Management Violations — Lapu-Lapu City

You can report illegal dumping, open burning, and other garbage problems in your area to the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO). Filing a complaint is free.

---

## What to Report

| Violation                     | Examples                                                                 |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Illegal dumping**           | Trash thrown in vacant lots, rivers, canals, sidewalks, or open spaces   |
| **Open burning**              | Burning household or yard waste, plastics, or tires                      |
| **Hazardous waste**           | Batteries, chemicals, or medical waste thrown out with household garbage |
| **Uncollected garbage sites** | Overflowing bins or piles of uncollected waste                           |

---

## How to Report

- **Your barangay:** for problems on your street, you can start at your Barangay Hall.
- **CENRO:** to file a formal complaint, follow the steps on [Report environmental problems](/services/environment/report-illegal-logging-quarrying-or-fishing). That page has the requirements, processing time, and the official Citizen's Charter for the complaint process.

### Tips for an Effective Report

- Take clear photos or videos, and include street names or landmarks.
- Note the time and date you saw the problem.
- Give your contact number if you want updates.
