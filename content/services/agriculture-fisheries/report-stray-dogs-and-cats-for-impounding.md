# Report Stray Dogs and Cats for Impounding — Lapu-Lapu City

Residents of Lapu-Lapu City can ask the City Veterinary Office (CVO) to pick up stray, roaming, or unwanted dogs and cats in their area. This page also explains how owners can claim a pet that was impounded.

## How to Report Stray Animals

### Step 1: Send your request to the City Veterinarian

- Visit the City Veterinary Office, call, or send a letter addressed to the City Veterinarian.
- Give your name, address, and contact number. The staff records your request.

### Step 2: Wait for the schedule

- The office schedules an impounding operation within 20 working days and tells you by phone or by letter.
- Two to three days before the operation, the office informs your barangay officials and asks for their help.

### Step 3: The impounding operation

- City Veterinary Office staff catch stray and roaming animals in one day, giving priority to unwanted dogs.
- Impounded animals stay in the city pound. Owners have 10 days to claim them. Unclaimed animals are screened for adoption.

---

## How to Claim Your Impounded Pet

### Step 1: Go to the Animal Welfare Section

- Visit the Animal Welfare Section of the City Veterinary Office within 10 days of the impounding.
- The staff talks with you about responsible pet ownership and the penalties under the city's rabies program, and gives you leaflets.
- If you cannot pay the ₱500 fee, the charter mentions a promissory note signed by your Barangay Captain. Ask the office how this works.

### Step 2: Pay the impounding fee

- Pay the fee at the City Treasurer's Office and keep the official receipt (OR).

### Step 3: Show your receipt to the impounding officer

- Present the official receipt to the impounding officer.
- If your pet is not registered or vaccinated, the staff registers and vaccinates it.

### Step 4: Take your pet home

- The staff releases your pet to you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long do I have to claim my impounded pet?**
A: 10 days. After that, unclaimed animals are screened for adoption.

**Q: How much is the penalty to get my pet back?**
A: ₱500 per pet for a first offense, ₱1,000 for a second offense, and ₱2,000 for a third or later offense. You pay at the City Treasurer's Office.

**Q: Can I adopt an animal that nobody claimed?**
A: Yes. See [Surrender or adopt a pet](/services/agriculture-fisheries/surrender-or-adopt-a-pet).
