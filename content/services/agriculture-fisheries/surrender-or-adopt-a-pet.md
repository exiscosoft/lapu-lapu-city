# Surrender or Adopt a Pet — Lapu-Lapu City

The City Veterinary Office (CVO) takes in dogs and cats that their owners can no longer keep, and lets the public adopt impounded pets that no owner claimed.

## How to Surrender a Pet

The City Veterinary Office accepts pets whose owners can no longer care for them, for example because the animal is sick or may be a risk to people.

### Option A: Bring your pet to the office

#### Step 1: Fill out the surrender form

- Bring your pet. The staff records your request in the logbook.
- Fill out the surrender form and hand it to the staff, who checks your entries.
- If your pet is registered, hand over its Green Card (Registration Certificate).

#### Step 2: Place your pet in the city pound

- The staff helps you place your pet in a city pound cage.

### Option B: Ask for a home pick-up

#### Step 1: Call the City Veterinary Office

- Give your name, address, contact number, reason for surrendering, and the number of animals. The staff may ask for a sketch of how to find your house.

#### Step 2: Wait for the schedule

- The office calls you with the pick-up date.

#### Step 3: Hand over your pet at home

- On the scheduled day, fill out the forms and give them to the impounding officer.
- Present your pet and hand over its Green Card and vaccination certificate, if any.
- The staff takes your pet to the city pound.

---

## How to Adopt a Pet

### Step 1: Choose a pet at the City Pound

- Visit the City Veterinary Office and choose a pet at the City Pound.
- Fill out the adoption form and the registration form. The staff checks your form and evaluates you as an adopter.

### Step 2: Pay the adoption fee

- Pay ₱250 per pet. You get an official receipt.

### Step 3: Wait while your pet is prepared

- The staff registers and vaccinates your new pet and puts on a collar.
- You get its registration and vaccination certificates and reading materials on responsible pet ownership.

### Step 4: Take your new pet home

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

**To surrender a pet:**

- [ ] Registration Certificate (Green Card) - only if your pet is registered
- [ ] Filled-out surrender form - from the City Veterinary Office

**To adopt a pet:**

- [ ] 1 valid ID
- [ ] Filled-out adoption form and registration form - from the City Veterinary Office

### Eligibility

- **Surrender:** pet owners in Lapu-Lapu City
- **Adoption:** anyone (general public)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Where do the pets for adoption come from?**
A: They are dogs and cats that were impounded and not claimed by their owners.

**Q: Will my adopted pet be vaccinated?**
A: Yes. Before you take it home, the staff registers and vaccinates your new pet and gives you its registration and vaccination certificates.

**Q: Is there a fee to surrender a pet?**
A: Yes. The charter lists a fee of ₱1,000.
