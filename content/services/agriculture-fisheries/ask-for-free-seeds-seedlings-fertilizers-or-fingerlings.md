# Ask for Free Seeds for Backyard Gardening — Lapu-Lapu City

Residents, lot owners, and public or private groups can get free seed packs from the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) for backyard gardening and urban farming, for personal use or for educational purposes.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                               |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM                   |
| **Requirements**    | None                                                  |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                  |
| **Processing Time** | 1 day                                                 |

**What the charter covers:** The city's Citizen's Charter lists only seed distribution, under the Backyard Gardening / Urban Agriculture Program. It does not list free seedlings, fertilizers, or fingerlings. If you need those, ask the office whether any program is available.

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Ask for seeds at CENRO

- Tell the staff how many seed packs you need and whether they are for personal use or for educational purposes.
- The staff records your name, the number of seed packs, and your purpose, and reviews your request.
- The staff enters you in the recipients' logbook.

### Step 2: Receive your seed packs

- The staff gives you the seed packs you asked for.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I get free seedlings, fertilizers, or fingerlings?**
A: The Citizen's Charter covers only seed packs for backyard gardening. Ask CENRO whether other farming or fishing inputs are available.

**Q: Can a school or organization ask for seeds?**
A: Yes. Public and private entities can request seeds, including for educational purposes.
