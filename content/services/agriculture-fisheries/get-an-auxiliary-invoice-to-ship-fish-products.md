# Get an Auxiliary Invoice to Ship Fish Products — Lapu-Lapu City

If you ship fish or other fishery products, the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) issues the auxiliary invoice for your shipment. It costs ₱50 per box, and no documents are required.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                     |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)                       |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM; Saturday and Sunday, 5:00 AM – 2:00 PM |
| **Requirements**    | None. Bring the boxes or items you are shipping for inspection.             |
| **Cost**            | ₱50 per box                                                                 |
| **Processing Time** | 1 day                                                                       |

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Ask for an auxiliary invoice

- Tell the staff on duty that you need an auxiliary invoice.
- The staff inspects and counts your boxes or items.

### Step 2: Pay the fee

- The staff tells you how much to pay, based on the number of boxes.
- Pay the assigned staff member directly. You get the original copy of the official receipt (OR).

### Step 3: Receive your invoice

- The staff prepares and releases your auxiliary invoice.

---

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

- No documents are needed. Have your boxes or items ready for inspection.

### Eligibility

- Shippers
- Private and public entities, or anyone who ships fish and fishery products

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I get an auxiliary invoice on weekends?**
A: Yes. The service is open Saturday and Sunday from 5:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

**Q: Do I pay at the City Treasurer's Office?**
A: No. For this service, you pay the assigned CENRO staff member and get the original copy of the official receipt.
