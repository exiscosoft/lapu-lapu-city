# Have Livestock Slaughtered and Meat Inspected — Lapu-Lapu City

Meat suppliers and dealers bring animals for slaughter to the city slaughterhouse, where the City Veterinary Office (CVO) inspects them before and after slaughter. Meat slaughtered elsewhere and sold in the city's markets and supermarkets also goes through a post-abattoir inspection.

---

## How to Apply

### Slaughter and inspection

#### Step 1: Show your documents at the main gate

- Present the veterinary health certificate (VHC) or shipping permit when you enter the main gate.
- For cattle and goats, also present the certificate of ownership.
- The livestock inspector, with the security guard on duty, checks the origin, owner, and documents.

#### Step 2: Ante-mortem inspection

- The livestock inspector checks every animal delivered before slaughter. Only healthy animals are slaughtered. Unhealthy animals are separated and dealt with accordingly.

#### Step 3: Pay the fees

- Pay the fees through the City Treasurer's Office representative at the slaughterhouse (the slaughterhouse collector). You get an official receipt.

#### Step 4: Slaughter and post-mortem inspection

- Present your animals for slaughter.
- Slaughterhouse staff weigh each carcass and log the weight and the owner's name.
- The meat inspector checks the meat and internal organs, stamps each carcass "Inspected and Passed," and issues a Meat Inspection Certificate (MIC) per carcass.

#### Step 5: Deliver the meat

- Slaughterhouse staff give your meat van driver a gate pass.
- The guard on duty checks the gate pass and the number of carcasses in the van before it leaves.

### Post-abattoir inspection (meat from other slaughterhouses)

#### Step 1: Present your Certificate of Meat Inspection

- Your NMIS-accredited meat transport van representative presents the Certificate of Meat Inspection (COMI) from an accredited slaughterhouse.
- The post-abattoir collector checks the number of entries, and the meat inspector inspects the meat.

#### Step 2: Pay the regulatory service fee

- Pay the fee after inspection. The City Veterinary collector issues an official receipt.

#### Step 3: Proceed to your destination

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the slaughterhouse open on holidays?**
A: Yes. It operates daily, including holidays, in three shifts that cover the full day.

**Q: What happens if my animal is found unhealthy?**
A: Only healthy animals are slaughtered. Animals found unhealthy during the ante-mortem inspection are separated and dealt with accordingly.

**Q: I bring meat from another slaughterhouse. What do I need?**
A: A Certificate of Meat Inspection (COMI) from an accredited slaughterhouse and an NMIS-accredited meat transport van. You pay a post-abattoir inspection fee per head or per kilo.
