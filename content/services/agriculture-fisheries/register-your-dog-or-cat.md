# Register Your Dog or Cat — Lapu-Lapu City

All dog and cat owners in Lapu-Lapu City can register their pets with the City Veterinary Office (CVO). Registration is free and done once. You get a Registration Certificate, also called the Green Card.

---

| Information         | Details                                                                                                         |

---

## How to Apply

You can walk in to the office, or call to ask for a home visit.

### Option A: Walk in to the City Veterinary Office

#### Step 1: Fill out the registration form

- Ask the staff for the registration form, fill it out, and hand it back.

#### Step 2: Present your pet

- Bring your dog or cat. The staff registers your pet and issues the Registration Certificate (Green Card).

#### Step 3: Keep your Green Card

- Keep the Green Card and bring it on your next visit, for example for your pet's anti-rabies vaccination.

### Option B: Ask for a home visit (on-call)

#### Step 1: Call the office

- The staff logs your request and schedules a home visit.
- The office calls you within 3 working days to tell you the schedule.

#### Step 2: Register your pet at home

- On the scheduled day, fill out the forms and give them to the field staff.
- The staff checks your entries and registers your dog or cat.

#### Step 3: Get your Green Card and tag

- You receive the Registration Certificate (Green Card) with a tag number. Keep it for your next visit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does it cost to register my pet?**
A: Registration is free and done only once. If you need a replacement Green Card, the fee is ₱100.

**Q: Do I need to register my pet before it can get an anti-rabies shot?**
A: Yes. The City Veterinary Office asks for the Registration Certificate or Green Card when you bring your pet for anti-rabies vaccination.
