# Get a Fishing Permit and Register Your Boat — Lapu-Lapu City

If you fish or own a fishing boat in Lapu-Lapu City, the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) issues your fishing permit, boat registration, and permit to operate your boat's engine.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Where**           | City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)                                            |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM                                                              |
| **Requirements**    | Application form, barangay certificate, and details and photos of your boat (see list below)     |
| **Cost**            | ₱20 a year for a local fisherfolk permit; ₱150 – ₱250 for boat registration or permit to operate |
| **Processing Time** | 3 days after you submit complete requirements (for each permit)                                  |

CENRO handles three separate permits:

| Permit                | What it is for                         | Fee                                         | Processing time |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| **Fishing permit**    | Your permission to fish                | ₱20 a year for local fisherfolk (see below) | 3 days          |
| **Boat registration** | Registers your pump boat with the city | ₱150 – ₱250, based on your boat's tonnage   | 3 days          |
| **Permit to operate** | Covers your boat's engine              | ₱150 – ₱250, based on your boat's tonnage   | 3 days          |

### Fishing permit fees

| Who is fishing                             | Fee                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Local fisherfolk (marginal or subsistence) | ₱20 a year                   |
| Fisherfolk from other cities or towns      | ₱1,000 a year                |
| Leisure or sports fishing                  | ₱1,000 a year                |
| Research                                   | ₱50 per item                 |
| Commercial fishing                         | Per contract; ask the office |

### Boat registration and permit to operate fees

| Boat size (gross tonnage, GT) | Fee  |
| ----------------------------- | ---- |
| 2 GT up to 3 GT               | ₱250 |
| 1 GT up to 2 GT               | ₱200 |
| 1 GT and below                | ₱150 |

## How to Apply

The steps are the same for all three permits.

### Step 1: Go to CENRO for an interview

- Tell the staff which permit you need. They will interview you briefly.
- Fill out the application form.

### Step 2: Submit your requirements

- Hand in the documents and boat details listed below.
- The staff checks them. If everything is complete, they tell you how much to pay.

### Step 3: Pay at the City Treasurer's Office

- Pay the fee at the City Treasurer's Office.
- Bring the official receipt (OR) back to CENRO.

### Step 4: Wait for approval

- CENRO prepares your permit document.
- The fishing permit and boat registration go to the City Administrator's Office for signature. The permit to operate goes to the City Mayor's Office for approval.
- Approval depends on the signing official's availability.

### Step 5: Claim your permit

- Sign the release document.
- You receive your permit with your official receipt attached.

## Requirements

### Documents Needed

**For a fishing permit:**

- [ ] Filled-out application form - from CENRO
- [ ] Certificate of Residency - from your Barangay Captain
- [ ] Residence Certificate (Cedula)
- [ ] Name of your vessel
- [ ] CFVL number or Certificate of Number
- [ ] Gross tonnage of the vessel
- [ ] Home port
- [ ] Number of crew (assisting vessel)
- [ ] Name of the vessel's owner
- [ ] Photo of the pump boat

**For boat registration:**

- [ ] Filled-out application form - from CENRO
- [ ] Certificate of Ownership - from your Barangay Captain
- [ ] Measurements of the pump boat (length, width, height)
- [ ] Stencil of the engine number (2 pieces)
- [ ] Invoice receipt for the engine (new applications only)
- [ ] Photo of the vessel, 5" x 7" (2 pieces)
- [ ] Tonnage calculation - from the boat inspector
- [ ] Certificate of Residency - from your barangay
- [ ] A garbage scoop (sapyaw)
- [ ] A garbage can or sack

**For a permit to operate:**

- [ ] Filled-out application form - from CENRO
- [ ] Stencil of the engine number (2 copies)
- [ ] Engine trademark or make, horsepower, number of cylinders, and serial number
- [ ] Copy of your boat registration with its certificate number
- [ ] Other relevant information about the vessel and its owner or operator

### Eligibility

- **Fishing permit:** residents, fisherfolk, fishing boat owners, and institutions of Lapu-Lapu City, and people from other cities or towns
- **Boat registration and permit to operate:** residents and fishing boat owners of Lapu-Lapu City

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need both a boat registration and a permit to operate?**
A: They are separate permits. The permit to operate covers your boat's engine, and you need a copy of your boat registration to apply for it.

**Q: I am not from Lapu-Lapu City. Can I get a fishing permit?**
A: Yes. Fisherfolk from other cities or towns can apply. The fee is ₱1,000 a year.

**Q: Where do I pay?**
A: At the City Treasurer's Office. Bring the official receipt back to CENRO.
