# Get Your Dog or Cat Vaccinated Against Rabies — Lapu-Lapu City

Pet owners in Lapu-Lapu City can have their dogs and cats vaccinated against rabies for free at the City Veterinary Office (CVO), or at home through the on-call service. Your pet must be registered and at least 3 months old.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information         | Details                                                                                                         |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**           | City Veterinary Office, Sitio Seabreeze, Brgy. Pusok, Lapu-Lapu City, or at your home (on-call service)         |
| **When**            | Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, except holidays                                                            |
| **Requirements**    | Your pet's Registration Certificate (Green Card); pet must be 3 months or older                                 |
| **Cost**            | Free                                                                                                            |
| **Processing Time** | Up to 1 hour at the office; for home service, the office calls you back within 3 working days to set a schedule |

The City Veterinary Office runs this service under City Ordinance No. 275-2005 as part of the city's campaign against rabies, a fatal disease spread through animal bites.

**Were you bitten by an animal?** This page is about vaccinating pets. If a person was bitten, see [Get rabies vaccination after an animal bite](/services/health-services/get-rabies-vaccination-after-an-animal-bite).

---

## How to Apply

You can walk in to the office, or call to ask for a home visit.

### Option A: Walk in to the City Veterinary Office

#### Step 1: Show your pet's Green Card

- Present your pet's Registration Certificate (Green Card). The staff checks it against the master list.
- If your pet is not yet registered, [register your dog or cat](/services/agriculture-fisheries/register-your-dog-or-cat) first.

#### Step 2: Have your pet vaccinated

- Present your dog or cat. The staff gives the anti-rabies vaccine.

### Option B: Ask for a home visit (on-call)

#### Step 1: Call the office

- The staff logs your request and schedules a home visit.
- The office calls you within 3 working days to tell you the schedule.

#### Step 2: Vaccinate your pet at home

- On the scheduled day, fill out the forms and give them to the field staff.
- The staff checks your entries and vaccinates your pet.

#### Step 3: Get the vaccination certificate

- You receive a Vaccination Certificate with a tag number.
- The staff records the details in the barangay's dog registry book.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the anti-rabies shot for my pet free?**
A: Yes. The charter lists no fee for this service.

**Q: My puppy is 2 months old. Can it be vaccinated?**
A: Not yet. Dogs and cats must be at least 3 months old.

**Q: My pet is not registered. What should I do?**
A: Register your pet first. Registration is free and is done at the same office.
